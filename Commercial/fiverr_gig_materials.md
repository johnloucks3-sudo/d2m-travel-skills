But every itinerary is personally reviewed and refined by me before it reaches you. You get the speed of AI and the judgment of a real advisor.

A: Yes. As a licensed travel advisor, I have direct access to cruise lines, hotel wholesalers, and supplier networks. The Premium tier includes a 15-minute consultation where we can talk through booking. Many Fiverr clients become full advisory clients — it's the most natural next step.

**Q3: What destinations do you specialize in?**
A: Luxury cruises are my core specialty — Mediterranean, Caribbean, Alaska, and Asia-Pacific, with lines including Silversea, Regent Seven Seas, Cunard, Oceania, Seabourn, Viking, AmaWaterways, and Ponant. I also cover European city and resort travel, Japan, and complex multi-destination journeys. If it's luxury, I can plan it.

**Q4: Why does this cost more than other itinerary gigs?**
A: Because it's a different product. Most services hand you a text list. I deliver a professionally designed PDF document — hotel comparisons, destination research, dining guides, real pricing data, branded design. It's the difference between a Post-it note and an architect's blueprint. The right buyer recognizes that immediately.

**Q5: What if I need changes after delivery?**
A: Every tier includes revisions (1 for Basic, 2 for Standard, 3 for Premium). Revisions beyond the included number are available as a gig extra. I want you to love the final document — if something isn't right, we fix it.

## 5. REQUIREMENTS LIST

*This is what Fiverr shows buyers after purchase — tells them exactly what to send you.*

```
To build your itinerary, I'll need the following. The more detail, the better:

1. DESTINATION(S): Where do you want to go? List all cities, regions, or cruise ports.

2. TRAVEL DATES: Exact dates or a general timeframe (month/year). Flexibility is fine — note it.

3. GROUP SIZE & AGES: Number of travelers. Ages matter for recommendations (families vs. adults-only, accessibility needs, etc.).

4. BUDGET RANGE: Approximate total trip budget, or per-person budget. This shapes hotel and experience recommendations. No judgment — just precision.

5. INTERESTS & PRIORITIES: What matters most? (e.g., food and wine, culture and history, outdoor adventure, pure relaxation, luxury spas, specific cuisine, local experiences vs. landmarks)

6. SPECIAL OCCASION: Anniversary, honeymoon, milestone birthday, family reunion? Tell me — it changes the recommendations.

7. EXISTING BOOKINGS: Have you already booked flights, a cruise, or a hotel? Share what's locked in so I build around it.

8. CRUISE PREFERENCE (if applicable): Which cruise line, ship, or sailing are you considering? Or do you want recommendations?
```
