### Pricing Logic
- **Basic at $97:** 5-10x the competition. Justified by the deliverable quality — a designed PDF versus a plain text doc. This is a visual product, not a list. The price filters out bargain hunters and attracts quality-conscious buyers.
- **Standard at $197:** The anchor tier. Most Fiverr buyers pick the middle option. The hotel comparison + dining guide makes this feel like a full service. This is where volume should land.
- **Premium at $297:** The conversion tier. The 15-minute consultation call is the upsell mechanism. If John converts 1 in 3 Premium buyers into a full advisory client booking a $10K+ trip, the commission from one booking ($2,500+) eclipses a month of Fiverr revenue.
- **The real play:** Fiverr is a client acquisition channel, not a primary revenue source. Every Premium order is a warm lead for D2M advisory services.
