## THE PROBLEM WITH TRADITIONAL TRAVEL PLANNING

Most travel agencies operate reactively:

- You call with dates
- Agent checks availability "next Thursday"
- You wait 5 days for a call back
- Agent sends you a generic list of options (often 10+ flights, none truly curated)
- You pick one randomly because there are too many to evaluate
- Agent books it
- You get confirmation 3 days later

By the time you're ready to book hotels, it's 3 months out. By the time you're thinking about dining, it's already full.

## HOW THE TIMELINE PROTECTS YOU

### You Never Miss a Deadline

With traditional planning, **you** have to remember:
- *"When should I book flights?"*
- *"Are hotel rates going up?"*
- *"Is that restaurant I wanted going to fill up?"*

With our system, **I'm watching the calendar.** On June 19, you'll get an email: *"Here are your flight options."* You don't have to chase me. You don't have to remember. It just happens on schedule.

### You Get Curated Options, Not Generic Lists

When I research flights, I don't send you 15 options. I research 50+ routings, eliminate the bad ones, and send you 3 that are actually viable:
- Best price-to-convenience ratio
- Best timing for your embarkation
- Best seat availability for your group

Same with hotels, excursions, dining. You get thoughtfully curated choices, not a firehose of data.

### You Control the Pace

If circumstances change (wedding date, family illness, budget shift, new preferences), you call me and the entire timeline reshapes in real-time. One date change doesn't break the system — it cascades intelligently through dependent decisions.

Example: If you told me today that your daughter's wedding moved to July, I could recalculate your entire timeline and show you how each phase shifts. It takes 30 minutes, not 3 weeks of back-and-forth emails.
