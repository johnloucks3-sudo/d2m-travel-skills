# PROPOSAL — "Lend It Out": Free AI for Public Good
**For Commander · Hale synthesis + staff cell · 2026-06-14**
**Mission anchor:** "For friends I'd serve for free, and clients who deserve better than Pavlus."

## BOTTOM LINE
Give away the capability, never the key. Ship a **one-page web tool** each person opens on their own phone/computer with their **own free Google key** — $0 to D2M, scales infinitely, no ToS risk. Justify it as a **character/brand play** (referral tail is gravy, not the reason). Pilot to **one person first**, "as-is, unsupported," 90-day kill switch.

## STAFF CELL

**Sterling (build):** NOT the gmn.sh CLI — Node install kills non-technical users. Ship `gemini-buddy.html`: single static page, recipient pastes own key (saved in browser), calls Gemini REST directly. Works Mac/Win/Linux/**phone**. Strip all D2M paths/keys; fail loud if no key; no phone-home. Ship gate: 1 green REST test.

**Harlan (cost):** D2M exposure = **$0** confirmed — Google bills the key owner. Hard rule: distributed tool ships with ZERO D2M key and NO fallback to ours (the 1,500/day is D2M's ops quota). If ever a shared-bot-on-our-key for a small group: 50/50 split, per-user 5 Pro/100 Flash cap, ops reserve untouchable. Separate decision.

**Padre (ethics):** Yes, it's right. Best target = **small nonprofits / lone mission-workers** (church secretary, food-pantry coordinator, one-person vet office) — real need, text-shaped work, a motivated person who multiplies it to everyone they serve. Guardrails (3 plain sentences, not a ToS): it's confidently wrong sometimes—verify what matters; don't paste others' private info; the decision stays human. Keep it true generosity: "the gift still works when no one knows who gave it." One quiet "from a friend at D2M" line, no banner. ⚠️ Don't fold fellow travel advisors into a *public*-good program — serve them as friends, separately.

**Castillo (strategy):** Form = giftable page + ruthlessly-tested 3-step guide (reject shared bot / hosted service — they make you an operator). Return: brand ("the advisor who gives back"), network warming, slow 12–18mo referral tail. Don't sell yourself on ROI — sell on who you are. Scope discipline = the survival rule: ship "as-is, unsupported," one async FAQ, no bespoke versions, 90-day pilot, kill if support >30 min/wk. Roll to 1 person, then 3–5, then curated wider.

**Dani (desire/adoption):** Lead with **dignity, not capability** — it's "the patient expert who never makes you feel old, and it's *yours*." Killers: blank "ask me anything" box, setup before the first win, jargon. Magic FIRST (you demo live on your screen), plumbing second. One real starter prompt per audience, not a menu:
- USAFA classmate: *"Explain this Medicare/insurance letter in plain English and tell me what to do next."*
- Nonprofit: *"Turn these rough notes into a donor thank-you letter."*
Referral spark = a story where THEY were the hero ("it wrote the eulogy I couldn't start"), plus a one-line forward so passing it on is effortless.

## CONFLICT RESOLVED
Dani ("magic before plumbing, pre-load a key") vs Harlan ("never ship our key"): **You demo it live on your device first** — they feel it in 60 seconds — *then* help them get their own free key. Production key never leaves.
