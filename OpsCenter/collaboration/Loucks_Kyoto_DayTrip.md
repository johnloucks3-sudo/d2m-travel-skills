## 📍 1. DEPARTURE: Hilton Tokyo Odaiba (06:30 AM)
You need to leave the hotel by **6:30 AM** to comfortably make the Shinkansen connection at Tokyo Station.
*   **Step 1:** Walk from the Hilton to **Daiba Station** (connected to the hotel). Take the Yurikamome Line to **Shimbashi Station** (~15 mins).
*   **Step 2:** At Shimbashi, transfer to the JR Yamanote Line or JR Tokaido Line to **Tokyo Station** (~5 mins).
*   **Morning Coffee/Snack:** Grab a quick coffee and baked goods at a bakery inside Tokyo Station before boarding.

*   **Train to Book:** The **Tokaido Shinkansen (Nozomi train)**. (2 hrs 15 mins).
*   **Time:** Book a train departing around **7:30 AM** (Arrives Kyoto ~9:45 AM).
*   **Pro-Tip (Mt. Fuji View):** When selecting seats in the SmartEX app (`ID: 3740314183`), choose **Seat E** (the window seat on the right side of the train) for the best chance to see Mount Fuji.

## ⛩️ 5. AFTERNOON EXPLORATION & LIGHT SNACKS (1:00 PM - 5:00 PM)
Since you'll be full from the 3-hour food tour, you'll likely just want light snacks while exploring.
*   **Sightseeing:** Take a taxi up to **Kiyomizu-dera Temple** (the massive wooden stage overlooking the city) or head south to the **Fushimi Inari Shrine** (the iconic orange torii gates).
*   **Susie-Approved Snacks:**
    *   *Near Kiyomizu-dera:* Look for **Yatsuhashi** (a famous Kyoto sweet made of cinnamon mochi, often filled with red bean or matcha) and freshly roasted sweet potatoes.
    *   *Matcha Break:* Stop at **Maccha House** or **Gion Tsujiri** (near your tour end point) for world-class matcha lattes and parfaits.
    *   *Savory Bite:* If you walk through Nishiki Market, look for **Tamagoyaki** (sweet rolled omelets) or **Karaage** (Japanese fried chicken)—both excellent, fully cooked options.

## 🚄 6. THE RETURN & DINNER: Kyoto to Tokyo (6:00 PM)
*   **Dinner Strategy:** Grab a famous *Ekiben* (train bento box) at the massive food halls inside Kyoto Station to eat on the ride back.
    *   *For Susie (No Raw Seafood):* Look specifically for a **Wagyu/Sukiyaki Beef Bento**, a **Tonkatsu (Fried Pork) Bento**, or an **Unagi (Grilled Eel) Bento**. The stations have massive sections dedicated purely to premium cooked meats.
*   **Train Home:** Pre-book a **Nozomi Shinkansen** departing around **6:00 PM** or **6:30 PM**.
*   **Arrival:** You will be back at Tokyo Station between 8:15 PM and 8:45 PM. 

## 📍 7. ARRIVAL: Back to Hilton Tokyo Odaiba (9:30 PM)
*   **Transfer:** Take the JR line to Shimbashi, then the Yurikamome Line back to Daiba Station. Walk directly into the Hilton Odaiba and get some rest before your 7:50 AM Mt. Fuji bus tour the next morning!
