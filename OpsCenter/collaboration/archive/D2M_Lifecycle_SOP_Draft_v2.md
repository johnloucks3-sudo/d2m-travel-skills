# Client Lifecycle SOP (15-18 Month Version)

## Introduction

As the Chief Strategy Officer for Dreams2Memories Travel, I have been tasked with revising the Client Lifecycle SOP to address the Commander's feedback. This revised SOP expands the timeline to 15-18 months out, making the phases highly flexible to accommodate the shifting needs and preferences of our elite clientele. Additionally, specific AI Personas have been assigned to key touchpoints throughout the process.

## The Dream/Inception Phase (15-18 Months Out)

**Timeline:** 15-18 Months Out
**Objective:** Establish an initial connection with the client, understand their dreams and aspirations, and begin the process of bringing their vision to life.

**Key Touchpoints:**
- **Initial Client Outreach (A3 Dani):** Reach out to the client, introduce Dreams2Memories Travel, and gauge their interest in our services.
- **Dream Discovery Session (A6 Luna):** Conduct a deep-dive interview with the client to understand their travel preferences, budget, and specific dream experiences they wish to create.
- **Concept Development (A2 Dembe):** Collaborate with the client to develop a preliminary itinerary and experience concepts that align with their vision.

**Flexibility and Adaptability:**
- The timeline for this phase is flexible, as the client's schedules and priorities may shift during this initial stage.
- The specific touchpoints and their timing may be adjusted based on the client's availability and responsiveness.
- Regular check-ins and status updates will be conducted to ensure the client's needs and expectations are being met.

## The Crafting Phase (12-15 Months Out)

**Timeline:** 12-15 Months Out
**Objective:** Refine the itinerary and experience concepts, secure necessary reservations and arrangements, and begin the logistics planning process.

**Key Touchpoints:**
- **Itinerary Refinement (A3 Dani):** Collaborate with the client to finalize the itinerary, accommodations, and activities based on their feedback and any changes in their preferences.
- **Logistics Planning (A6 Luna):** Coordinate with our travel concierge team to secure all necessary reservations, transportation, and other logistical arrangements.
- **Progress Updates (A2 Dembe):** Provide regular updates to the client on the status of the planning process and address any questions or concerns they may have.

**Flexibility and Adaptability:**
- The timeline for this phase may be adjusted based on the client's availability and the complexity of the travel arrangements.
- The specific touchpoints and their timing may be modified to accommodate the client's preferred communication style and responsiveness.
- Regular check-ins with the client will ensure that any changes in their plans or preferences can be seamlessly incorporated into the itinerary.

## The Execution Phase (3-6 Months Out)

**Timeline:** 3-6 Months Out
**Objective:** Finalize all arrangements, provide a comprehensive pre-travel briefing, and ensure a seamless and memorable experience for the client.

**Key Touchpoints:**
- **Pre-Travel Briefing (A3 Dani):** Conduct a detailed briefing with the client, covering the finalized itinerary, travel logistics, and any additional information they may need.
- **Concierge Support (A6 Luna):** Assign a dedicated concierge to the client, who will be available throughout the trip to address any needs or requests that may arise.
- **In-Trip Check-Ins (A2 Dembe):** Maintain regular communication with the client during the trip, ensuring their satisfaction and addressing any issues or requests promptly.

**Flexibility and Adaptability:**
- The timeline for this phase may be adjusted based on the client's travel dates and the complexity of the trip.
- The specific touchpoints and their frequency may be tailored to the client's preferences and communication style.
- The concierge support and in-trip check-ins will be highly responsive to the client's needs, allowing for a seamless and adaptable execution.

## Conclusion

This revised Client Lifecycle SOP reflects the Commander's feedback by expanding the timeline to 15-18 months out and incorporating a high degree of flexibility to accommodate the shifting needs and preferences of our elite clientele. Additionally, specific AI Personas have been assigned to key touchpoints throughout the process, ensuring a personalized and responsive approach to client engagement and support.

This SOP is ready for coordination with Claude and implementation by the Dreams2Memories Travel team.
