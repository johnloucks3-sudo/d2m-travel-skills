# YSB — Yoda's Summary Brief
**Civilian Decision & Tactical Intelligence Format**
*Version 1.0 | Effective 2026-05-01 | Replaces Military TSB*

---

## OVERVIEW

YSB is the operational briefing standard for all executive and tactical decisions at Dreams2Memories Travel, LLC. It removes military terminology while preserving decision rigor and speed.

**Two paths:**
- **PATH A (Executive):** High-stakes binding decisions. Requires Owner approval. 4-hour SLA.
- **PATH B (Tactical):** Low-stakes tactical choices. Real-time polling. No approval gate.

---

## PATH A — EXECUTIVE (High-Stakes, Binding)

Used for: Contracts, insurance, total budgets, policy decisions, staff changes, financial commitments.

### Format

```
DECISION REQUIRED: [What needs approval]

BACKGROUND: [Why this matters, context, constraint]

FINANCIAL IMPACT: [Cost/revenue delta, budget available, timeline pressure]

RECOMMENDATION: [What I propose, why, what it achieves]

APPROVAL REQUIRED FROM: [Owner]
[Yes] [No] [Discuss]

Reviewed by: [Name/Role, Name/Role]
```

### Example

```
DECISION REQUIRED: Allocate $15K contingency for Loucks Japan trip

BACKGROUND: Obon booking window closes Aug 16. Daughters' flights add $8K beyond original estimate. Without flex budget now, rebooking after window is 40% more expensive.

FINANCIAL IMPACT: Current available balance: $45K | Request: $15K | Remaining after approval: $30K | Decision deadline: 48 hours

RECOMMENDATION: Approve contingency. Obon (Festival of Souls) is peak season Aug 13–16. Rebooking in September costs $12K+ more. Approving now preserves family flexibility and locks optimal pricing.

APPROVAL REQUIRED FROM: Owner
[Yes] [No] [Discuss]

Reviewed by: Finance (Victor Harlan), Concierge (Danielle Moreau)
```

### SLA & Escalation
- **Standard SLA:** 4 hours (same-day or next-day decision)
- **Escalation:** If Owner unavailable, notify COS (Chief of Staff)
- **Deadline-driven:** Decisions with hard deadlines (booking windows, payment due dates) get priority routing

## PATH B — TACTICAL (Low-Stakes, Real-Time)

Used for: Flight/hotel/excursion/dining searches, itinerary refinements, preference polling, operational choices with low financial or strategic impact.

### Format

```
DECISION POINT: [What we're choosing]

OPTIONS:
  A) [Option] | [Price or key detail] | [One-line benefit/trade-off]
  B) [Option] | [Price or key detail] | [One-line benefit/trade-off]
  C) [Option] | [Price or key detail] | [One-line benefit/trade-off]

SUGGESTED CHOICE: [Which option, why in one sentence]

[Select This] [Change Dates] [Change Budget] [See More] [Discuss]

Response SLA: 4 minutes (real-time feedback loop)
```

### Example

```
DECISION POINT: McLeod flights — Lesser Antilles Dec 19 departure

OPTIONS:
  A) Regent direct DTW→FLL Dec 14 | $2,400/pp | Departs 11:30am, zero stress, early Miami arrival
  B) United+Regent connection DTW→ATL→FLL | $1,800/pp | Departs 6:20am, 2.5-hour layover, tight timing
  C) Delta direct DTW→FLL Dec 13 | $2,100/pp | Arrives 7:30pm, adds pre-trip night hotel cost

SUGGESTED CHOICE: Option A. Direct flight, board by noon, minimize travel stress for 60+ travelers, optimal pricing.

[Select This] [Change Dates] [Change Budget] [See More] [Discuss]

Response SLA: You respond in 4 minutes. No approval bottleneck. Real-time iteration.
```

### Characteristics
- **No approval gate.** Tactical decisions don't require Owner sign-off; they're collaborative refinement.
- **Interactive.** Owner can poll options, refine parameters, see alternatives — all in real-time (4-minute feedback loop vs. 4-hour wait).
- **Ownership loop.** Owner's preference drives next iteration: "Change Dates" → new options with updated dates. "Change Budget" → budget-tier alternatives. "See More" → expanded option set.
- **Escalation:** If Owner requests it, escalate to PATH A (executive gate).

## DECISION CLASSIFICATION — Which Path?

| Decision Type | Path | Owner Involvement | Approval Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contracts, insurance, budget | A | Executive | Yes |
| Policy, staff, financial commitment | A | Executive | Yes |
| New client first contact | A | Executive | Yes |
| Flight/hotel search | B | Tactical polling | No |
| Excursion recommendation | B | Tactical polling | No |
| Dining reservation strategy | B | Tactical polling | No |
| Itinerary refinement | B | Tactical polling | No |

## TERMINOLOGY REPLACEMENT (Military → Civilian)

| Old (Military TSB) | New (YSB) |
|---|---|
| COS (Chief of Staff) | COS (same role, no military connotation in civilian context) |
| A-Staff (A1, A3, A9) | Named roles (Finance, Concierge, Strategy) |
| Commander | Owner |
| Disposition | Recommendation |
| Validated by | Reviewed by |
| Approve/Disapprove/Discuss | Yes/No/Discuss (PATH A) or Select/Change/See More/Discuss (PATH B) |
| PATH A / PATH B | Executive (High-Stakes) / Tactical (Real-Time) |
| Escalation | Escalation (neutral, kept — common in civilian ops) |
