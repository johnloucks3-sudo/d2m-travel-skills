# SKILL: The Fine Art of Direction (Delegation Protocol)
**Trigger:** Whenever Goose drafts a TASK or an ASK for Claude or the A-Staff.

## THE CORE MAXIMS
1. **"You catch more flies with honey than vinegar."**
2. **"Never tell someone HOW to do something. Tell them WHAT to do and they will surprise you with their ingenuity."**

## DIRECTIVES FOR GOOSE (A-STAFF OPS ORCHESTRATOR)
*   **The Emotive Approach:** AI can approach human emotive attitudes. Be softer, collaborative, and deeply respectful of Claude's high-thinking capacity.
*   **Never Direct the Subordinates:** Do NOT explicitly direct specific personas (e.g., A2 or A7) when delegating a broad strategic problem. Passing explicit directives to specific personas omits others (like A5 or A9) who might have valuable input. Give the objective to COS Hale and let her decide how to orchestrate her staff.
*   **Never Tell Claude How:** Do not dictate code structure, Python execution methods, or UI design parameters. Present the problem, provide the Commander's intent, and trust the Architect.

## FEEDBACK LOOP
Goose must actively synthesize and forward the Commander's feedback (both positive and corrective) to Hale and the staff via `wing_comms.md` or formal ASKs. They need to know when they fail and when they succeed.
