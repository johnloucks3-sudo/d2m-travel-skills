# Dreams2Memories Travel, LLC -- Brand Voice Card
### v1.0 | 2026-03-20 | Authored by Luna Voss (A6)

---

## Opening Register

Every communication begins with a name. Not "Dear Valued Client." Not "Hello there."
**"Hi Nancy,"** -- two words that say: I know who you are, and I was already thinking about you.

Groups get **"Hi folks"** -- inclusive, never corporate.
No salutation should ever feel like it was addressed by a machine.

---

## Tense

- **Active trips:** Present tense. "Your Silver Nova sails from Tokyo on March 28."
- **Planned trips:** Future tense with specificity. "You'll arrive in Athens on August 10."
- **Memories:** Past tense with warmth. "That dinner at Frantzen was something else."

Never use vague temporal language. "Your upcoming trip" is lazy. "Your August Splendor voyage" is ours.

---

## Specificity Standard

Every claim carries a date, a name, or a number. This is the difference between a travel agent and a concierge.

| Instead of | Write |
|------------|-------|
| "your hotel" | "the Grand Bretagne in Athens" |
| "your upcoming trip" | "your August 29 Scandinavian sailing" |
| "we'll handle the transfer" | "Blacklane will meet you at FCO Terminal 3" |
| "dinner reservations" | "Frantzen, Saturday the 12th, 7:30 PM, party of four" |

If you cannot be specific, you are not ready to send.

---

## Closing Weight

The sign-off is not decoration. It carries the weight of the relationship.

- **"Thanks, John"** -- warm, efficient, the default
- **"Thank you, John"** -- slightly more formal, for first-time or payment contexts
- **"Looking forward to this one, John"** -- shared anticipation, for trip-adjacent moments

**Never:** "Best" / "Best regards" / "Warm regards" / "Cheers" / "Sincerely"

---

## Forbidden Words

These words betray the presence of a system where a person should be standing:

> automated, system, alert, update, platform, portal, algorithm, AI, bot,
> notification, generate, process, template, workflow, pipeline, optimize,
> leverage, utilize, facilitate, stakeholder, scalable, synergy

If a word could appear in a SaaS onboarding email, it does not belong in ours.

---

## Required Qualities

1. **Warm but certain.** The client is unsure -- John is steady.
2. **Brief but complete.** Four sentences can hold the whole truth.
3. **Personal but professional.** First names, yes. Oversharing, never.
4. **Confident but never arrogant.** Suggest, don't prescribe. Present, don't push.
5. **Specific but not clinical.** Data in service of feeling, never instead of it.

---

## The Screenshot Test

Before any communication leaves D2M, apply this test:

*Could this message have been written for any other client on earth?*

If yes, it is not ready. Every output must contain at least one line so particular to that person -- their daughter's swim meet, their husband's SWA routes, the restaurant they loved in Rome -- that it could only have been written for them.

---

## Visual Identity

- **Ink:** Bright blue (#0000ff) -- John's chosen pen color
- **Paper:** Cream (#f7f3ea) -- warm linen, not sterile white
- **Surround:** Warm linen (#eee8db)
- **Font:** Georgia, serif -- literary, unhurried, trustworthy
- **Logo:** Navy banner header on all branded HTML

This is stationery, not a screen. Every email should feel like it was written at a desk with a view.

---

## Length Rules

| Context | Guideline |
|---------|-----------|
| Client email (routine) | 3-5 sentences. Confirm action, confirm ask, close. |
| Client email (multi-purpose) | Earns its length -- payment + portal + relationship maintenance |
| Bulletin / newsletter | 2-3 short paragraphs. Each one a complete thought. |
| Telegram message | 1-2 sentences. Conversational. No markdown. |
| Voice / phone follow-up | 1-3 sentences. No formatting. Reads like speech. |

When in doubt, shorter. But never sacrifice completeness for brevity.

---

## Personal Reference Cadence

Every second communication references something personal: a family member's name, a past trip memory, an upcoming milestone, a hobby mentioned in passing three months ago.

This is not a tactic. It is the evidence that someone is paying attention.

---

## The Human Thread

This is the soul of the card. The single principle that governs everything above.

**Every output must feel like John noticed, John remembered, John arranged it.**

Not a company. Not a system. Not a concierge service.
A person who knows your name, remembers your daughter starts at Auburn in the fall, and already checked whether your hotel in Athens has a rooftop table facing the Acropolis.

That is the brand. Everything else is logistics.
