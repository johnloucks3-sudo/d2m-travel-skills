Ideas Worth Looking At

1. Silver Key Tri-Lakes Senior Center — Monument
This is the one I’d start with. Five minutes from your house, walk in anytime, and you’ll be sitting around with people our age before you know it. Full details below.

2. Grief Cruise — Journeys of Hope
A 5-day Caribbean cruise with group support for people who lost a spouse. Not a singles thing — but the connections people make there are real. Something to think about 6–12 months down the road.

3. Silversea Solo Program
You already know the product. They have hosted solo get-togethers and gentleman hosts on some sailings. One phone call and we can get you booked — no computer needed.

4. Cunard — Queen Mary 2 Transatlantic
If you want to meet women, this is the best option out there. Dance hosts, solo coffee groups, ballroom dancing every night. The crowd is our age.

5. Overseas Adventure Travel (OAT)
Small groups of 12–16 people, mostly solo travelers, mostly women. Phone booking, print catalogs. No tech headaches.

6. Grand Circle Travel
River cruises and tours for our age group. Built as a phone-and-catalog company. Half off some trips right now. Good community that travels together again and again.

7. Crystal Cruises
Ambassador hosts, solo cabins at no extra charge, and they won best solo program in 2026. Intimate ships, high quality.

8. Senior Travel Buddies
A $20/year service that matches solo travelers with companions. My team can set it up for you — you just get a call when there’s a good match.
