THE VOYAGE — LESSER ANTILLES JOURNEY

You're heading into the southern Caribbean at its finest — 10 nights roundtrip from Miami, five distinct island personalities, and two pairs of sea days that give you exactly what a ship like Grandeur is built for. The timing is deliberately special: Christmas Eve in Antigua, Christmas Day in St. Kitts. You'll spend the holiday in the Caribbean sun rather than a living room, with no obligations and nowhere to be except where you already are. YOUR PORTS OF CALL

Sat Dec 19 — Miami, Florida (Depart 5:00 PM)
Your voyage begins at the Port of Miami. Board early, find Suite 863, and watch the city fall away as you head south. Two sea days follow — use them.

Dec 20–21 — At Sea
Back-to-back sea days are a gift. The spa, the pool deck, Compass Rose at midday — the ship is yours. No alarms, no excursion briefings, just open Caribbean water ahead.

Tue Dec 22 — St. Thomas, U.S. Virgin Islands (8:00 AM – 5:00 PM)
Charlotte Amalie wraps a protected harbor in Danish colonial architecture, duty-free streets, and deep Caribbean blue pressing in from every angle. Magens Bay is one of the world's finest beaches: a mile-wide arc of calm, sheltered water reachable by a short taxi ride over the ridge. For history, the 99 Steps neighborhood delivers centuries of the Caribbean's layered past. Coral World offers snorkeling with sea turtles in a protected marine setting. A well-oiled port — good day to set your own pace.

Wed Dec 23 — Roseau, Dominica (10:00 AM – 7:00 PM)
Dominica is the nature island — undeveloped, volcanic, and wild in a way the rest of the Caribbean traded away long ago. Trafalgar Falls spills twin plumes through rainforest into mountain pools below. Boiling Lake sits in a UNESCO-protected volcanic valley after a two-hour jungle hike that earns every step. Nearly 365 rivers drain the interior; the air smells of sulfur and green. The ship stays late, giving you a real day. This is the port that rewards those who go inland.

Thu Dec 24 — St. John's, Antigua (10:00 AM – 8:00 PM) — Christmas Eve
Antigua famously claims 365 beaches. Nelson's Dockyard — a UNESCO 18th-century British naval base still in active use as a marina — is the architectural centerpiece. Dickenson Bay is the island's most polished stretch of sand. Shirley Heights offers a dramatic clifftop fort with sweeping views across English Harbour. On Christmas Eve, expect warmth, music, and the festive mood the Caribbean carries particularly well. The ship departs at 8:00 PM — a long, full day ashore.

Fri Dec 25 — Basseterre, St. Kitts (8:00 AM – 5:00 PM) — Christmas Day
Christmas morning in the Caribbean, on one of its most authentically unspoiled islands. Brimstone Hill Fortress — the “Gibraltar of the West Indies” — rises 800 feet above the coast and carries UNESCO World Heritage status. The St. Kitts Scenic Railway loops 30 miles through sugarcane fields and mountain villages. Basseterre itself is one of the most genuinely local capitals in the region: Creole architecture, the Victorian Circus, a quiet dignity that hasn't been overrun. A morning worth wandering at your own pace.

Sat Dec 26 — Tortola, British Virgin Islands (8:00 AM – 5:00 PM)
The BVI's largest island sits at the center of some of the Caribbean's finest sailing waters. Cane Garden Bay is postcard-perfect. The Baths at Virgin Gorda — massive granite boulders forming sea caves and tidal pools — are accessible by short boat trip and rank among the region's genuinely unmissable natural features. Sage Mountain National Park offers shaded hiking through the highest point in the Virgin Islands. Tortola is a day to breathe, wander, and let the BVI work its quiet magic.

Dec 27–28 — At Sea
The mirror image of the outbound passage — two sea days returning north. By now you've found your favorite table at Compass Rose, your preferred deck chair, and the exact hour the pool goes quiet. Use these days for that.

Tue Dec 29 — Miami, Florida (Arrive 7:00 AM)
Ten nights, five islands, a very good run. Home in time for New Year's.

DINING ON BOARD

Compass Rose — The main dining room for breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Continental and regional cuisine, rotating menu, no reservations required. The social center of the ship and consistently strong.

Chartreuse — Grandeur's exclusive French fine-dining specialty restaurant. Classic technique, elegant presentation, limited seating. The hardest reservation on the ship — request it on Day 1. A Christmas holiday sailing makes this one worth the effort.

Prime 7 — The signature steakhouse. USDA prime cuts, Maine lobster, and a wine list built for the setting. Requires a reservation; books quickly on 10-night sailings. Get this one early.

Sette Mari at La Veranda — Italian dinner service each evening, transforming the La Veranda space from a casual lunch venue. Fresh pasta, regional specialties, a less formal atmosphere than the other specialty rooms.

La Veranda — Casual indoor/outdoor breakfast and lunch buffet with made-to-order stations. Best choice for a relaxed morning before a port day.

Pool Grill — Casual outdoor midday dining: fresh salads, grilled fish, burgers. The right call on sea days when you don't want to change out of your swimsuit.

All venues are included in your Regent fare. No cover charges anywhere on board.
