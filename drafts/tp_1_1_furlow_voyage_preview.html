Your Voyage Route

You'll depart Stockholm on August 29 and return September 8, visiting five distinct Scandinavian ports, each with its own character and history.

Stockholm, Sweden (Embarkation – 2 Days)

The "Venice of the North" spans 14 islands where medieval Old Town architecture meets modern Scandinavian design. You'll board on August 29. We recommend the Swedish Nature Experience excursion to explore Stockholm's archipelago, or simply wander Gamla Stan's cobbled streets and waterfront promenades.

Berlin/Warnemünde, Germany (September 1–2)

The port of Warnemünde on Germany's Baltic coast provides access to Berlin (2.5 hours by coach) and the historic medieval city of Rostock. Your reserved excursions include tours of Berlin's iconic sites and culinary experiences. Alternatively, enjoy the sandy beaches and charming Baltic seaside atmosphere of Warnemünde itself.

Copenhagen, Denmark (September 3–4)

Denmark's capital is a designer's dream: colorful Nyhavn waterfront, world-class restaurants, and bicycle-friendly streets. You have two days to explore Tivoli Gardens, Christiansborg Palace, or simply enjoy Copenhagen's café culture and hygge (cozy warmth).

Kristiansand, Norway (September 5)

A charming coastal town in southern Norway, perfect for a leisurely afternoon exploring local shops, harbors, and the scenic Kristiansand promenade—or relaxing onboard.

Oslo, Norway (September 7)

Norway's capital seamlessly blends Viking heritage with contemporary culture. The included excursion "Oslo During World War II" offers historical insight, or explore the Opera House, Munch Museum, and harbor district on your own.

What to Expect & How to Prepare

Weather: Late August/early September in Scandinavia runs 55–70°F (12–21°C). Layer with sweaters, a light jacket, comfortable walking shoes, and rain gear.

Packing: Casual elegance for dinner (no formal dress required), comfortable shoes for exploring, and layers for changing temperatures.

Currency & Payments: Sweden and Norway use the krona/krone (not euros). Regent accepts credit cards onboard; ATMs are widely available in ports.

Documents: Passports valid for 6+ months beyond return (yours are confirmed ✓). No visas required for US citizens visiting Schendinavia.
