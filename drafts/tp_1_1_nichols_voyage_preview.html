The "Venice of the North" welcomes you aboard on August 29. This city of water, islands, and Nordic charm offers countless experiences: cobblestone Old Town streets, modern design districts, island-hopping by boat. Your reserved Highlights of Stockholm & Vasa Museum excursion showcases Sweden's maritime heritage and historic capital. Berlin/Warnemünde, Germany (September 1–2)

Warnemünde provides access to both the medieval city of Rostock and Berlin (2.5 hours away). Your multi-day itinerary includes The Berlin Experience (September 1), Amazing Rostock, and Medieval Flavors of Rostock (September 2)—perfect for history buffs and those seeking authentic local experiences.

Copenhagen, Denmark (September 3–4)

Two full days in Denmark's elegant capital—famous for Tivoli Gardens, colorful Nyhavn harbor, innovative cuisine, and bicycle culture. Your excursions include A Tour of Two Kingdoms (Denmark to Sweden) and Tivoli Gardens & Canal Cruise, but the city also rewards independent exploration. Cafés, museums, and waterfront strolls are all waiting.

Kristiansand, Norway (September 5)

A picturesque Norwegian harbor town—ideal for a relaxed afternoon exploring the waterfront, local markets, and Scandinavian charm. Or stay aboard and enjoy the ship's amenities with a view of the coastline.

Oslo, Norway (September 7)

Norway's vibrant capital where Viking history meets modern innovation. Your included excursion, Panoramic Oslo, covers the city's highlights, or explore independently—the Opera House, Munch Museum, and harborfront district are all walkable and rewarding.

What to Expect & How to Prepare

Weather: Late August/early September in Scandinavia averages 55–70°F (12–21°C). Layer with sweaters and light jackets, pack comfortable walking shoes, and bring rain gear. The weather can change quickly, but it's manageable with proper preparation.

Packing: Business casual for dinner (no formal dress required on Regent), comfortable walking shoes, and a small day pack for excursions. Pack items for relaxing onboard as well—the spa, pool, and lounges are wonderful places to unwind.

Currency & Payments: Sweden and Norway use krona/krone. Regent accepts major credit cards onboard; ATMs are readily available in all ports.

Documents: Your passports are valid through February 2030 ✓. No visas required for US citizens visiting Scandinavia.
