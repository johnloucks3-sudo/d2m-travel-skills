Celebrity Ascent Exclusive Bonus
Florence & the Frecciarossa

The Celebrity Ascent's Rome homeport unlocks one of Europe's finest day trips: Florence via Italy's world-class high-speed rail. Board at Roma Termini, arrive at Firenze Santa Maria Novella in 90 minutes. The Uffizi, Michelangelo's David, the Duomo, Ponte Vecchio — all in one unforgettable day. This option does not exist with the Explora II Venice–Athens routing.

🚄 The Train — Frecciarossa / Italo

🇮🇹 Italy's High-Speed Rail

Route: Roma Termini → Firenze Santa Maria Novella

Journey Time: ~90 minutes each way

Frequency: Every 30–60 min, 05:00–21:00

Price: ~€25–55/pp each way (book in advance)

Book: trenitalia.com · italotreno.it

Tip: Frecciarossa 1A (first class) includes meal service, wider seats, quiet carriage. Worth the upgrade for the family.

"Explora II is the dream trip. Celebrity Ascent is the perfect trip."

Explora II delivers the most rarefied Mediterranean experience afloat. But at twice the price, without teen programming, and without the energy that makes a family voyage truly electric for 16 and 18-year-olds, the calculus shifts. Celebrity Ascent gives the girls a world they'll talk about at college — Sicily, Olympia, Florence, the Magic Carpet, new friends from around the world. The Grande Bretagne post-stay is the one area where Explora clearly pulls ahead. Both are extraordinary. The question is which version of this trip the Westbrook family most deserves to take.
