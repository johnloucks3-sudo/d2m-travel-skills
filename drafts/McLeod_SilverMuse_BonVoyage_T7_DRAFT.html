Erik, Melissa — one week out.

I hope the two of you are in that good kind of busy right now — the lists, the packing, the last few things before you hand the next three weeks over to Italy and the Adriatic. We have been working toward this date for a long time, and it is finally here. Next Thursday you fly to Rome.

I wanted to send a note before you go — not with anything for you to do, but just to say everything is set, and to let you feel a little of what is coming. From the moment your car pulls away from Armstrong Drive, the chain is built end to end. You don’t have to carry any of it. That is the whole point.

Your Voyage — Confirmed

Ship: Silver Muse · Silversea

Voyage: Mediterranean & Adriatic — Rome to Venice

Depart Denver: Thursday, June 18 · business class to Rome

Rome: Baglioni Hotel Regina · 4 nights before the ship

Embark: Civitavecchia · June 23

Disembark: Venice (Fusina) · July 3

Venice: Hilton Molino Stucky · 3 nights on Giudecca

Home: Denver · July 6

Booking: 298475-25 · Paid in full ✓

A few days in Rome first — the Baglioni sits right on the Veneto, and your transfers in from Fiumicino and out to the port are already booked and in your file, so Rome is simply yours to wander. Then the Silver Muse, and ten nights that read like a love letter to this part of the world.

What I keep picturing for you:

  • Taormina, the morning after Naples — the Greek theatre with Etna smoking in the distance and the sea below. Few views on earth like it.
  • Noto from Siracusa — honey-colored baroque stone, the quietest of your ports, and all the better for it.
  • Valletta in Malta — a walled city you walk straight into history.
  • A full day at sea crossing the Adriatic — the one day with nowhere to be. Use it well.
  • The Blue Cave at Kotor, then Dubrovnik, Split, and Zadar — the Dalmatian coast, one luminous town after another, before Venice rises out of the lagoon to bring you home.

Your shore excursions are confirmed across every port, and your specialty dinners are reserved — La Dame, Kaiseki, and the rest — all set and waiting. The water taxi that carries you from the ship across to the Molino Stucky on July 3, and the one to the airport on the 6th, are both booked and paid. Everything on our side is arranged. All that’s left is to go.

One small thing, only if the spirit moves you: if either of you has a quiet hope for a particular evening — a table somewhere, a sunset you want to be sure you catch, a corner of one of these towns you’ve always wanted to see — tell me before you fly. I love adding the personal touches, and there is still time to fold one in.

Otherwise, my only assignment for you is to enjoy every minute of it. Send a photo from the theatre at Taormina if it moves you to — I’d love to see it through your eyes.

Safe travels, both of you. Buon viaggio.
