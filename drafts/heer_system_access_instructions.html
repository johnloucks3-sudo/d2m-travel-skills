Ann — here's how to get into the system.

Follow these steps in order. It takes about two minutes the first time.

Step 1 — Open the Terminal

Go to https://code.d2mluxury.quest/ttyd in your browser. You'll see a black screen with a prompt — that's the command line.

Step 2 — Log In

Enter the username and password I sent you separately. Once you're in, you'll see a prompt ending with $ — that means you're connected.

Step 3 — Launch opencode

Type this exactly (one command, all lowercase):

opencode --model anthropic --provider anthropic

Press Enter. It'll take a few seconds to load. You'll see a startup message when it's ready.

Step 4 — Pick Your Model

Once opencode starts, you'll see a model selection screen. Choose between:

ModelBest For
HaikuFast queries, research lookups, quick answers
SonnetWriting, analysis, deeper reasoning

Start with Haiku to get comfortable. You can switch models anytime.

That's it. Once you're in, the interface works like a conversation — type what you need and it responds. If you get stuck or something doesn't look right, call me and I'll walk you through it.
