Viking Mars has two specialty restaurants that go beyond the included dining — and for a group of six over 10 nights, they're worth planning deliberately. Both require reservations, both fill up on embarkation day, and group tables for six take a bit more coordination than a table for two.

🍝 Manfredi's Italian Kitchen

Viking's signature Italian restaurant. Classic antipasti, handmade pasta, branzino, osso buco. Relaxed but elevated — smart casual dress. Reservations open on embarkation day at the restaurant podium.

Manfredi's on Viking

🍽️ The Chef's Table

Multi-course tasting menu paired with wines. More formal, slower pace, typically 2.5–3 hours. Limited seating; one of the first things to fill on any Viking sailing.

Chef's Table on Viking

What I Need to Know

  1. Dietary restrictions or allergies for any of the six travelers? (Chef's Table in particular needs advance notice for modifications.)
  2. Any celebration nights? Anniversary, birthday, or other occasion during the voyage I should flag for a special table or amenity request?
  3. Dining pace preference: Does your group prefer a leisurely multi-course evening (Chef's Table pace) or a more relaxed dinner that wraps in 90 minutes (Manfredi's pace)?
  4. Both restaurants, or focus on one? With 10 nights, you could do both — or do one well. What sounds right?
  5. Any by-name requests? Specific dishes, wine preferences, or "I've heard the [DISH] is excellent" moments I should note?
