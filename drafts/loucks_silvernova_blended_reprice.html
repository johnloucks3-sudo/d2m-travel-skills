Why private Ephesus is the smart upgrade: the direct Ephesus specialists run a private car + licensed guide for the two of you at roughly the same price as the ship’s 40-person bus — your own pace through the ruins, no crowd, for about $11 pp more. Best value upgrade on the whole trip.

Two honest caveats before you book independent: (1) the 5 independent prices are current live listing floors pulled from the operators’ own pages — Viator and GetYourGuide block automated cart pulls, so treat these as “from” figures and confirm the exact cart when you book (I’d expect them to hold within a few dollars). (2) An independent tour carries no “ship-waits-for-you” guarantee — that’s why I kept the ship on the far-flung days (Gythion, Crete) and only went independent on the close-in ports where you can walk back to the pier. For Ephesus, the specialist operators issue a back-to-ship guarantee in writing — ask for it.
