Excursion Price Check — Silversea vs. Independent
Silver Nova · Venice → Athens & Greek Isles · May 2027 · For John & Susie

Yoda — you asked whether booking excursions independently beats Silversea’s prices. The honest answer is “it depends which of three channels you use” — and the surprising part is that Silversea is not the overpriced option people assume.

Channel vs. Silversea
1. Budget online (Viator, GetYourGuide)
group bus, book-it-yourself		Often CHEAPER — sometimes a lot (Olympia from $30, Mykonos ~$52, Rhodes from $22). Trade-off: bigger bus, and no “ship-waits-for-you” guarantee.
2. Premium reseller (ShoreExcursionsGroup)
cruise-focused, back-to-ship guarantee		Usually MORE than Silversea (+30–75% on Santorini, Mykonos, Olympia). Dubrovnik is the lone exception ($57 vs $69).
3. Private guide (just the two of you)
own car + guide		Most per person — you split one car two ways ($150–300+ pp). Worth it only where pace/comfort justify it.
My recommendation for you & Susie
•  Don’t default to independent to “save money” — on a cruise-focused reseller you’ll usually pay more, and Silversea’s tours already include the one thing that matters most: the ship waits for its own excursions.
•  Where to book independent & save: the easy, close-in ports with big tour markets — Dubrovnik (walk off, ~$30–57 vs $69), Kotor (bay boat from ~$14), Mykonos, Olympia, Rhodes, Athens. Budget group tours run 30–60% under the ship.
•  Where the ship tour earns its price: ports where the sites are a long drive or a tender away — Ephesus (Kusadasi), Olympia, the Mani (Gythion) — the back-to-ship guarantee is real insurance at our age.
•  Where private is worth the splurge: 2–3 marquee days — Ephesus and Santorini — for your own pace, no 40-person bus, and a reputable operator’s guarantee.
•  Thin-market ports (Koper, Zadar, Gythion, Paros, Milos, Patmos): few independents — take the ship tour or walk off on your own.

The one thing the price tag doesn’t show: Silversea and reputable resellers carry a “ship waits for you” guarantee — if their tour runs late, the ship holds. A random Viator/GetYourGuide booking does not; miss the all-aboard and you’re chasing the ship to the next port on your own dime. That guarantee is exactly what the cruise-tour premium buys — cheap insurance on the far-flung ports (Ephesus, Olympia, the Mani), less critical where you can walk back to the pier (Dubrovnik, Kotor, Mykonos).

Fine print: figures are 2026 market prices used as a proxy — operators publish 2027 pricing roughly a year out, so we’d re-pull closer to sailing. The budget-online numbers are entry-level “from” floors pulled from listings; a few ports (Viator/GetYourGuide) block automated price scraping, so where I couldn’t confirm a single source I marked it rather than guess.
