Your Voyage Route

Departing Stockholm on August 29, returning September 8, you'll visit five distinct Scandinavian ports. Each offers its own cultural richness and unforgettable experiences.

Stockholm, Sweden (Embarkation – 2 Days)

Welcome to the "Venice of the North"—a city of islands, historic architecture, and modern Scandinavian charm. You'll board on August 29. Your reserved Swedish Nature Experience excursion takes you through Stockholm's stunning archipelago, or explore Gamla Stan's medieval Old Town and waterfront on your own. August 28–29 you'll enjoy At Six Stockholm, a beautiful pre-cruise hotel in the heart of the city.

Berlin/Warnemünde, Germany (September 1–2)

Warnemünde's port opens access to Berlin (2.5 hours away) and the medieval city of Rostock. Your excursions include Amazing Rostock and Medieval Flavors of Rostock—perfect for experiencing local history and Baltic cuisine. Berlin lovers can also explore independently.

Copenhagen, Denmark (September 3–4)

Two full days in Denmark's capital—a city of color, design, and culinary excellence. Your reserved excursion, Christiansborg Palace & Tivoli Gardens, plus A Tour of Two Kingdoms (Denmark to Sweden), showcase the best of this region. Or simply enjoy Nyhavn's waterfront cafés and Copenhagen's relaxed elegance.

Kristiansand, Norway (September 5)

A peaceful Norwegian coastal town perfect for a leisurely afternoon—explore local markets, enjoy the harbor, or simply relax onboard with a view of Scandinavia's rugged coastline.

Oslo, Norway (September 7)

Norway's capital blends ancient Viking heritage with contemporary innovation. Your included excursion, Oslo During World War II, provides historical depth, or explore the Opera House, modern art museums, and vibrant harbor district independently.

What to Expect & How to Prepare

Weather: Late August/early September in Scandinavia is cool and changeable—55–70°F (12–21°C). Pack layers, comfortable walking shoes, a light jacket, and rain gear. Scandinavians are dressed for the weather, and so should you.

Accessibility: Regent ships are modern with elevators, accessible cabins, and onboard assistance available. Please let us know if you need any special arrangements.

Packing: Business casual for dinner (no formal dress), comfortable waterproof walking shoes for shore time, and items for onboard relaxation. A small day pack is helpful for excursions.

Currency & Payments: Sweden and Norway use krona/krone. Regent accepts major credit cards onboard; ATMs are widely available in all ports.

Documents: Your passports are valid through 2030 ✓. No visas required for US citizens visiting Sweden, Norway, or Denmark.
