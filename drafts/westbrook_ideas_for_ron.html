Ideas We Found for Ron

1. Silver Key Tri-Lakes Senior Center — Monument, CO
This is the one I’d start with. It’s five minutes from his house on 3rd Street, free to walk into, and full of people his age. More on this below.

2. Grief Cruise — Journeys of Hope, Healing & Health
A 5-day Caribbean cruise with structured grief support circles, including groups specifically for spouse loss. Not a dating thing — but the connections people make in shared grief are genuine and lasting. Something to look at 6–12 months out.

3. Silversea Solo Program
He already knows the product from the Silver Nova booking. Select sailings have gentleman hosts and solo meetups. Familiar brand, 25% solo supplement, and we can handle the whole booking by phone — he doesn’t touch a computer.

4. Cunard — Queen Mary 2 Transatlantic
This is the best option if he wants to meet women his age in a natural setting. QM2 has gentleman dance hosts, solo coffee groups every morning, and ballroom dancing every night. The demographic skews older, refined, and heavily female on the solo side.

5. Overseas Adventure Travel (OAT)
Small group tours (max 16 people) for the 50+ crowd. Over half their travelers are solo, and 80% of solos are women. Free single rooms on select departures. Phone-booking only, print catalogs — no tech required.

6. Grand Circle Travel
River cruises and land tours for seniors, built as a direct-mail and phone business. Up to 50% off 2026 trips right now. Excellent repeat-traveler community that builds ongoing friendships.

7. Crystal Cruises
Ambassador host program, dedicated solo cabins at no supplement, and they won Cruise Critic’s 2026 Best for Solo Travelers — Luxury Category. Ships around 900 guests so it feels intimate.

8. Senior Travel Buddies
An online matching service for travelers 50+ looking for companions. At $20/year it’s low-cost. I could set up his profile and manage the platform side — he just gets a call when there’s a good match for a specific sailing.

Silver Key Tri-Lakes Senior Center — What They Offer

This is the no-brainer first step. It’s at 238 3rd St, Monument (inside the Community Presbyterian Church), open Monday–Friday 9–4. Phone: 719-884-2300. Here’s what they told me:

Connections Café Hot meals every Wednesday, $2.25 donation. Sit-down, social, zero pressure.
Line Dancing Tuesdays at 1:30 PM. No partner needed. This alone is worth mentioning.
Fitness Classes $8 drop-in or $50/month unlimited. Senior-appropriate, gentle movement.
GriefShare Last Tuesday of the month, 10:30 AM–noon. Spousal-loss support group run through Colorado Palliative & Hospice. Contact Sue Walker at 719-330-0241.
Card Games & Social Groups Ongoing. Walk-in friendly.
Arts & Crafts, Educational Workshops Varies by month — they have a printed calendar.
Group Day Trips Front Range and mountain destinations. Organized through the center.
Shuttle Transportation Available for events — they can pick him up from Monument.

No membership fee. Everything is either free or very low cost. He can literally walk in on a Monday morning and be sitting with people his age by 9 AM.

My suggestion on sequencing

I’d have him start at Silver Key — the Wednesday lunch and the Tuesday line dancing are easy, low-stakes ways to get out of the house and around people. Let that settle for a few months. Then when he’s ready to think about travel again, the grief cruise or a familiar Silversea sailing is a natural next step. The Cunard QM2 transatlantic would be my long-game recommendation for meeting women — but that’s a year or more out.
