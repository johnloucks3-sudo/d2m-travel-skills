## Port Quick Notes
**Panama City (Fuerte Amador):** Embark port on a causeway off the Pacific entrance to the canal. Casco Viejo old town, Miraflores Locks visitor center.
**Panama Canal Transit:** The centerpiece — a full daylight transit through the locks. Deck viewing; commentary. No excursion needed.
**Colón:** Caribbean-side canal city. Gatún/Agua Clara Locks, Panama Railway, Portobelo. Viking-recommended tours competitive vs PE here.
**Puerto Limón:** Costa Rica's Caribbean coast — rainforest, Braulio Carrillo aerial tram, banana/cacao plantations, wildlife. PE saves ~$21pp.
**Roatán:** Honduran Bay Island — reef snorkeling, beaches, monkey/sloth encounters. Highest-rated tour in PE dataset. PE saves ~$40pp.
**Belize City:** Gateway to Maya ruins (Altun Ha, Lamanai), cave tubing, barrier reef. Viking-recommended tours better value here.
**Cozumel:** Mexico's premier dive island — Palancar reef, Mayan ruins, beach clubs. Christmas Eve call. PE top-rated ★4.78.
