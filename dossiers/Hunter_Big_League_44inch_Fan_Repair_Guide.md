## ⚠️ SAFETY FIRST: PRE-REPAIR CHECKLIST
Before opening or touching any internal electrical components:
1. **Shut off power at the main circuit breaker panel** (do not rely solely on the wall switch).
2. Use a non-contact voltage tester at the fan canopy to confirm power is completely zero.
3. Use a sturdy stepladder; do not work alone if pulling the fan down from the ceiling.

---

## 🔍 1. IDENTIFYING YOUR FAN SPECIFICATIONS
* **Model Location:** On Hunter fans from this era, the silver/black model sticker is located on the **top surface of the main motor housing** (facing the ceiling).
* **Common Model Numbers:**  
  • **20436:** Hunter 44" Big League (Natural Wood / Baseball Glove Motif)  
  • **20438:** Hunter 44" Big League (White / Sports Edition)  
* **Electrical Architecture:** AC Motor, 3-Speed Pull Chain (or optional Hunter RF Remote / Wall Switch), CBB61 Dual/Triple Capacitor (4.5uF + 6uF + 5uF typical).

## 🧮 2. DIAGNOSTIC MATRIX (SYMPTOM vs. CAUSE vs. FIX)

| Symptom / Observed Behavior | Most Likely Cause | Primary Repair Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fan hums loudly but blades do not spin** | Blown/Deteriorated Motor Start Capacitor | Replace CBB61 Capacitor in lower switch housing ($8–$12 part). |
| **Fan only spins on High speed (Medium/Low fail)** | Defective 3-Speed Pull Chain Switch or Capacitor | Replace Zing Ear TP-01 / Zing Ear ZE-268S6 4-wire switch. |
| **Fan runs very slowly on all speed settings** | Degraded Capacitor microfarad rating (µF drop) | Replace CBB61 4.5µF/6µF/5µF 5-Wire Capacitor. |
| **Fan wobbles excessively at high speed** | Loose blade iron screws or warped wooden blades | Tighten blade-to-hub screws; balance with weighted clip kit. |
| **Light fixture works, but fan motor completely dead** | Blown thermal fuse or disconnected wire nut in canopy | Check wire connections inside ceiling outlet box / canopy. |
| **Fan operates via remote, but remote unresponsive** | Blown remote receiver internal fuse or dip-switch mismatch | Reset receiver dip switches or replace 12V 23A remote battery. |

## 🔧 3. STEP-BY-STEP REPAIR PROCEDURES

### PROCEDURE A: Replacing the Speed Capacitor (Solves Humming / Slow Speed)
On 18+ year old Hunter fans, **90% of motor failures are due to a dried-out CBB61 capacitor**.

1. **Remove Switch Housing Cover:** Unscrew the 3 side screws holding the bottom switch housing (below the motor).
2. **Locate the Capacitor:** It is a small black plastic rectangular box with 4 or 5 wires leading out.
3. **Check Specifications:** Look at the microfarad rating printed on the side (e.g., `4.5uF + 6uF + 5uF 250VAC`).
4. **Disconnect & Solder/Wire-Nut:** Cut wires close to the old capacitor. Strip 1/4" insulation.
5. **Install New Capacitor:** Match wire colors to the speed switch schematic (typically Black=Power, Red=Low, Brown=Med, Purple/Grey=High).
6. **Reassemble & Test:** Tuck wires neatly, re-attach housing cover, and restore breaker power.

---

### PROCEDURE B: Replacing the 3-Speed Pull Chain Switch
If the pull chain is stuck, snapped, or won't change speeds:

1. **Access Switch Housing:** Remove bottom housing cap.
2. **Identify Switch Model:** Most 2000s Hunter 44" fans use a **Zing Ear ZE-268S6** or **ZE-108M** 4-wire switch (Ports L, 1, 2, 3).
3. **Map Wires Before Removal:**
   * **Terminal L:** Black (Power Input from Reverse Switch)
   * **Terminal 1:** Grey / Blue (High Speed)
   * **Terminal 2:** Brown (Medium Speed)
   * **Terminal 3:** Red (Low Speed / Light jumper)
4. **Release Wires:** Insert a small paperclip into the wire release slots on the old switch to pop wires free.
5. **Insert Into New Switch:** Push stripped wire ends into corresponding ports L, 1, 2, 3 until clicked tight.

---

### PROCEDURE C: Rebuilding / Replacing the Reverse Switch
If the fan spins in reverse or feels loose:
* The slide switch on the side of the switch housing controls seasonal rotation (Summer counter-clockwise for downdraft / Winter clockwise for updraft).
* Replace with a standard 6-pin DPDT slide switch if contacts are corroded.
