## Entry/Exit System (EES) — Active Since Oct 2025

The EU's Entry/Exit System launched in October 2025. At Schengen entry ports, US citizens will have biometric data collected (fingerprints, facial image, passport details, entry/exit dates). This replaces passport stamping.

- No advance registration required
- No fee
- Adds approximately 5–10 minutes to first Schengen border crossing
- For cruise passengers, the ship handles port formalities — shore excursion groups typically clear together
- No action required by passengers prior to travel
