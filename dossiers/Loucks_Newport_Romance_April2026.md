## 📍 09:30 AM | The Slow Start: Upper Back Bay
**Location:** Newport Beach Marriott Bayview (500 Bayview Cir)
Start the morning without rushing. The Marriott Bayview overlooks the Upper Newport Bay Nature Preserve. Grab a coffee and take a short walk along the perimeter path of the Back Bay. The morning light hitting the wetlands here is incredibly peaceful before the city wakes up.

## 📍 10:15 AM | The Short Drive to Family
**Route:** Marriott Bayview to 14 Vienna, Newport Beach
**Drive Time:** ~7 Minutes (2.5 miles)
**Directions:**
1. Head south on **Jamboree Rd** (towards the ocean) for about 2 miles.
2. Turn right onto **Bison Ave** (or Eastbluff Dr, depending on their specific gate entrance).
3. Proceed into the neighborhood to **14 Vienna**.

## 📍 10:30 AM – 12:00 PM | Visit with Greg & Karen
Enjoy a relaxed mid-morning visit at 14 Vienna. 

## 📍 12:00 PM | The Bridge to Island Time
**Route:** 14 Vienna to Balboa Island
**Drive Time:** ~12 Minutes (4 miles)
**Directions:** 
1. Head back out to **Jamboree Rd** and turn Right (South).
2. Follow Jamboree all the way down until it ends at Pacific Coast Highway (PCH), but turn Right just before PCH onto **Bayside Dr**.
3. Turn Left onto **Marine Ave** — you will cross the short bridge and instantly feel the shift to "island time."
4. *Parking Tip:* Finding parking on Marine Ave can be tight. Look for street parking on the immediate side streets (Park Ave or Pearl Ave) just off the main strip.

## 📍 12:30 PM | A Romantic Island Lunch
**Location:** Balboa Island (Marine Ave)
For a perfect romantic lunch, you have two distinct vibes on the Island:

*   **Option 1: The Village Inn (Classic & Lively)**
    *   *Vibe:* A Balboa Island institution with a fantastic patio, great coastal American food, and a relaxed, historic charm. Perfect for people-watching.
    *   *Link:* [The Village Inn](https://www.vibalboaisland.com/)
*   **Option 2: Basilic (Intimate & Quiet)**
    *   *Vibe:* If they happen to be open for a private/special lunch or you want something uniquely intimate, this tiny Swiss-French spot is a hidden gem. (Note: Usually dinner-only, so Village Inn is your safest lunch bet, or **Cora's** for an Italian café feel).
*   *Dani's Pivot:* If you want absolute waterfront views for lunch, drive straight onto the **Balboa Auto Ferry** (a 3-minute, highly romantic, old-school boat ride across the channel) and eat at the **Lighthouse Café** at Marina Park on the Peninsula. 

## 📍 02:00 PM | The Perimeter Stroll
**Location:** The Balboa Island Loop
After lunch, leave the car parked and walk the **Balboa Island Perimeter Path**. 
*   *The Romance:* The loop is exactly 1.7 miles of flat, paved boardwalk right against the water. You’ll walk past gorgeous waterfront cottages on one side, and the harbor filled with sailboats and Duffy boats on the other. It is quiet, sun-drenched, and perfectly paced.

## 📍 03:15 PM | The Essential Newport Tradition
**Location:** Sugar 'n Spice (310 Marine Ave)
Before heading back, you must stop at the original Balboa Island treat stand. 
*   *The Move:* Get a classic hand-dipped Balboa Bar or a Frozen Banana, split it, and eat it on a bench watching the afternoon harbor traffic. 
*   *Link:* [Sugar 'n Spice](https://sugarnspicebalboaisland.com/)
