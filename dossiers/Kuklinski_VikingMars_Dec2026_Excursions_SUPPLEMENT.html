🍽️ C — FOOD & WINE RECOMMENDATIONS

No dietary restrictions on file for any traveler. Viking Mars includes all specialty dining (Manfredi's Italian, The Chef's Table, The Kitchen Table) at no additional cost — no excursion budget needed for meals onboard. Below are port-side food experiences worth considering.

🇵🇦 Colón — Gatun Lake Canal Tour + Local Lunch
If the Viking tour includes a lunch stop, expect Panamanian staples: sancocho (chicken soup with yuca/corn), patacones (fried plantains), fresh ceviche, and arroz con pollo. Kyle/Nick's split-off aerial tram passes through Gamboa Rainforest Resort — excellent on-site restaurant with river terrace views.
🇨🇷 Puerto Limón — Tropical Fruit Tasting
The Sloth Sanctuary + Plantation tour includes tropical fruit tasting at the farm stop: pineapple, mango, papaya, banana, carambola (star fruit), and the unique pejibaye (peach palm fruit) — a Costa Rican staple boiled in salt water, served with mayo. Coffee grown on the plantation slopes; ask for fresh Café Chorreado (traditional drip).
🇭🇳 Roatán — Caribbean Seafood & Rum
West Bay Beach has excellent beachfront seafood shacks — fried snapper, conch fritters, lobster (in season). Local tip: Creole bread stuffed with seafood. AJ's Monkey & Sloth tour adds chocolate & rum tasting — Roatán rum cake and local chocolate. Kyle/Nick snorkeling split: Bite on the Beach at West Bay for fresh ceviche and cold Salva Vida beer.
🇧🇿 Belize City — Rice & Beans + Fresh Ceviche
Altun Ha tour typically returns for lunch at the ship (SBC meal, no extra cost). If a port-side meal is desired: Wet Lizard (tourist mainstay, rooftop, ceviche) or Nerie's (local favorite for rice & beans with stewed chicken — authentic Belizean). Belizean staple: "rice and beans" (red beans, coconut milk) with stewed chicken, potato salad, fried plantains. Finish with Belikin beer or One Barrel rum.
🇲🇽 Cozumel — Christmas Eve Feast 🎄
This is the port where the food experience matters most. Christmas Eve dinner on the ship will be special regardless, but Cozumel has exceptional Mexican food. The Paradise Beach pass includes ALL-INCLUSIVE food + open bar — fresh guacamole, tacos al pastor, ceviche, chiles rellenos, plus margaritas and cerveza. Dinner back on ship: The Chef's Table will have a holiday menu. Book ahead. If doing San Miguel stroll: La Choza (authentic Cozumel, family-run since 1989) or El Moro (fresh seafood on the water).
