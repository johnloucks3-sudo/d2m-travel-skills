### The headroom play
Booking the cheapest option everywhere lands at **~$1,591 pp — over $900 under your $2,500 cap.** That money is best spent *upgrading the 3–4 days you most want to remember*:

- **Crete → keep SDH-I** ($89) — already the gem, no upgrade needed.
- **Nafplion → upgrade NAV-K → NAV-F** (+$20) for the flagship Skouras cellar tasting (and ✅ limited-mobility).
- **Milos → upgrade MLO-C → MLO-D Flavours of Milos** (+$170) — the single best food experience on the whole voyage (cheese pies, cave winery, sand-cooked lamb).
- **Kusadasi → add S.A.L.T. olive-oil or Sirince cooking** if you'd rather a food day than ruins (KUS-J $219 / KUS-N $249).
- **One Athens day → upgrade PIR-S → PIR-J** (Acropolis + museum + Greek lunch, $229) for a proper capstone.

Even with **all** of those upgrades you'd land near **$2,100 pp — still under budget.**

### Money-savers if you'd rather trim
- **Skip both Athens turnaround tours** (you'll see the city on embark/disembark days anyway): −$138.
- **Make Paros (NSA-D) or a Gythion day a free ship/beach day:** −$80–90 each.
- **Repeat ports** (Gythion twice, Athens 3×) are natural "rest aboard" candidates — the Nova's pools, spa and suites are a fine day in port.
