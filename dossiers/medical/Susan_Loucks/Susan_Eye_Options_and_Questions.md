**Why can an eye be worse *after* a "successful" cataract surgery? The things to ask Dr. Smith about (each has different, often non-surgical, treatments):**
| Possible cause | What it is | Typical treatment options |
|---|---|---|
| **Macular swelling (cystoid macular edema / "Irvine-Gass")** | Fluid swelling of the central retina — a *known* post-cataract complication, often weeks–months later | **Anti-inflammatory eye drops (NSAID/steroid)**, sometimes a steroid injection — usually **NOT surgery**; often reversible |
| **Epiretinal membrane / macular pucker** (she has this, LEFT) | A thin scar film on the macula that can become more symptomatic after cataract surgery | Observe **or** membrane-peel surgery (vitrectomy) **if** it's distorting vision enough |
| **The implanted lens itself (VIVITY, an "extended-depth" lens)** | EDOF/premium lenses can cause **glare, halos, reduced contrast, or a "smudge"** some people never adapt to | Time/neuroadaptation, **glasses**, treat dry eye, or **lens exchange** (surgery) if truly intolerable — *this is a cataract-surgeon question, not retina* |
| **Leftover focus error (residual refraction)** | The new lens may not have landed on the exact prescription | **Glasses/contacts**, or a laser touch-up |
| **Dry eye** (she has it) | Surgery commonly worsens dry eye, which blurs/fluctuates vision | More aggressive drops, punctal plugs, etc. |
| **Dry AMD** (now intermediate) | The macular degeneration itself can cap clarity | No surgery; AREDS 2 + monitoring |

**Honest framing:** A retina specialist (Dr. Smith) is the right person for the *macula* causes — **swelling (CME), the membrane, and AMD** — and several of those have **non-surgical** treatments worth pursuing before any operation. If the problem turns out to be the **lens implant itself**, that's a question for the **cataract surgeon**, and Dr. Smith can say which it is. The goal of the visit: **figure out *which* of these is making the left eye worse, and what (drops, injections, glasses, or surgery) can actually help.**
