# Questions for Dr. Smith — for Susan (warm version, by Grace)
*Susan's main worry: her LEFT eye is worse since cataract surgery, and she wants to know ALL her options — not just surgery.*

It is completely natural to want clear answers about your eyes, Susan, and writing these down is a wonderful way to make sure your doctor hears everything on your mind.

### My Left Eye and My Recent Surgery
1. Since my cataract surgery, my left eye feels worse instead of better. What do you think is causing this? Could it be swelling in the back of my eye, the membrane, my macular degeneration, or the new lens itself?
2. Is there some swelling from the surgery? If there is, could we try eye drops or an injection first to help it, rather than another operation?
3. Could the new VIVITY lens itself be causing the glare, halos, or smudge I see? If so, what can we do — adjust it, use glasses, or swap the lens? Who makes that decision, you or my cataract surgeon?
4. Is the membrane on my eye part of why things are worse? Would a procedure to peel it help, and is that something we should do now, later, or not at all?
5. My records have a note about MRIs. Is it completely safe for me to have an MRI scan with this VIVITY lens in my left eye?

### All of My Options and Next Steps
6. I want to explore every single way to help this left eye get better, not just surgery. What are all my options?
7. Could a simple update to my glasses prescription help, or is dry eye making things worse for me?
8. Dr. Luu suggested we wait and see unless it affects my daily life. It is definitely affecting my life now. If you were in my shoes, what would you do next?
9. My right eye still has a cataract. Is it worth having that cataract taken out, or would my macular degeneration limit how much better I can see?
10. Who should I see for my regular checkups now — Dr. Luu, you, or both of you? How often should I come in, and should I keep checking my eyes with the grid card at home?
11. What is a realistic picture of how my eyesight will be in the future, and do I need to get new glasses soon?

### My Macular Degeneration and General Health
12. My dry macular degeneration has changed from early to intermediate. What does this mean for my eyes over the next few years?
13. What is the chance of this turning into the 'wet' kind of macular degeneration? What specific warning signs should tell me to call your office right away?
14. Am I taking the right eye vitamin (AREDS 2)? Is there anything else I can do to protect my sight — like changing my diet, managing my blood sugar, or wearing sunglasses? Are there any new treatments out there for the dry kind?
15. Is there anything else in my records you are keeping a close eye on, like the membrane, that small bleed that cleared up, or my blood sugar?

***
Please remember to take your time reading these, and you can always ask the doctor to double-check any answers you want to write down.

— *with care, Grace*
