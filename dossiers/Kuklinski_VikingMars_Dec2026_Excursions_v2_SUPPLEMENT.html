How the $200/pp D2M Credit Works

Each of the 6 guests receives $200 in excursion credit from Dreams2Memories.

• Credit applies to any provider: Viking, GetYourGuide, or Project Expedition
• Credit is per-person, per-trip — used within the voyage dates (Dec 17–27)
• Unused credit does not carry forward
• Guest balances remaining credit after each booking

Example: Joshua books a $185 rainforest tour. His $200 credit covers it completely. He has $15 remaining credit for a second tour if he chooses.

G. Important Notes & Resources

Cancellation Policies

  • Viking Journeys: Free cancellation up to 5 days before port date
  • GetYourGuide: Free cancellation up to 24 hours before tour start
  • Project Expedition: Full refund if cancelled 7+ days before tour

What to Bring

  • Passport (for all Caribbean ports) — kept in cabin safe
  • Sun protection: SPF 50+ sunscreen, hat, UV-blocking shirt
  • Water shoes or comfortable walking shoes (reef protection + grip)
  • Quick-dry clothing for water-based tours
  • Camera & waterproof phone case
  • Small backpack for personal items (tour operators provide dry bags)

Health & Safety Considerations

  • Mobility-Friendly Track: All tours are designed with accessible restrooms & shaded rest areas. Tour operators are experienced with mature travelers.
  • Active Track: Fitness level = good health, no serious joint issues. All tours include safety briefings & professional equipment.
  • Both tracks: Travel insurance with evacuation coverage recommended (all Caribbean ports have medical facilities).
