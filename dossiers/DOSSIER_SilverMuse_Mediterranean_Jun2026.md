3a. VENICE DESTINATION INTEL — GIUDECCA ISLAND
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Compiled: March 26, 2026 | Status: READY TO SEND (pending Commander go-ahead)

Giudecca is a long, thin island directly across the Giudecca Canal from the main island —
one vaporetto stop, 2 minutes from Zattere. Most tourists never go. That's what makes it special.
Laid-back, local feel. No cruise crowds.

RECOMMENDED RESTAURANTS:

• La Palanca
  The quintessential Giudecca stop. Canal-side, mostly locals, very reasonable by Venice standards.
  Lunch only for full meals — view across to Zattere is worth building an afternoon around.
  TripAdvisor: https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g187870-d1087324-Reviews-La_Palanca-Venice_Veneto.html

• Trattoria Altanella
  Old-school Venetian trattoria, family-run for generations. Fritto misto is legendary.
  Cash only. Reserve ahead.
  TripAdvisor: https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g187870-d1490920-Reviews-Trattoria_Altanella-Venice_Veneto.html

• Skyline Rooftop Bar (Molino Stucky Hilton)
  Not for dinner — for a Spritz with one of the best panoramic views of Venice.
  The Molino Stucky is a converted flour mill; the rooftop is genuinely stunning.
  https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/vcemshi-hilton-molino-stucky-venice/

• Il Palato
  Quieter, more intimate. Good for a longer lunch. Local wine list.
  https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g187870-d6754823-Reviews-Il_Palato-Venice_Veneto.html

GETTING THERE:
  Vaporetto Line 2 from San Marco or Zattere stops — Palanca stop for La Palanca/Altanella.
  Redentore stop for the western end and Il Palato.

NOTE FOR MELISSA: These were researched in the context of their Jul 3-6 post-cruise Venice stay.
