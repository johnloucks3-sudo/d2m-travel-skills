## 🌟 THE HIGHLIGHT — DEC 18 PANAMA CANAL TRANSIT (scenic sailing)

**No excursion to book. This IS the excursion.**

The Viking Mars transits the 48-mile Panama Canal from Pacific (Fuerte Amador/Balboa) to Atlantic (Colón) — through Miraflores Locks, Gatun Lake, and Gatun Locks. Ships are lifted 85 feet above sea level. Transit takes roughly 8–10 hours.

**What to do:**
- Stake out a spot on the top deck by 0600. Binoculars are worth it.
- The Miraflores Locks are the most dramatic — close-up wall views, parallel ships in adjacent chambers.
- Gatun Lake stretch is 21 miles of jungle-lined freshwater — wildlife (birds, crocodiles) visible from deck.
- Viking will narrate throughout. The World Café serves breakfast/lunch on deck.

**Group note:** Joshua and Erica should be in comfortable deck chairs for the long stretch. Bring sunscreen — this is the equator.
