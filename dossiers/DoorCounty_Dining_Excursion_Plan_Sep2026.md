### 1.3 Wine Trail — ranked by fit to John's dry-red / Susie's white preference

Door County's wine identity leans fruit/cherry-forward — most houses are sweeter than John's Cab/Malbec preference. Ranked so the plan reads as a fit assessment, not an equal list:

| Rank | Winery | Location | Why it fits | Drive from resort | Booking |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Stone's Throw Winery** ⭐ | Egg Harbor | Door County's only ultra-premium **all-grape** winery (fresh California grapes, not fruit wine) — closest match to John's dry-red palate. Tasting in a converted 1900s dairy barn, self-paced. Small tasting fee incl. keepsake glass. | ~15–20 min | ⚪ Walk-in, open daily 10am–5pm. (920) 839-9660 |
| 2 | **Simon Creek Vineyard & Winery** | Sturgeon Bay (Egg Harbor side, off Hwy 42/57) | Largest, most modern vineyard in the county — broadest range including drier options. Deck overlooking the vines, charcuterie-board pairing, live music in season. Best "leisurely afternoon" pick for both of them together. | ~20–25 min | ⚪ Walk-in, free tasting/tours. Mon–Sun 10am–5pm (peak season). |
| 3 | **Orchard Country Winery & Market (Lautenbach's)** | Fish Creek | The cherry-identity stop — 50 estate-grown fruit wines/ciders, self-guided flight, cherry-picking if in season. Best fit for Susie's whites + the "famous for cherries" angle Commander asked for. Very short drive from resort. | ~10 min | ⚪ Walk-in. Sun 9–4, Mon–Thu 9–5, Fri–Sat 9–6. (920) 868-3479 |
| 4 | **Door Peninsula Winery** | Carlsville (between Egg Harbor & Sturgeon Bay) | Free 25-min tours on the hour 10am–4pm, 70+ wines/ciders/meads, shares the building with Door County Distillery. Good if they want a tour format rather than just tasting. | ~20 min | ⚪ Walk-in, no reservation for small groups. (800) 551-5049 |
| 5 | von Stiehl Winery | Algoma | Wisconsin's oldest winery, historic 1800s limestone cellar tours (11am/1pm), Lake Michigan terrace — genuinely special, but **~1 hr+ south of Sister Bay**. ⚠️ Flags as a long-haul day trip, not a casual stop, given the low-distance preference. Only worth it if Commander wants a dedicated "Algoma day." | **~60–70 min (one-way)** | ⚪ Walk-in. Not recommended as a default stop — optional add if trip pace allows. |

**Recommendation:** Cluster **Stone's Throw + Simon Creek** (both near Egg Harbor, ~5 min apart) as one relaxed wine afternoon; do **Orchard Country** as a separate short cherry-focused stop closer to the resort. Skip von Stiehl unless Commander specifically wants the Algoma day trip.

## 2. EXCURSION PLAN — gentle/flat, matching John's mobility profile

| Item | Location | Mobility | Cost | Booking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Eagle Tower** ⭐ TOP FLAT PICK | Peninsula State Park, Fish Creek | ✅ **Fully ADA accessible** — 850-ft ramp, never exceeds 5% grade, 16 rest points, reaches same observation deck as the 100-step stair alternative. Best mobility-certain item in the whole plan. | Park entry only (~$8/day WI plate or $13 non-resident) | ⚪ Walk-in, no reservation |
| **Eagle Bluff Lighthouse** | Peninsula State Park, Fish Creek | ✅ Grounds/paved path flat (~100 yards from lot). ⚠️ Interior tower tour involves stairs — **optional**, skip the climb and still see the exterior/grounds. | $7 pp self-guided; guided tour similar | ⚪ Walk-in, 11am–4pm daily mid-May–mid-Oct |
| **Sven's Bluff Overlook + Skyline/Shore Road scenic drive** | Peninsula State Park | ✅ Fully drivable, pull-off viewpoints — no walking required beyond the car | Included in park entry | ⚪ No booking, self-drive |
| **Cana Island Lighthouse + Ridges Sanctuary** | Baileys Harbor | ⚠️ **Partial/conditional, not clean-flat** — causeway crossing (walk or wagon ride) can flood at high lake levels; island paths are unpaved footpaths; tower climb is steep stairs. Grounds are manageable but this is not as certain a flat pick as Eagle Tower. | $7 causeway/grounds fee (approx.) | ⚪ Walk-in, 10am–5pm May–Oct. | **Washington Island Ferry** | Northport (~10 mi north of Sister Bay) | ⚠️ **Wheelchair/mobility accessibility not confirmed in research** — flag for Commander to verify directly with operator before counting on it as a low-effort day. | **Sister Bay / Ephraim / Fish Creek village walks** | All three villages | ✅ Flat, paved, short — waterfront parks, marina, galleries, Founders Square (Fish Creek). Ephraim is the quietest/most preserved; Sister Bay has the most services + largest waterfront. | Free | ⚪ No booking — self-paced |
