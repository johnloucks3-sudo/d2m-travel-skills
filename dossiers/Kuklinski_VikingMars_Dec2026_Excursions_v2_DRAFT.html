Platform-Specific Steps

Viking Excursions (My Viking Journey)

  1. Log into vikingjourney.com with cruise booking reference
  2. Navigate to "Shore Excursions" for Dec 2026 voyage
  3. Browse by port and add selected tours to cart
  4. Review charges and confirm; SBC (if applicable) applies at checkout

GetYourGuide Direct Booking

  1. Visit getyourguide.com; search destination (e.g., "Puerto Limón Tours")
  2. Filter by date (Dec 20) and guest count (3 per booking)
  3. Review ratings, cancellation policy, and confirm selection
  4. Checkout and download voucher to present on tour day

Project Expedition Direct Booking

  1. Visit projectexpedition.com; search destination by port
  2. Review "Back to Ship Guarantee" and "Cruise-Friendly Refund" policies
  3. Pay at booking; receive confirmation with meeting location & time
  4. Note: PE tours depart independently — ship does NOT hold for PE delays
