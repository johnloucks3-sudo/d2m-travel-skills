1. BOOKING & CABIN SELECTION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Use a TA with Regent experience — Regent will ONLY talk to the TA once booked
• A TA can often offer on-board credit or benefits beyond what Regent offers direct
• Itineraries may change after booking — wars, port policies, natural disasters
• Concierge level is the sweet spot — often upgraded for free, suites similar size to Penthouse
• Mid-ship, low deck for motion-sensitive travelers
• Motion sickness: Bonine free at ship's desk, bartenders make anti-motion tea
• Walk-in closet on every Regent ship at every suite level
• Check suite sizes before booking — some below-Penthouse are the same square footage

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. EXCURSIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Book as soon as they open — best ones fill fast, some ports have very limited options
• Read full descriptions: travel time, walking amount, terrain difficulty — be honest with yourself
• Consider departure time (6:45 AM?) and duration (5 hours on a bus?)
• Europe excursions: generally high quality, good guides, well-maintained buses
• Caribbean: more unpredictable quality, transportation and guides vary
• Can't book what you want? Check Destinations Services desk onboard — waitlists work, cancellations happen daily
• Some excursions have an added fee — can use on-board credit for these
• At check-in: verify all excursion tickets match what you booked
• Excursions are private contractors — NOT run by Regent
• "Tips included" does NOT cover tour guides or bus drivers — TIP THEM
• Tipping not appropriate in Japan — check Destinations desk per country
• One person should carry all group tickets to check-in (keeps group together)
• Umbrellas available departing ship if raining; towels for water/beach tours
• No bottled water on excursions — fill water bottle at ship stations before departing
• Report great (or bad) guides to Destinations desk by name

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. RESTAURANT RESERVATIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Book specialty restaurants the moment they open online
• Best time slots fill fast — coordinate with excursion return times
• Use "travelling with" button to link booking numbers for group dining
• One reservation per specialty restaurant before sailing; more available onboard
• Onboard changes: Deck 5 desk (across from Reception), one person + computer, opens 9 AM
• No reservations needed for: Compass Rose, Sette Mari
• Large groups: talk to restaurant manager in advance

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. PACKING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Pack everything you think you need — then take HALF
• Free laundry service: one-day turnaround, cleaned/pressed/folded, returned "packaged like a gift"
• Dry cleaning available for additional fee
• For 20-day cruise: pack for 5-6 days with mix-and-match
• PRO TIP: Don't separate items by person — share space across all suitcases (if one is lost, both still have clothes)
• Carry-on MUST contain: valuables, medications, laptops/tablets/chargers, change of clothes
• Regent has a "holding room" for carry-ons if cabin isn't ready at boarding
• Luggage forwarding services exist — not personally recommended but many love them

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. DRESS CODE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• "Elegant casual" required after 6 PM in common areas (with limited exceptions)
• Read Regent's website — the printed rules are the rules, ignore Facebook opinions
• Sport coat over collared shirt is fine for men — tie optional
• Formal nights are OPTIONAL — can continue with elegant casual
• Shorts/jeans after 6 PM: in-suite dining or room service only
• First and last nights of cruise: more lenient
• Special pool deck events may have different requirements — check Passages
• Tom Little: "I've dined with the Captain wearing a polo shirt and nice slacks"

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. WHAT TO BRING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Power strip (NOT surge protector) — never enough outlets, even on newer ships
• Small fan — suites are climate controlled but airflow matters
• Small speaker for phone music
• Laptop optional — "aren't you on holiday?"
• Only the credit cards + ID you need — some cards charge foreign transaction fees
• Cash: credit card + USD sufficient most places; ship can convert currency in some regions
• Leave expensive jewelry at home — every suite has a safe
• Never wear expensive items ashore — pickpocket risk in ports

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. BEFORE THE CRUISE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• READ AND FOLLOW all pre-cruise communications — complete online prerequisites
• ALWAYS arrive at embarkation port one day early — missed flights happen
• If Regent transfer not included: Lyft/Uber often cheaper and more efficient
• Regent transfers are contracted out (not Regent employees) — results vary
• Phone: purchase cruise plan or overseas plan, otherwise keep on airplane mode

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. BOARDING & FIRST DAY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Tip luggage handlers at the dock
• Keep essentials bag with you (jewelry, meds, anything you can't do without)
• Check-in depends on port operations — Regent doesn't control it
• Key card: VERY IMPORTANT, keep it with you entire cruise (small wallet)
• Facial recognition may be used for boarding/disembarking
• Suites ready ~2-3 PM on changeover day
• While waiting: explore the ship — reception desk, destinations desk, restaurants, boutiques, spa, pool, lounges
• Lunch available: La Veranda (buffet) and pool bar
• CULT FAVORITE: Lobster nachos at the pool — popular first onboard treat

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9. SUITE AMENITIES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Walk-in closet + plenty of drawer space
• Umbrella, refillable water bottles, stocked refrigerator
• Concierge and above: bag (yours to keep) + binoculars
• Champagne on ice, fresh flowers, fruit bowl (refreshed regularly)
• Steward/butler: your greatest asset — can get almost anything
  - Specific items for fridge, bottles of liquor (no charge unless premium)
  - Bed made to preference, mattress swap (even on day one)
• Luggage: slides under bed; excess in closet; butler helps if needed
• Passages newsletter: daily, covers next day's activities — READ IT
  - Keep a copy with port/dock info when going ashore
  - Channel 1 on TV: morning briefing with key info
  - TV also shows restaurant menus daily

10. FOOD & DINING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Food is exceptional on every ship — "if you have a problem, you're probably the problem"
• Don't like something? Tell your waiter — replacement comes quickly
• Half portions available at ANY restaurant
• Order off-menu: pancakes/waffles at breakfast, custom pasta at lunch
• Ask the chef to make their favorite pasta dish — "the chef will be pleased and you'll be delighted"
• At breakfast: ordering any egg dish gives you TWO unless you specify otherwise
• Day's special is usually outstanding
• Vegetarian: always plenty of options; gluten-free/allergies: tell restaurant manager

### Cult Favorites
• Lobster nachos — pool deck
• Salt bread / salt sticks — Compass Rose
• Escargot — in Compass Rose (NOT Chartreuse)
  - Pro tip: ask waiter NOT to bring garlic toast — other breads absorb the garlic butter better
• Miso glazed sea bass — Compass Rose menu on Seven Seas Society night (DO NOT MISS)
• Prime 7: onion rings, surf and turf, popcorn sundae (so rich half size recommended)
• Pool bar burgers — often regionally themed
• Pool bar pizza — if cold, ask for a fresh one made to order (delivered to your table)
• Lunch buffet: always a pasta station with daily feature, always interesting/innovative
• Special dining events: BBQ on pool deck — GO, crew makes it incredible (warning: calories)

### Wine
• Featured white + red at dinner — usually quite good
• Don't like the featured wine? Ask for anything — sommelier will find it
• "Go to" wines: just ask and they'll stock it (Tom likes Raymond Red Blend, Susie likes Villa Maria Chenin Blanc)
• Can have a bottle of each in your suite
• Premium wine list available for a fee
• PRO TIP: Wine not limited to dinner — have some at lunch, champagne too. You're on a glorious trip.

11. ONBOARD ACTIVITIES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Try EVERYTHING — all lounges, deck games, activities
• Deck games: easy, fun, earn Rewards Points (Regent-logo shirts, bags, golf balls, visors)
• Trivia: usually 4:30 PM, teams of 6 — "if you really want to win, get Brits or Aussies on your team"
• Afternoon tea at 4 PM — Chocoholic Tea Time is "truly decadent"

### Lounges
• Differ by ship — try each one, different bartenders/specialties/entertainment
• Observation Lounge: on all ships except Navigator, great views, largest lounge
• Navigator lounges: smaller but still terrific
• Furniture is heavy — intentional for rough seas
• PRO TIP: Meet new people on excursions? Invite them to a lounge that evening

### Entertainment
• Production shows: ~9:30 PM, ~45 min, usually musicals — young, energetic cast
• Guest entertainers: vocalists, instrumentalists, comedians, jugglers, ventriloquists
• KREWE KAPERS: crew-produced show, near end of cruise — DO NOT MISS
  - Get there before doors open, drinks/snacks served before show
  - "Take a tissue with you. Trust me."
• Guest lecturers: almost daily, replayed on TV if you miss them

### Boutiques, Casino, Spa
• Boutiques + casino: open at sea ONLY, shut down in port
• Boutiques: jewelry, perfume, designer items — expensive, duty-free, limited sizes for larger frames
• Casino: standard, open at sea only
• Gym: adequate, classes + trainer, has a scale ("why would they do that?"), sports drinks in cooler
• Spa: contract operation, generally good, pricey — use OBC, reservations needed
• Some ships have infinity pool near gym area

### Culinary Classes
• Available on 3 newer ships, for a fee
• Hands-on, you eat what you prepare — sign up in advance

### Pool
• Padded loungers, shaded + sunny areas, towels provided
• Bar waiters readily available
• DON'T be a chair hog — crew should remove unattended items but don't always

12. INTERACTING WITH CREW
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Read name tags, address crew by name — they'll know YOUR name by day 2
• They work very hard for you and appreciate the courtesy
• Ask about their home and family — Regent encourages personality, not robots
• Record names of standout crew members — note them on cruise survey (mid-cruise + end)
• Return cruisers: crew members will remember and greet you enthusiastically

13. SUMMARY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Your cruise will be exceptional
• If you want something or have a concern — TELL SOMEONE, it will be fixed
• Fellow passengers: vast majority are delightful, rare exceptions exist
• "Above all, have a great time. Enjoy every moment and take in every port and every new experience."
