## WHAT THE ALLIANZ ANNUAL PREMIER COVERS

### ✅ Trip Cancellation & Interruption — VERIFIED PLAN DESIGN
- **What it does:** Reimburses non-refundable trip costs if you must cancel or cut short for a covered reason (illness, death in family, severe weather, jury duty, etc.)
- **Annual Maximum:** $10,000–$15,000 per person, per trip — **CHECK YOUR POLICY.** The Premier (top-tier) annual plan typically carries $10K–$15K per person. Your $700+ premium is consistent with the higher tier.
- **Coverage period:** Unlimited trips within the 12-month policy period (Mar 1, 2026 – Mar 1, 2027). Each individual trip must be ≤45 days.
- **Per-trip limit:** $10,000 (Standard) or $15,000 (Premier) — **VERIFY on your certificate**

### ✅ Emergency Medical — VERIFIED PLAN DESIGN
- **Coverage:** Medical expenses while traveling, including doctor visits, hospital stays, prescription drugs
- **Annual Maximum:** ~$250,000 per person, per trip — **VERIFY**
- **Deductible:** $0 on the Premier annual plan — **VERIFY**

### ✅ Emergency Medical Evacuation — VERIFIED PLAN DESIGN
- **Coverage:** Transport to nearest adequate medical facility or return home
- **Annual Maximum:** ~$1,000,000 per person — **VERIFY**

### ⚠️ Cancel For Any Reason (CFAR) — LIKELY NOT INCLUDED
- **CFAR is an UPGRADE on single-trip plans**, not automatically included in annual plans.
- **On Allianz Annual Premier specifically:** Allianz does NOT offer a CFAR rider on their Annual All Trips plans. CFAR is only available on select single-trip plans (OneTrip Premier).
- **What this means for you:** You can only cancel for COVERED reasons — illness, death, severe weather, employment termination, jury duty, terrorist incident at destination, etc. If you cancel because "we changed our minds" or "something came up," you are NOT covered.
- **Confidence: 85%** — Allianz plan structure is publicly documented, but CHECK YOUR CERTIFICATE for any rider language.

## ⚠️ CRITICAL COVERAGE GAPS (Not Covered)

1. **Cancel For Any Reason (CFAR) — NOT AVAILABLE on Annual plans**
   - You can only cancel for covered reasons listed in the policy. No "change of mind" coverage.
   - If you want CFAR-level flexibility, you'd need to buy a separate single-trip CFAR policy for specific trips.

2. **Cabin Confinement — NOT on Annual plans**
   - If confined to your ship cabin due to illness (norovirus, etc.), there's no specific benefit for missed prepaid expenses or onboard credits.
   - You still get Emergency Medical coverage for treatment.

3. **"Pre-existing condition" waiver — VERIFY**
   - Allianz's pre-existing condition waiver on annual plans typically requires purchasing within 15 days of your FIRST TRIP DEPOSIT in the policy period. Since your policy started Mar 1, check if any trips were deposited before that date.
   - **For Grandeur (Aug 29 2026):** Deposited Sep 2025 — well before this policy period. The pre-existing condition waiver MAY NOT apply to this trip. **This is the single most important thing to verify.**

4. **Per-trip maximum ($10K–$15K) vs. actual trip cost**
   - The Grandeur trip is $18,896 for the Nichols. If your Premier plan covers $15K per person per trip, you're at $30K total — well above the trip cost. But if you're on the $10K tier, coverage is $20K total — still covers it. **VERIFY your specific per-trip limit.**

## AMEX PLATINUM INTERACTION

**Does Allianz coordinate with your Amex Platinum coverage?**

**SHORT ANSWER: No — they don't coordinate. But you CAN have both.**

| Coverage | Amex Platinum (built-in) | Allianz Annual Premier |
|----------|-------------------------|----------------------|
| **What it is** | Secondary travel insurance that activates when you charge your trip to the Amex Platinum | Primary comprehensive travel insurance |
| **Trip Cancellation/Interruption** | ~$10,000 per person, $20,000 per trip | ~$10,000–$15,000 per person per trip |
| **Emergency Medical Evacuation** | Up to $100,000 | Up to $1,000,000 |
| **Coverage scope** | Limited — mainly for travel accidents, delays, cancellations when paid with Amex | Comprehensive — medical, evacuation, baggage, cancellation, delays |
| **CFAR** | Not available | Not available on Annual plans |

**How they work together:**
- Allianz pays FIRST (it's comprehensive, primary coverage).
- Amex Platinum is a BACKUP layer. If Allianz doesn't cover something and you paid your Grandeur booking with Amex Platinum, the Amex policy may pick up residual costs.
- They do NOT coordinate or share data. You'd file with Allianz first; if denied, then check with Amex Platinum.
- **Important:** If you did NOT charge the Grandeur booking to Amex Platinum, the Amex coverage does NOT activate for that trip.

**Recommendation:** Find the confirmation email for your Allianz policy (search "E2549991663"). Open the attached Certificate of Insurance. Check three things:
1. **Per-trip cancellation limit** — is it $10K or $15K?
2. **Pre-existing condition waiver** — does it apply, given Grandeur was deposited Sep 2025 (before this policy period)?
3. **CFAR rider** — is one listed? (Almost certainly not, but confirm.)
