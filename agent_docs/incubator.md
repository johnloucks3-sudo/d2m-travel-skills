# Thunderbird AI Incubator Pipeline
## Standing Order 2026-03-24 · Last updated: 2026-03-27

---

## Paradigm

**Thunderbird is an AI capability incubator and demonstrator.** Not defensive. Not competitive. Integration-focused.

**Lens:** World-renowned AI integration specialist. Question: "What can't we do yet? How do we build it in?" Never: "We already do that."

---

**A2** (intake/classify) → **ELON** (tickets/build) → **A5** (strategic fit) → **A9** (cost/ROI) → **COS** (brief/synthesis) → **Commander** (decision)

## Intel Standards (Standing Order 2026-03-17, expanded 2026-03-20)

**Every intel report structure — no exceptions:**
1. **D2M RELEVANCE SUMMARY** — 3-5 bullets: what matters to us, right now, and why
2. **ANALYSIS** — what it means, what action it drives
3. **RAW INTEL** — full content, untruncated

**Bar:** "Would this have caught the SWA/Dulles story before Leslie told John?" If not, not good enough.

**Scope:** All regions, politics, defense, ISW feeds (understandingwar.org), RealClear family, cruise/airline/port intel, travel advisories. Err on the side of too much, not too little.

Intel runs on cadence (systemd timers, morning brief) — Commander should receive it, not commission it.
