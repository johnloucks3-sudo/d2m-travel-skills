## HOW TO READ THIS FILE

Six pairings are **★ CANONICAL** — the architecture doc (`intel/REVERIE_EMOTIONAL_ARCHITECTURE.md`, Part 2) names these as a traveler type's natural home pillar. Those get a full, bespoke opening paragraph. They are the strongest, most field-tested combinations and should be used close to verbatim in register, not just structure.

The other 36 cells are **off-diagonal** — a traveler type living in a pillar that isn't their default (e.g., an Achiever who is actually HELD this trip, not EARNED). These are real and happen constantly — a Curator's parent trip can be TOGETHER, an Escapist can be celebrating something WITNESSED. Each still gets a genuine, specific example, built from the same compositional formula every time:

```
[Pillar's opening thesis, one sentence]  +  [Traveler-type register modifier, one clause]
```

**Rule:** these are starting points, not fill-in-the-blank scripts. If a client profile lands on an off-diagonal cell, Luna (A6) reviews and rewrites for the specific client before the opening leaves the narrative pass — do not ship a template sentence verbatim to a client. The canonical cells have earned more trust through repeated use; the off-diagonal cells have not yet and should be treated as first drafts.

**Register modifiers per traveler type** (used consistently across every pillar below):
- **Achiever** — direct, unapologetic about value, no hedging
- **Curator** — names the rare/specific angle, assumes sophistication
- **Connector** — names the group/family explicitly, comfort-of-others framing
- **Escapist** — short sentences, release language, minimal detail upfront
- **Witness** — urgency and seriousness, no cheerful throat-clearing
- **Celebrant** — names the occasion before anything else

## I. THE RETURN
*"Travel doesn't take you away from your life. It returns you to who you were before your life became what it is."*

**★ THE RETURN + Achiever (CANONICAL)**
> You've spent years being excellent at everything except this. The version of you that existed before the calendar filled up is still in there — this is where you go to remember what he sounds like when he isn't performing.

**THE RETURN + Curator**
> You have been everywhere except back. This itinerary isn't a new stamp in the passport — it's the one place your collection has been circling without landing.

**THE RETURN + Connector**
> This isn't a trip you're managing for everyone else this time — for these eleven days, you get to be the one who's cared for, too.

**THE RETURN + Escapist**
> Nobody needs you for eleven days. Not your inbox, not your calendar, not the version of you that answers at 11pm. Just go.

**THE RETURN + Witness**
> The place hasn't changed as much as you have — which means this trip is less about seeing it again than about seeing what's still true.

**THE RETURN + Celebrant**
> Twenty years since you were last here together, and the timing — this exact date — is not an accident. You picked it on purpose.

## II. HELD
*"The relief of being completely taken care of. Not pampered. Held."*

**HELD + Achiever**
> You don't need to manage this one. Every decision that used to be yours to make has already been made — correctly.

**HELD + Curator**
> Even the details you'd normally insist on vetting yourself have already been handled to a standard you won't need to check.

**HELD + Connector**
> Every person in your group is covered, confirmed, and accounted for — which means for the first time on this trip, you don't have to be the one holding it together.

**★ HELD + Escapist (CANONICAL)**
> The logistics have disappeared. Not simplified — gone. There is nothing left for you to manage between now and the moment you step off the plane into someone else's competence.

**HELD + Witness**
> What you're going to see doesn't need your attention split with logistics — we've cleared the path so all of it can land the way it's supposed to.

**HELD + Celebrant**
> The only thing on your calendar for this occasion is the occasion itself — everything underneath it is already handled.

## III. WITNESSED
*"The deep human need to have one's life seen — and found beautiful."*

**WITNESSED + Achiever**
> You built this. Quietly, over years, without needing anyone to notice — and this trip is the one place that gets to notice anyway.

**WITNESSED + Curator**
> This is the itinerary you'd build for someone whose taste you trusted completely — because that is, in fact, exactly what this is.

**WITNESSED + Connector**
> Years from now, this is the trip your family points to — and every person in it is going to feel like the reason you built it.

**WITNESSED + Escapist**
> Nobody's asking anything of you here except to notice that this — this exact version of a life — is one worth having built.

**WITNESSED + Witness**
> You came to see something true, and what's true is that a life spent noticing this much is a life lived correctly.

**★ WITNESSED + Celebrant (CANONICAL)**
> Your life is worth exactly this. Not as a reward for getting through something — as a plain fact, the kind that deserves a harbor like this one to say it out loud.

## IV. EARNED
*"The particular satisfaction of a reward that took time."*

**EARNED + Achiever**
> Nobody handed you this. You built the years that made it possible, and this is what those years are for.

**EARNED + Curator**
> This is the trip you kept almost booking for three years — and every time you didn't, you were quietly saving it for exactly this version.

**EARNED + Connector**
> Every year you put everyone else's trip first. This is the one where the math finally runs the other way.

**EARNED + Escapist**
> You don't have to justify this one. The years already did that for you.

**EARNED + Witness**
> You waited until you could do this right, not fast — and that patience is the whole reason it's going to land the way it will.

**EARNED + Celebrant**
> This is the reward for something specific, and it's been a long time coming — which is exactly why it deserves to be said plainly: you earned this.

## V. THE DREAM THAT HAS A DATE
*"The particular electricity of a journey that finally has a date."*

**THE DREAM THAT HAS A DATE + Achiever**
> "Someday" is now a date on a calendar with your name attached to it, and that's the difference between wanting something and having built it.

**THE DREAM THAT HAS A DATE + Curator**
> The dream was never generic — it was cherry blossoms on one specific morning, at one specific bend in the river — and that morning now has a date.

**THE DREAM THAT HAS A DATE + Connector**
> The whole family has heard "someday" for years. Someday just became a date everyone can put on their calendar.

**THE DREAM THAT HAS A DATE + Escapist**
> It stopped being a maybe the moment it became a booking. All that's left is the waiting, and even the waiting is part of it.

**THE DREAM THAT HAS A DATE + Witness**
> The place you've been meaning to see finally has a date attached to it — and that date arrived before the place could change without you.

**THE DREAM THAT HAS A DATE + Celebrant**
> You've said "someday" about this for years, at every anniversary, every birthday — and this is the year someday finally has a date.

## VI. DEPTH
*"The hunger to feel the weight of the world."*

**DEPTH + Achiever**
> This isn't the version of the trip everyone else takes — it's the one that requires knowing what to actually ask for, which you clearly do.

**★ DEPTH + Curator (CANONICAL)**
> This is not the Santorini of the postcards. It's the Santorini of the fishermen who are still out before the ferries arrive — the version most people never get shown, because most people never ask.

**DEPTH + Connector**
> This is the version of the place your group won't forget in a slideshow — the one where you actually met someone who lives there, not just saw where they live.

**DEPTH + Escapist**
> No guidebook circuit, no schedule that runs you ragged — just the real texture of a place, at the pace of someone who isn't performing tourism.

**DEPTH + Witness**
> What you're going to encounter is disappearing in ways most travelers never notice — this is the difference between visiting and actually witnessing it before it's gone.

**DEPTH + Celebrant**
> For an occasion like this one, the packaged version wasn't going to be enough — so this is built around the real thing instead.

## VII. TOGETHER
*"The trip as relationship repair, renewal, or revelation."*

**TOGETHER + Achiever**
> You've spent years being excellent separately. This is built so you get to be excellent at something together instead.

**TOGETHER + Curator**
> The place is remarkable — but what this trip is actually collecting is uninterrupted time with the person you're taking it with.

**★ TOGETHER + Connector (CANONICAL)**
> Every person in this group is accounted for, and every day is built so the group gets to actually be a group — not three couples managing three sets of logistics, but one trip, held together.

**TOGETHER + Escapist**
> No inbox, no obligations pulling either of you sideways — just the two of you, in a place built specifically so nothing else can get a word in.

**TOGETHER + Witness**
> What you're both going to see out there matters less than the fact that you're the ones seeing it side by side.

**TOGETHER + Celebrant**
> Whatever brought you to this exact date, the point of this trip isn't the ship or the ports — it's the two (or twenty) of you, marking it together.
