Climate Preview: {{ Weather_Forecast|default('Tropical climate with warm temperatures and gentle sea breezes.', True) }}

Casual Wear: Lightweight cotton, linens, and breathable fabrics for daytime exploration.

Evening Formal: Two formal nights typically featured on luxury cruises—tuxedos or dark suits, formal gowns.

Casual Evening: Smart casual—dress pants/slacks with collared shirts or elegant dresses.

Shore Excursions: Comfortable walking shoes, sun protection, light layers.

Don't Forget: Sunscreen (SPF 30+), sunglasses, hat, medications (original bottles), and any specialized gear.

Onboard Charges: Your cabin is billed to a single account. Gratuities are typically added at approximately {{ Gratuity_Rate|default('$15-16 per person per day', True) }}.

Local Currency: ATMs are available at most ports. We recommend notifying your bank of travel dates.

Tipping at Ports: 15-20% customary for guides, drivers, and restaurant servers.

Credit Cards: Visa and Mastercard accepted worldwide. Notify your bank of international travel to avoid fraud blocks.

Travel Documents: Valid passport required (check expiration—must be valid 6 months beyond travel dates).

Medications: Bring sufficient quantities in original bottles with prescription labels. Pack in carry-on luggage.

Travel Insurance: We recommend comprehensive coverage including trip cancellation and medical evacuation.

Vaccinations: Check with your healthcare provider regarding recommended vaccinations for your destinations.

Medical Facilities: All luxury ships feature onboard medical clinics staffed by doctors and nurses.

Fitness & Wellness: Most ships include gyms, yoga, and wellness programs. Check your ship's daily bulletin for schedules.

Motion Sickness: Consider medication or pressure bands if prone to seasickness, especially on rough seas.

Internet: WiFi packages available for purchase or as part of premium cabin categories. International roaming charges apply if using cellular.

Phone Service: Satellite phone calls are extremely expensive. Consider local SIM cards at ports for emergencies.

Adapters: Most international ships use 110V and 220V outlets. Universal adapters recommended.

Photography: Memory cards and batteries readily available on ship, but at premium prices—bring extras.

Passwords & Security: Use VPN for sensitive transactions on public WiFi. Store documents digitally in secure cloud storage.

Dining Schedule: {{ Dining_Schedule|default('Multiple seating times available for main dining room and alternative restaurants.', True) }}

Dietary Restrictions: Notify dining staff of allergies or preferences—most ships accommodate special diets with advance notice.

Shore Excursions: Book early through your cruise line for the best availability. Moderate, strenuous, and easy options available for all fitness levels.

Local Guides: Licensed local guides provide exceptional insight into history, culture, and hidden gems.

Independent Exploration: Tender ports require tenders (small boats) to go ashore. Plan accordingly based on ship schedule.
