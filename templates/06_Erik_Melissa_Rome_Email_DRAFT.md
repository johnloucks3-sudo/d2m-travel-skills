**JUNE 21 — OPTION B: LA TERRAZZA ROOFTOP BAR, FLORENCE** (romantic aperitivo before the train back)
Hotel Continentale | Ponte Vecchio views | Sunset cocktails
~35 euros/person (cocktail + Italian small plates)

If you'd rather take in Florence a little longer before the train, La Terrazza sits on top of a medieval tower at Hotel Continentale, directly overlooking the Ponte Vecchio.
