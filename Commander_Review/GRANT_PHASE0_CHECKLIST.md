# GRANT PHASE 0 — REGISTRATION CHECKLIST
## Dreams2Memories Travel, LLC · Action Required Before Any SBIR Submission

*These are manual registrations — no Claude session needed. Complete before April 1.*

---

## 1. SBA VetCert — SDVOSB Certification
**URL:** https://veterans.certify.sba.gov/
**Time:** ~30 minutes to apply, 12 days to process
**What you need:**
- [ ] DD-214 (Member Copy 4)
- [ ] VA disability rating letter (60%)
- [ ] LLC formation documents (Articles of Organization)
- [ ] EIN confirmation letter from IRS
- [ ] Business bank account statement showing D2M LLC
- [ ] Personal financial statements

**Why it matters:** Required for 5% SDVOSB set-aside contracting. Strengthens SBIR applications. Start today — processing clock begins on submission.

---

## 2. SAM.gov Registration — Get Your UEI
**URL:** https://sam.gov/
**Time:** ~1 hour to register, 1-3 business days for UEI
**What you need:**
- [ ] EIN for Dreams2Memories Travel, LLC
- [ ] NAICS code: **561510** (Travel Agencies) — primary
- [ ] Secondary NAICS: **541511** (Custom Computer Programming) — for SBIR applications
- [ ] Business bank account (for EFT setup if federal payments ever come)
- [ ] Legal business name, address, and contact info

**Note:** UEI replaces DUNS number. Required for every federal grant and contract. Must be active 6-8 weeks before SBIR submission.

**Check first:** If your other LLC had a SAM.gov registration, that UEI is entity-specific. D2M LLC needs its own.

---

## 3. Grants.gov Account
**URL:** https://grants.gov/
**Time:** 15 minutes
**What you need:**
- [ ] SAM.gov UEI (complete Step 2 first)
- [ ] Organization email address

**Note:** NSF SBIR submissions go through grants.gov. Create account as soon as UEI is confirmed.

---

## 4. IVMF Military Founders Lab Application
**URL:** https://ivmf.syracuse.edu/programs/entrepreneurship/start-up/military-founders-lab/
**Cohort start:** April 20, 2026 — **apply this week**
**Time:** 30-45 minutes
**What you need:**
- [ ] Brief business description (use the narrative hook from Grant Narrative v2, Section 10)
- [ ] Revenue stage (early revenue — $15,666 commissions)
- [ ] Service era and branch
- [ ] Goals for the program

**Why it matters:** Free. Network access is worth more than the $15K nominal grant. JPMorgan Chase CEOcircle pipeline for post-accelerator.

---

## 5. Mt. Carmel VBOC — Colorado Springs
**URL:** https://www.veteranscenter.org/vboc/
**Phone:** Call or walk in — they're in Colorado Springs
**Time:** 1 phone call

**Ask for:**
- SBIR application prep counseling
- SAM.gov registration help if needed
- State grant program guidance (Colorado OEDIT)
- Introduction to SBA district office (El Paso County)

**Note:** Free federal resource. They've seen hundreds of SBIR applications and will tell you exactly what NSF reviewers look for.

---

## 6. Resolve Old LLC Status
**Action:** Confirm whether your previous SDVOSB/SAM-registered LLC is:
- [ ] Active → inform VetCert during D2M application (disclose both entities)
- [ ] Dissolved → get dissolution paperwork; include in VetCert application

**Why:** SBA VetCert will ask about all business entities. Transparency = faster approval.

---

## SEQUENCE

```
Day 1:  SAM.gov registration → get UEI
Day 2:  SBA VetCert application (needs EIN + DD-214 + VA letter)
Day 2:  IVMF application (cohort April 20 — do not delay)
Day 3:  Grants.gov account (needs UEI)
Day 4:  Call Mt. Carmel VBOC
Week 3: VetCert certified (12-day processing)
Week 4: NSF solicitation opens → drafting begins
```
