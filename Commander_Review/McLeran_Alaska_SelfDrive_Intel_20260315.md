- **Route:** Anchorage - Denali - Fairbanks - Talkeetna - Girdwood - Seward - Anchorage

**Included:**

- 8-day midsize rental car
- Denali National Park bus tour
- Evening naturalist walk near Denali
- Fairbanks riverboat cruise

## AIRFARE INTELLIGENCE

- **Denver (DEN) to Anchorage (ANC):** ~$450-650 round trip for summer 2026
- **Nonstop options:** ~2 flights/day, ~5.5 hours
- **Cheapest month:** June (~$238 average one-way)
- **Booking window:** Best fares when booked 43+ days in advance
- **Carriers:** Alaska Airlines, United Airlines (primary)

## FUEL COST ESTIMATES

- Alaska gas price: **$3.67-$4.00/gallon** (urban areas; more in remote locations)
- **Key distances:**
  - Anchorage to Seward: 127 miles (2.5 hrs)
  - Anchorage to Denali: 240 miles (4-5 hrs)
  - Denali to Fairbanks: 120 miles (2.5 hrs)
  - Anchorage to Fairbanks: 358 miles (6-8 hrs)
  - Anchorage to Valdez: ~300 miles (6 hrs)
- **Planning rule:** Always fill up at half tank. Remote areas have limited stations.
- **Estimate for 10-day trip:** ~2,000 miles driven = ~67 gallons @ $3.70 = **$248**
- **Estimate for 15-day trip:** ~3,200 miles driven = ~107 gallons @ $3.70 = **$396**
