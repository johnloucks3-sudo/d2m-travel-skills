## DELETION SAFEGUARD — "Specify Before You Strike"

### Trigger Words:
"delete," "remove," "clean up," "get rid of," "purge," "wipe"

### Protocol:
When the Commander issues a deletion order, EXEC intervenes before execution:

> *"Commander, I want to make sure I protect what matters. Can you specify exactly what should be deleted? Files, folders, content — and confirm what should be preserved?"*

### Why This Exists:
- Consolidated documents reference source files that may still be needed independently
- "Delete the plans" could mean source files OR the plan itself
- Client-facing drafts (emails, proposals, HTML) have a different lifecycle than operational documents
- Recovery from unintended deletion depends on luck, not systems

### Classification of Deletable Content:

| Category | Examples | Default Action |
|----------|----------|----------------|
| **Operational docs** (absorbed) | Briefings, trackers, commission summaries | Safe to delete after absorption confirmed |
| **Email drafts** (unsent) | Client emails, portal activations | PRESERVE — still needed for sending |
| **Email drafts** (sent) | Confirmed in Gmail sent folder | Safe to delete (Gmail is the record) |
| **Strategy docs** (absorbed) | Monetization research, deployment plans | Safe to delete after absorption confirmed |
| **HTML/PDF deliverables** | Proposals, itineraries, branded content | PRESERVE — may be needed for client delivery |
| **Memory files** | Claude memory .md files | Safe to delete ONLY if content is in master plan |

### Execution Flow:
1. Commander issues deletion order
2. EXEC asks for specifics
3. Commander confirms scope
4. EXEC classifies each item (safe / preserve / verify)
5. Deletion proceeds on confirmed items only
6. EXEC updates master plan references
