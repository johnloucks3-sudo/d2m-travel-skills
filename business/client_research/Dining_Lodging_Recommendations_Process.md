## Overview

Standardized processes for dining recommendations and lodging suggestions research. Includes restaurant cuisines, hotel evaluation, property comparison, and client preference matching. Integrated with destination intelligence and event-driven lifecycle.

## Core Research Domains

### 1. **Restaurant Research & Curation**
- **Geographic Coverage:** Port cities, pre/post-cruise locations
- **Cuisine Types:** Local specialties, international, dietary accommodations
- **Price Tiers:** Budget-friendly, mid-range, luxury splurge
- **Client Matching:** Preferences, dietary restrictions, occasion type
  - **Special Occasions:** Anniversaries, birthdays, celebrations
  - **Dietary Needs:** Gluten-free, vegan, kosher, allergies
  - **Ambiance Preferences:** Romantic, lively, quiet, scenic views

### 2. **Lodging Evaluation Framework**
- **Property Types:** Hotels, resorts, boutique, all-inclusive
- **Quality Assessment:** Star ratings, guest reviews, amenities
- **Location Analysis:** Proximity to port, attractions, dining
- **Value Proposition:** Price vs. amenities, seasonal rates
- **Client Suitability:** Family-friendly, romantic, business, accessibility

### 3. **Client Preference Matching System**
- **Taste Profiling:** Culinary preferences, dietary restrictions
- **Comfort Requirements:** Room size, bed preferences, view priorities
- **Amenity Priorities:** Pool, spa, fitness center, dining options
- **Budget Alignment:** Price range expectations, value perception
- **Experience Goals:** Relaxation, adventure, cultural immersion

### 4. **Quality Assurance Checklist**
- **Vetting Criteria:** Hygiene ratings, safety standards, accessibility
- **Review Validation:** Multiple source verification, recent reviews
- **D2M Partnership Status:** Preferred partners, commission structures
- **Experience Sampling:** When possible, firsthand testing
- **Client Feedback Loop:** Post-visit reviews collection

## Research Methodology

### Phase-Based Approach
```
Destination Intelligence → Client Profiling → Research → Curation → Delivery
   (A2 Dembe)              (A6 Luna)        (A2+A6)    (A6)      (A3 Dani)
```

**Phase 1: Destination Intelligence (T-270)**
- Identify dining/lodging landscape in port cities
- Establish baseline options across price points
- Map geographic distribution relative to port
- Note seasonal availability and closures

**Phase 2: Client Profiling (T-180)**
- Analyze Google Forms responses for preferences
- Review past travel patterns and feedback
- Identify special occasions or requirements
- Establish budget parameters

**Phase 3: Targeted Research (T-120)**
- Deep dive on shortlisted options
- Contact properties for group rates/availability
- Check recent reviews and ratings
- Verify commission arrangements

**Phase 4: Curation & Presentation (T-90)**
- Create personalized recommendation sets
- Prepare visual comparisons (photos, maps)
- Develop rationale for each recommendation
- Format for client presentation

**Phase 5: Booking Support (T-60 to T-30)**
- Assist with reservations as needed
- Provide booking tips and advice
- Offer alternatives if preferred options unavailable
- Confirm details pre-travel

## Quality Standards

### Restaurant Vetting Criteria
1. **Hygiene & Safety:** Health department ratings A/B only
2. **Review Consistency:** ≥4.0/5.0 across 3+ platforms
3. **Menu Stability:** Consistent quality over time
4. **Service Standards:** Professional, attentive staff
5. **Value Alignment:** Price justified by quality
6. **Accessibility:** ADA compliant where applicable
7. **Sustainability:** Ethical sourcing, environmental practices

### Hotel Vetting Criteria
1. **Cleanliness:** Spotless rooms, common areas
2. **Maintenance:** Well-maintained facilities
3. **Staff Service:** Professional, responsive
4. **Amenity Functionality:** All advertised amenities operational
5. **Location Accuracy:** As described, safe neighborhood
6. **Sleep Quality:** Quiet, comfortable beds
7. **WiFi Reliability:** Strong, consistent connection

### Client Matching Accuracy
- **Preference Alignment:** ≥90% match to stated preferences
- **Budget Compliance:** Within 10% of stated range
- **Experience Delivery:** Meets or exceeds expectations
- **Special Requirements:** 100% accommodation when possible
