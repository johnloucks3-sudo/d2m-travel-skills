Your Journey

June 12 – 15 · Rome

The Eternal City — all together

You begin where the whole family is together. Three days in Rome to settle in after the flight from Denver and take in the city at an unhurried pace — the Vatican Museums and the Sistine Chapel, the Colosseum, St. Peter's, and an evening tour when the monuments are lit and the crowds have thinned. A private welcome dinner is woven in to mark Bill & Kathleen's 50th.

June 15 – 23 · Mediterranean Voyage aboard the Disney Wish

Eight nights through Greece and southern Italy

From Rome's port at Civitavecchia, the family sails the Mediterranean together — a rhythm of storied islands and quiet days at sea.

We'll bring you a hand-picked set of shore excursions for each port to choose from — and we'd love to hear which Santorini experiences are already on your list.

June 23 · Two paths home

A gentle fork in the road

After the voyage, the family divides for the journey home. Tim & Joanne and the boys fly home from Rome, while the rest of the family continues north — first to Florence, then into the Alps. We'll arrange both movements with care so the day is smooth on every side.

June 23 onward · Florence

Tuscany and a table of your own

A stay in the heart of the Renaissance, reached by a fast train up from Rome. The centerpiece is a hands-on cooking class — a long, unhurried afternoon learning a few Tuscan dishes and then sitting down to enjoy them together.

Late June · The Swiss Alps

Zermatt & Interlaken

Into the mountains for the part of the trip closest to the family's heart — Zermatt beneath the Matterhorn, and Interlaken in the lake country between the peaks, connected by Switzerland's scenic rail. Time to simply be in the Alps.

July 1 – 2 · Zurich

A sweet finish

The journey closes in Zurich, with a Swiss chocolate experience among the send-off touches, before the flight home on July 2.
