## 1. Liassidi Palace ★★★★
**Address:** Ponte dei Greci, 3405 — Castello district
**Phone:** +39 041 520 5658

### The Neighborhood
The **Castello** sestiere — Venice's largest and most authentically Venetian neighborhood. This area east of San Marco feels like the "real Venice" — quieter streets, local trattorias, neighborhood bacari (wine bars), and laundry hanging between buildings. You're steps from the cultural cluster around the Greek Orthodox church (San Giorgio dei Greci) and the Naval History Museum. Mornings here are peaceful before the San Marco crowds arrive.

### About This Hotel
A restored 15th-century palazzo with just 26 rooms — intimate and boutique. Every room has a hydromassage bathtub or shower. Murano glass accents, original architectural details. Entirely non-smoking. This is the kind of place where staff know your name by day two.

## 2. Colombina ★★★★
**Address:** Calle Del Remedio, Castello 4416
**Phone:** +39 041 277 0525

### The Neighborhood
You're in the **heart of Castello**, right behind the Doge's Palace — literally steps from St. Mark's Square. This stretch along the Canonica Rio canal has a direct, unobstructed view of the **Bridge of Sighs** from the hotel itself. The Calle del Remedio is a lively pedestrian street with restaurants, gelaterias, and mask shops. Touristy but undeniably magical — you're at ground zero of iconic Venice.

### About This Hotel
Two-wing property: the main Palazzo overlooking the canal (with Bridge of Sighs views) and a noble floor wing reached through a private Venetian courtyard with an ancient well. Refined 18th-century Venetian decor — damask walls, chandeliers, gilded mirrors. The canal-view rooms are the real draw.

### The Neighborhood
**Right on the Grand Canal, steps from the Rialto Bridge.** The Riva del Vin is one of Venice's most photographed promenades — the sunny side of the Grand Canal where gondolas glide past and the Rialto Market bustles every morning. Newly renovated 19th-century building with contemporary design woven into original architecture. Murano glass fixtures, Venetian textiles, modern amenities. The **rooftop terrace bar** is a standout — sunset cocktails overlooking the Grand Canal. Design-forward with polished, professional service.

## 4. Maison Venezia | UNA Esperienze ★★★★
**Address:** Ruga Do Pozzi, 4173 — Cannaregio district
**Phone:** +39 02 6982 6982

### The Neighborhood
**Cannaregio** — Venice's most authentic residential sestiere. This is where Venetians actually live. The Strada Nova (main shopping street) is nearby, but the side streets are quiet, lined with neighborhood bakeries, wine shops, and restaurants with menus only in Italian. You're near the Venice Casino and the beautiful Ca' d'Oro palace. Cannaregio also contains the historic Jewish Ghetto — the world's first — worth a quiet afternoon visit.

### About This Hotel
A restored historic building with modern Venetian-style interiors and Murano glass chandeliers. Overlooks the quiet Canal Priuli. Part of the Italian UNA hotel group — reliable quality, professional service. This hotel has the most "local" feel — you'll walk through real Venetian life to get to the tourist sites.

**Address:** Riva Ca' di Dio, 2182 — Castello / Arsenale district
**Phone:** +39 06 398 061

### The Neighborhood
The **Contemporary Art District** at the entrance to the Arsenale — Venice's ancient shipyard and now home to the Biennale exhibitions. This waterfront stretch along the Riva faces the lagoon with views toward San Giorgio Maggiore island. Quieter and more contemplative than central Venice — art galleries, the Naval Museum, and leafy public gardens are your neighbors. The Giardini della Biennale are a short walk east.

## Getting Around Venice

- **Venice is a walking city** — no cars, no buses on the islands. Everything is on foot or by boat.
- **Vaporetto (water bus)**: A 3-day pass costs ~$44/person for unlimited rides. Lines 1 and 2 run the Grand Canal. Line 4.1/4.2 goes to Murano.
- **Water taxis**: Private and dramatic (~$75-130 per trip) — perfect for airport transfers or special occasions.
- **From Marco Polo Airport**: Alilaguna water bus (~$16, 90 min) or private water taxi (~$120, 30 min) direct to your hotel's nearest landing.
- All five hotels are within Venice's historic center — nothing is more than a 20-minute walk from anything else.
