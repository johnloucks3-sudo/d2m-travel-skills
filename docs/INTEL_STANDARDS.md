# Thunderbird Intel Standards
## Standing Order 2026-03-17, expanded 2026-03-20

## Intel Report Structure — No Exceptions
Every intel report must include:
1. **D2M RELEVANCE SUMMARY** — 3-5 bullets: what matters to us, right now, and why
2. **ANALYSIS** — what it means, what action it drives
3. **RAW INTEL** — full content, untruncated

**HIGH-PRIORITY TASK NOTIFICATION:** For all high-priority tasks (P0, P1), confirm completion via direct console notification to the Commander in addition to standard inbox delivery.

Intel runs on cadence (systemd timers, morning brief) — Commander should receive it, not commission it.
Test: "Would this have caught the SWA/Dulles story before Leslie told John?" If not, not good enough.

## Scope

**2026-03-20:** "Send me MORE intel than you think I would need. You never know WHAT a client will ask." Coverage: all regions, politics, defense, ISW feeds (understandingwar.org), RealClear family, cruise/airline/port intel, travel advisories. Err on the side of too much, not too little.

## Staff Paper Format (All Persona Emails to Commander)

```
ISSUE: [one sentence]
DISCUSSION: [context, analysis, client cross-refs]
OPTIONS: [numbered, when applicable]
ACTIONS I RECOMMEND TAKING: [numbered recommendations]
---
Staff Paper from [Name], D2M Travel
```
