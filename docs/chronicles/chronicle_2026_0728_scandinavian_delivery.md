### 2. The 4-Image Overnight Doctrine — Built From Failure

**Four image audit cycles.** Each time the Wing produced images, the Commander identified exactly what was wrong. Each failure taught a specific rule. The doctrine did not exist at the start of this session. It exists now — locked in standing orders, enumerated, non-negotiable.

**The rule, codified in SO-ItineraryImages_v1.md v2.3 §9-10:**

- Single-day ports: 2 images (1 iconic landmark/scene + 1 emotional/dreamy)
- Overnight ports: 4 images minimum
- Stockholm (3 nights): 4+ images — arrival through archipelago, At Six/Östermalm at night, boat tour/harbor waterfront, Gamla Stan or Vasa excursion day
- Sea days: ship interiors only (Grandeur-specific: Observation Lounge, Chartreuse, Compass Rose, Prime 7)
- **The sail-in rule:** "What will they see arriving by sea — no manufacturing, no docks, no cranes, no construction"
