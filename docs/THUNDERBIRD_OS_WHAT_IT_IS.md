Travel is deployment #1. The OS is the product.

## What Thunderbird Is NOT Good At

Be honest about the edges:

- **High-volume / transactional** — 500 clients at $50 each. This OS is optimized for 20–50 clients at $5K–$50K each.
- **Team businesses** — Thunderbird is built for a solo Commander with AI staff. Add a human team and the C2 architecture gets complicated.
- **Commodity services** — If your value prop is price, not relationship, the voice ledger and dossier system are overkill.

Thunderbird OS is most valuable in businesses where:
