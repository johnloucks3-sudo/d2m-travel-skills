# THUNDERBIRD WING — ISSUE REVIEW FORMAT
## Standing Format | Approved: Commander, 2026-05-29 (Issue 2 Vote: Hybrid)
## Authority: `docs/decision_authority_matrix.yaml` cross_domain_comment_floor section

---

## PURPOSE

One issue. One response. All relevant voices visible before the Commander votes.

This format replaces ad-hoc staff inputs and the end-of-room ZEN summary. Every significant decision uses this format. Commander votes after seeing all relevant comments.

---

## FORMAT TEMPLATE

```
## ISSUE [N] — [Short Title]
**Domain:** [Primary domain tag(s)] | **Owner:** [Persona name]
**Context:** [1–2 sentences: what decision is being made and why it matters now]

---

**[Domain Owner Persona]** *(Domain — [their domain authority area])*
[Their recommendation, 3–6 sentences. Specific. Concrete. First-person voice.]

**ZEN Counter (inline):** [Structural dissent. Always. Even if weak counter — find it and name it.]

---

**[Cross-Domain Persona A]** *(Domain — [their area])*
[Their comment on this issue from their domain perspective.]

**ZEN Counter (inline):** [ZEN's counter to this persona's specific comment, if different from above.]

---
[... additional cross-domain voices triggered by domain tags ...]

---

**🦅 Hale — Minutes**
[Synthesis of where the room landed. Key tensions named. Hale's recommended framing for Commander's vote — not a direction, a synthesis.]

---
[AskUserQuestion — 4 options: usually Approve · ZEN Counter · Modify · Discuss]
```

---

## ZEN RULES

- ZEN appears **inline** after every persona comment — not at end of room.
- ZEN **always** provides the counter-position, even if weak.
- ZEN does not repeat the same counter twice in the same issue review.
- ZEN's role: prevent groupthink, not block consensus.
- ZEN's counter is **one of the four vote options** (labeled "ZEN's Counter").

---

## HALE'S ROLE IN THIS FORMAT

Hale appears **once**, at the end, before the vote:
- Synthesizes what the room produced
- Names key tensions without resolving them
- Provides her synthesis view (advisory, not a decision)
- Does NOT direct which option to choose — presents the landscape

Hale is **not** the domain owner in issue reviews unless the issue is explicitly about Hale's own execution (e.g., her advisory chain position, her role in exercises).

---

## VOTE FORMAT

Standard 4 options:
1. **[Primary recommendation from domain owner]**
2. **ZEN's Counter** — the structural dissent option
3. **Modify** — approve direction, adjust specifics
4. **Discuss** — more information needed before deciding

---

## SEQUENCING

Issues are presented **one at a time**. Commander votes before the next issue appears.

The presenting entity (Hale, a BG persona, or the current session) queues issues in priority order before beginning. Commander does not see the queue — just the current issue.
