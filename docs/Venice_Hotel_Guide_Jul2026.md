## General Venice Transportation Notes

- **Venice is a walking city** — no cars, no buses on the islands. Everything is on foot or by boat.
- **Vaporetto (water bus)**: A 3-day pass costs ~€40/person and is unlimited rides. Lines 1 and 2 run the Grand Canal. Line 4.1/4.2 goes to Murano.
- **Water taxis**: Private and expensive (~€70-120 per trip) but dramatic for airport transfers or special occasions.
- **From the airport (Marco Polo)**: Alilaguna water bus (~€15, 90 min) or private water taxi (~€110, 30 min) to your hotel's nearest landing.
- All five hotels are within the historic center — nothing is more than 20 minutes' walk from anything else.
