A D2M Brand Voice Card -- one page, machine-readable, deployed as a system prompt layer to every client-facing output module. Specifies: opening register, tense, specificity standard, closing weight, and forbidden words (automated, system, alert, update, platform, portal).

2. **Opt-out disclosure on voice recording.** Before Dani Voice Agent takes a single call, every client must know their call is being captured and transcribed. This is both legal requirement and moral obligation.

3. **Elevated handling protocol for health/financial data.** Auto-CRM will capture things clients mention casually -- medical conditions, financial constraints, family situations. These need flagged handling, not flat storage alongside trip preferences.

The line between "concierge who remembers everything" and "surveillance system with a friendly face" is consent. If the client knows, consents, and sees the benefit returned to them in the form of better service -- it is care. If they do not know, it is extraction. The technology is identical. The ethics are opposite.
