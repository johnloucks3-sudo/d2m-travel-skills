## DISCUSSION

### Strategic Context

Harlan's Commercial Assessment (2026-03-07) laid out three paths. We are now executing Path 1 (Vertical SaaS) via a controlled beta, which simultaneously de-risks Path 2 (White-Label to Host Agencies) by generating proof points. The beta is not a test of technology — the technology works. It is a test of **product-market fit with a specific human profile**.

### The Ideal Beta Advisor Profile

The candidate who maximizes our learning is NOT the most technically capable advisor. It is the advisor who:

1. **Needs what we built** — small operation, drowning in manual work, can't afford a VA
2. **Can articulate what's broken** — tech-curious enough to use it, not tech-native enough to build around it
3. **Will talk about it** — active in industry groups, willing to provide testimonials
4. **Sells what we know** — cruise/luxury/experiential travel aligned with our supplier expertise
5. **Trusts us** — existing relationship or warm introduction available

### Competitive Landscape for Advisor Tools

Before we price this, here is what our beta candidates are currently paying for:

| Tool | Price | What It Does | What It Doesn't Do |
|------|-------|-------------|-------------------|
| TravelJoy | $35-50/mo | CRM, invoicing, itineraries | No AI, no email triage, no proposals |
| ClientBase | ~$30/mo | CRM, client profiles | Legacy UI, no automation |
| TRAMS | ~$40/mo | Back-office accounting, reporting | Enterprise-grade, overkill for solos |
| TESS (OA) | Included | CRM, booking management | No AI beyond MAGgie chatbot |
| MAGgie (OA) | Included | ChatGPT-4o Q&A | Cannot access bookings, no operational capability |
| Fora Platform | Included w/host | Booking + CRM integrated | Proprietary, locked to Fora advisors |

**The gap Thunderbird fills:** None of these tools research destinations, draft personalized client emails, audit commissions, generate branded PDF proposals, monitor fare changes, or coordinate across multiple workflows autonomously. We are not competing with CRMs. We are creating a new category: **AI Operations Center for Travel Advisors**.

## BETA PROGRAM FRAMEWORK

### Pricing

| Tier | Monthly Price | What's Included |
|------|--------------|----------------|
| **Beta Founding Advisor** | **$149/month** | Full MVP feature set (below), 1:1 onboarding, direct Slack/Telegram support channel with D2M, input on feature roadmap |
| **Post-Beta Solo** | $199/month | MVP features, standard support, onboarding docs |
| **Post-Beta Professional** | $349/month | MVP + advanced features (fare watch, commission audit, multi-channel), priority support |
| **Post-Beta Agency** | $499/month | All features, up to 5 advisor seats, shared client database, team coordination |

**Why $149 for beta:** Low enough to eliminate price objection. High enough to signal value (not free — free users don't give real feedback). $149 anchors against TravelJoy ($50) and TRAMS ($40) while delivering 10x more capability. The "Founding Advisor" title creates exclusivity and loyalty.

### MVP Feature Set for Beta

1. **Star Protocol Email Triage** — Star an email, get a draft reply in your voice within minutes
2. **Destination Research Engine** — Ask about any port/city/region, get structured intel with D2M relevance
3. **Branded PDF Proposals** — Hotel guides, cruise comparisons, flight quotes in advisor's branding
4. **Fare Watch** — Monitor specific sailings/flights for price changes, automated alerts
5. **Commission Tracker** — Log bookings, calculate host agency splits, flag payment deadlines
6. **Client Dossier System** — Centralized client profiles with preferences, history, notes
7. **Daily Briefing** — Morning summary of pending deadlines, fare changes, client follow-ups

**NOT in beta MVP (future releases):**
- Dani-style AI concierge (client-facing bot)
- Telegram C2 command interface
- Full Wing multi-persona system
- Voice agent capabilities
- Supplier portal browser automation
- Multi-advisor team coordination
