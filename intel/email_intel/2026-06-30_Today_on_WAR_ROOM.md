** BRINGING WWII TO LIFE THROUGH STORYTELLING
------------------------------------------------------------


** By Doug Douds and Darrell Driver, June 30, 2026
------------------------------------------------------------
Why does World War II still captivate our historical imagination today? Host Darrell Driver sat down with military historian Doug Douds to explore this exact question.
Douds, who recently provided expert commentary for a new Tom Hanks documentary on the subject, highlights the vital role of storytelling in making complex history accessible to the general public. This discussion delves into why this massive conflict continues to shape modern geopolitics. Together, Driver and Douds unpack pivotal moments—like the battles of Midway, Guadalcanal, and Stalingrad—that decisively shifted momentum toward the Allies.
The conversation also examines technological game-changers and the unsung strategic advisors who quietly influenced top leaders behind the scenes. They even playfully entertain historical counterfactuals, such as what might have happened if Germany had never declared war on the United States.
