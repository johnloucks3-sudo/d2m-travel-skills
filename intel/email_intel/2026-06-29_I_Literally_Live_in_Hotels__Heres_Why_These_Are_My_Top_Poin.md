airport hotel or a luxury Hyatt stay.

Redeem for transatlantic flights

 One of the most compelling ways to redeem Chase Ultimate Rewards points for 
transatlantic flights is bytransferring to Virgin Atlantic Flying Club 
<https://thepointsguy.com/travel/transfer-chase-ultimate-rewards-points-virgin-atlantic/?utm_section=intro>

taxes and fees), meaning your 100,000-point bonus could get you up to 16 
one-way flights!

 If economy isn’t your jam, premium economy starts at 10,500 points and 
lie-flat business class starts at 29,000 points (though the taxes and fees on 
these awards can get steep).

<https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/transfer-chase-to-flying-blue/?utm_section=intro>

means the bonus could cover up to five economy flights or one business-class
