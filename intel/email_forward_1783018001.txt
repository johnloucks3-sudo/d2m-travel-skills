Bill's email linked a Bing search for traditional Kyoto streets — no article behind it, so pulling from mission knowledge. Here are the strongest afternoon options for your Kyoto day (accessed via Osaka/Kobe port):

TOP PICKS — Traditional Streets & Neighborhoods:

1. Higashiyama District (Ninenzaka / Sannenzaka)
   Best single afternoon destination. Stone-paved lanes, wooden machiya shops, tea houses.
   Walk from Kiyomizudera temple down to Gion. 2–3 hours.

2. Gion — Hanamikoji Street
   Geisha district. Late afternoon is prime time; maiko/geisha often visible heading to engagements after 5pm.
   Combine with Higashiyama for a full traditional loop.

3. Nishiki Market
   Covered street market — Kyoto's kitchen. 100+ vendors, pickles, tofu, grilled skewers, matcha.
   Closes ~6pm; plan for late afternoon entry.

ACTIVE OPTIONS:

4. Fushimi Inari Shrine
   Thousands of vermillion torii gates ascending a mountain. Can do 30 min (lower loop) or 2+ hrs (summit).
   No crowds at the upper trails.

5. Arashiyama — Bamboo Grove + Tenryu-ji Garden
   15 min from central Kyoto. Bamboo corridor + UNESCO garden + river. Rickshaw rides available.

EXPERIENCE ADD-ON:

6. Traditional Tea Ceremony (En tea house or Urasenke Foundation)
   Pre-booking required. 45–60 min seated ceremony. Strong D2M value-add to arrange.

RECOMMENDATION:
Higashiyama → Gion loop + Nishiki Market is the strongest half-day traditional Kyoto circuit.
Add Fushimi Inari if the group has mobility for 45+ min of stairs/incline.
