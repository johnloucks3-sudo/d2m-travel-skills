## D2M RELEVANCE SUMMARY — TOP 5 ACTIONABLE SIGNALS

1. **Real Estate → Post-Sale Lifecycle Management**
   RE firms transitioning from one-time close revenue to recurring engagement models. AI-powered agentic CRMs retain clients post-transaction.
   **D2M Analog:** We close a booking and go dark. We should own the 18-month post-trip relationship, not just the booking.

2. **Financial Planning → AI Onboarding Cuts 4–6 hrs to 1 hr**
   Wealth managers use PreciseFP + Holistiplan to auto-process client intake.
   **D2M Analog:** Guest profile collection, preference capture, and dossier creation is currently manual. This is the same problem, solved.

3. **Virtual Meetings → Conversation-to-Completion (Zoom Agentic AI)**
   Agentic layer converts any client conversation into scheduled follow-ups, action items, and summaries — without human intervention.
   **D2M Analog:** Every Dani conversation should auto-generate task, reminder, or dossier update. This is exactly the Dani Engine's missing layer.

4. **Education → Micro-Learning / Async Content Modules**
   Engageli Studio converts PDFs and videos into interactive micro-lessons with embedded knowledge checks.
   **D2M Analog:** Pre-trip destination guides, cruise line explainers, and excursion briefings as interactive async modules — not flat PDFs. Clients learn on their schedule.

5. **Blogging → Brand Intelligence Layer (Jasper IQ)**
   Ingests style guide, past campaigns, approved messaging → generates content already in brand voice.
   **D2M Analog:** Every Dani draft, every EXEC proposal, every bulletin should run through a D2M brand intelligence layer before Commander sees it. We have voice rules; we don't have enforcement.
