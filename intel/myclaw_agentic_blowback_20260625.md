## 1. Agencies Face Agentic Blowback
**TL;DR:** Advertising agencies are racing to package AI agents as their next advantage, but the awkward twist is that brands can use the same tools to pull outsourced marketing work back in-house. WPP, Dentsu and Dept are testing agent systems, while Hyundai's bidding agents cut video CPMs 67%.

**D2M Relevance:** Disintermediation risk cuts both ways — agents that help travel agencies like D2M could also let clients book direct. Differentiation must be relationship depth + curation judgment, not booking execution.

**TL;DR:** First National Bank of Omaha is using AI agents to triage financial-crime alerts, cutting investigator time by ~50%. Agents gather evidence, summarize false positives, support sanctions and enhanced due diligence reviews — final judgment stays with humans.

## Wing Synthesis (Hale)
Three patterns confirmed across all 6 stories:
1. **Human-in-the-loop is the winning posture** — not human-vs-agent, but human-for-judgment + agent-for-execution. This is exactly the WF-17 architecture.
2. **Routing intelligence IS the product** — not the model tier, not the raw output. The layer that routes work correctly is the differentiated value.
3. **Speed of agent deployment separates winners** — agencies that adopt fastest keep the relationship; brands that wait find they no longer need the agency.

**Standing order implication:** ELON's adopt-first posture (SO_TECH_VANGUARD_ELEVATION_20260621) is validated by market data. Default-ADOPT is the correct doctrine.
