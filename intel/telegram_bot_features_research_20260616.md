### Telegram Features for Your Assistant

**1. Commands (Slash Words)**
* **What it is:** Short, pre-set words starting with a slash that people can tap to get quick answers.
* **How to use it:** You can create a `/schedule` command so a traveler can instantly see their daily flight and hotel times whenever they need them.

**2. Buttons and Keyboards**
* **What it is:** Tappable buttons that appear on the screen so people can make choices without typing.
* **How to use it:** When a traveler asks for help, you can show buttons like "✈️ Flight Info," "🏨 Hotel Address," or "📞 Call Me." They just tap the one they want.

**3. File and Photo Handling**
* **What it is:** A secure way to send and receive documents, pictures, and PDF files.
* **How to use it:** Your traveler can send you a quick photo of their baggage claim ticket, or you can send them a clean PDF of their boarding passes.

**4. Location Sharing**
* **What it is:** A way for a traveler to send their physical map location, or for you to send map coordinates.
* **How to use it:** If a traveler is looking for their private driver in a busy city, they can send their live location so the driver knows exactly where to pull up.

**5. Scheduled Messages**
* **What it is:** Setting up messages to go out automatically at a specific date and time in the future.
* **How to use it:** You can schedule a warm "Welcome to Paris! Your driver is waiting at Exit 4" note to reach their phone right as their flight lands.

**6. Inline Queries**
* **What it is:** A way to search and share information from your helper bot directly inside any chat.
* **How to use it:** If you are chatting with a traveler and their companion, you can type your bot's name to search your saved hotel list and share a nice recommendation card directly into the group.

**7. Web App Integration**
* **What it is:** Opening a small, simple webpage inside the chat window for more detailed tasks.
* **How to use it:** You can show a beautiful, interactive map where travelers can see their day-by-day route and tap on activities to see photos.

**8. Payments**
* **What it is:** A secure way to accept payments for services or bookings directly on the screen.
* **How to use it:** If a traveler wants to book an extra local museum tour on the go, they can pay the fee safely right inside the chat.

### The Top 5 to Build First

If you are working on this by yourself, it is best to start simple. Building these five features first will give your travelers the most comfort with the least confusion.

1. **Commands:** These are the foundation of everything. They give your travelers a reliable starting point. If they ever get lost or confused, they can always tap `/help` or `/start` to get their bearings.
2. **Buttons and Keyboards:** Typing on a phone while walking through a busy train station or airport is difficult. Tapping a button is easy. It keeps things stress-free for them.
3. **File and Photo Handling:** Travel runs on documents. Having a single, safe place to pass boarding passes and receipts back and forth is essential for keeping everyone organized.
4. **Location Sharing:** Being in a new city can feel intimidating. Being able to share a map pin or a live location provides immense peace of mind when finding hotels or meeting up with guides.
5. **Scheduled Messages:** Timing is everything in travel. Setting up messages in advance means you can deliver helpful reminders right when they are needed, without you having to stay awake at odd hours across different time zones.

*A quick note on safety:* Since this tool will handle important travel details, please be sure to double-check that any sensitive documents, like passport photos or personal financial info, are handled securely and not stored where others can see them.
