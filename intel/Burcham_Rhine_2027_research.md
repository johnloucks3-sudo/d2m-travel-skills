## THE ACCESSIBILITY SPINE — READ THIS FIRST (line-agnostic reality)

The biggest mobility risks on a Rhine cruise are **structural to river cruising, not specific to one line.** They apply to all four:

- **Side-by-side mooring ("rafting").** At busy Rhine ports (Cologne, Rüdesheim, Strasbourg/Kehl) ships moor 2–4 abreast on a single dock. To get ashore from an outer ship you **walk through — or up over the stairs of — the neighboring ship(s).** Unpredictable day to day. An elevator cannot solve this. *(Avalon Terms confirm it verbatim: "side by side docking… may require guests to climb and descend stairs to cross over other vessels." Sources: avalonwaterways.com/terms/; tipsfortravellers.com/10-things-european-river-cruise-lines-dont-like-talking-about/; travelpulse.com/news/cruise/the-curious-case-of-adjacent-riverboat-docking.html)*
- **Steep, narrow gangways that shift with water level.** Often a rope, not a handrail. Wheelchairs/scooters generally cannot be used on the gangway during boarding. *(Emerald Terms: "passengers in wheelchairs cannot be carried on boarding ramps (which may be steep due to water levels)." Source: emerald.cruises/US/en-US/terms-conditions/europe-river-terms-conditions)*
- **Elevators skip the top Sun Deck on ALL FOUR lines.** River ships are bridge-height-constrained; the elevator serves the interior cabin decks only. The open-air Sun Deck — the best scenic-viewing space, including the Rhine Gorge / Lorelei stretch — is **stairs-only** on Avalon, Uniworld, Emerald, and Scenic alike. *(CruiseMapper deck plans for SS Antoinette, Emerald Star, Scenic Jewel, Avalon Vista all confirm one elevator on the cabin decks only.)*
- **Cobblestone / hill ports.** Rüdesheim (the Drosselgasse), Heidelberg (castle), Marksburg/Braubach (castle climb), Strasbourg (old town), Cologne (cathedral square) all involve uneven surfaces, steps, and walking. Excursion grading matters here.
- **Companion requirement.** Uniworld and Emerald both **contractually require** a mobility-limited guest to travel with a capable companion (Rob would have Stefanie; confirm this satisfies the clause). Avalon's companion-requirement language was **UNVERIFIED** on its own Terms page.
- **Correction for credibility:** passengers do **not** step on/off "at locks" — locks are transited *with* the ship. The friction is moorings, gangways, and walking ashore.

**Implication:** No Rhine line is "accessible" in the ocean-cruise sense. The right question is not "which line is wheelchair-friendly" — none are — but **"which line's flexible/graded excursion program best lets Stefanie go active while Rob takes a gentle pace or stays aboard, and which ship gives Rob the most elevator-served comfort."** That reframes the whole comparison.

## LINE-BY-LINE

### 1. AVALON WATERWAYS — *independence champion, accessibility laggard*

- **Itinerary (CONFIRMED):** "Romantic Rhine," 8 days/7 nights, Amsterdam⇄Basel. Ports: Amsterdam (overnight), Cologne, Rüdesheim + Rhine Gorge/Lorelei, Ludwigshafen (Heidelberg), Kehl (Strasbourg), Breisach (Black Forest/Colmar), Basel. May 2027 sailings exist (e.g., May 4/8/11/25/29 on various Avalon ships). *(avalonwaterways.com; cruiseexperts.com/river-cruise/8-day-avalon-river-cruise-from-amsterdam-to-basel-2027-21236/)*
- **Ship / cabin (CONFIRMED):** "Suite Ships." Signature **Panorama Suite, 200 sq ft, with the only "Open-Air Balcony" in river cruising** — an 11-ft wall-to-wall sliding glass wall, and **the bed faces the view.** 4 decks (Indigo/Sapphire/Royal/Sky), one elevator. *(avalonwaterways.com/river-cruises/avalon-panorama/; cruisemapper.com/cabins/Avalon-Panorama-851)*
- **Independence (CONFIRMED — strongest of the four):** **Avalon Choice®** with three tiers — **Classic** (guided landmarks), **Discovery** (immersive), **Active** (hike/bike/kayak). **16 complimentary bikes + e-bikes per ship.** Markets free time hardest: *"Free Time. Reimagined,"* *"up to 28 ways to explore."* Built for opt-in, self-directed touring. *(avalonwaterways.com/why-avalon/excursions/; cruisecritic.com/articles/best-river-cruises-for-bicyclists)*
- **Mobility (CONFIRMED — weakest of the four):** Avalon is among the *least* accessible river lines. **No accessible cabins fleet-wide. No motorized scooters/wheelchairs abroad** — only a collapsible wheelchair within strict dimensions if disclosed. Terms require self-navigation of steps/ramps abroad; confirm rafting/stairs risk. Elevator does not reach the Sun Deck (strongly indicated; re-confirm per ship). *(avalonwaterways.com/terms/; cruisecritic.com/articles/a-guide-to-accessible-river-cruises)*
- **Net:** Best for Stefanie's independence; **highest mobility risk for Rob.** If Rob's limitation is minor (tires on long walks, no aid), Avalon works and shines on independence. If Rob uses any wheeled aid, Avalon is the **wrong** answer — no accessible cabin, restrictive wheelchair policy.

### 2. UNIWORLD — *luxury hand-holding; built for guided immersion, not independence*

- **Itinerary (CONFIRMED for 2026; 2027 CHANGED — re-verify):** "Castles along the Rhine," 8 days. Ports: Amsterdam, Zons/Cologne, Koblenz/Boppard, Rüdesheim, Germersheim, Strasbourg (berths at Kehl), Breisach, Basel. **⚠️ For 2027 the base "Castles" product appears to terminate at Arnhem, not Amsterdam** — the full Amsterdam–Basel corridor in 2027 is the new 10-day "Castles along the Rhine & Amsterdam" or the 11-day "Remarkable Rhine & Historic Holland." **Pull a live 2027 departure page for the actual May date.** *(uniworld.com/us/river-cruise/central-europe/rhine/castles-along-the-rhine/; uniworld.com/us/ships)*
- **Ship / cabin (CONFIRMED):** Ultra-luxury "floating boutique hotels" — maximalist decor, antiques, original art. **All-inclusive incl. gratuities + bicycles.** All cabins ≥ French balcony; higher decks get full balconies; lowest deck has fixed windows. 4 decks; **elevator serves the 3 cabin decks, NOT the Sun Deck** (Super Ships add a chair-lift to the Sun Deck — UNVERIFIED on primary source, confirm with Uniworld). *(quirkycruise.com/review-uniworld-boutique-river-cruises/; cruisemapper.com/deckplans/SS-Antoinette-913)*
- **Independence (CONFIRMED — weakest fit for the stated preference):** Uniworld's DNA is **curated, included, guided** group touring ("Masterpiece Collection"). It DOES offer free time and bikes — Strasbourg day verbatim: *"You have the entire afternoon to see more of Strasbourg on your own… take advantage of Uniworld's bicycles."* But each day is structured as *"choose one of several **guided** options,"* not "do your own thing." A client who never wants a group tour **can** opt out and use free time/bikes — but pays a premium for an included-excursion model she'd underuse. *(uniworld.com/us/excursions; uniworld.com/.../castles-along-the-rhine/)*
- **Mobility (CONFIRMED — strongest *support*, but still no accessible cabins):** Uniworld's contract is explicit: it will **not** provide a wheelchair or personal care, and **requires a capable companion.** No wheelchair-accessible staterooms (shower grab bars only — UNVERIFIED). It "will make reasonable efforts to assist" and may add free time when sites are inaccessible. The hand-holding/included-service model is the most supportive *experience* for a slower traveler even though the hardware isn't accessible. *(uniworld.com/us/terms-and-conditions/2026)*
- **Net:** Most comfortable, most-included experience for Rob's pace — but it **fights** Stefanie's independence preference, and 2027 routing needs confirmation. The premium buys guided immersion she's told us she doesn't want.

### 3. EMERALD CRUISES — *best self-guided tooling; contemporary value*

- **Itinerary (CONFIRMED):** "Jewels of the Rhine," 8 days/7 nights, Amsterdam⇄Basel. Ports: Amsterdam, Alkmaar, Düsseldorf & Cologne, Koblenz + Rhine Gorge, Heidelberg, Kehl/Strasbourg, Breisach/Freiburg, Basel. *(Rüdesheim UNVERIFIED on this routing — confirm 2027 port list.)* *(globaljourneys.com/trip/emerald-cruises-ewac-1)*
- **Ship / cabin (CONFIRMED):** Contemporary "Star-Ships," ~180 guests. Signature **indoor heated pool that converts to a cinema**, and the **Panorama Balcony** (top half of window wall electronically lowers). Cabins 162–315 sq ft. 4 decks; **elevator serves 3 of 4 — Sun Deck is stairs-only.** Same parent as Scenic (Scenic Group); positioned as the value brand. *(emerald.cruises/.../our-ships-europe; globalrivercruising.co.uk/ships/emerald-star/; cruisemapper.com/deckplans/Emerald-Star-1223)*
- **Independence (CONFIRMED — strong, best *tooling*):** **EmeraldACTIVE** (e-bike rides, hikes, kayaking) + **EmeraldPLUS** (cultural), both included. **Complimentary bikes/e-bikes.** Distinctive: a **"Tailormade" GPS app for self-guided walking/biking tours** — the most explicit self-guided support of the four. Caveat: EmeraldACTIVE bike spots are first-come, first-served. *(emeraldcruises.com/river/river-experience/excursions-experiences; cruisecritic.com/articles/why-emerald-is-the-river-cruise-line-for-you)*
- **Mobility (CONFIRMED — constrained, but ~1 accessible cabin exists):** Terms require a **capable companion**; **folding wheelchairs only**; elevator may not reach all decks; cabin doors/restrooms may be too narrow; crew may **refuse participation** or **decline the booking**. Emerald Sky and Emerald Star each have **one** wheelchair-accessible cabin (most other Star-Ships have none) — slightly better than Avalon's zero, but availability is nearly nil and not guaranteed. *(emerald.cruises/US/en-US/terms-conditions/europe-river-terms-conditions; cruisecritic.com/articles/a-guide-to-accessible-river-cruises)*
- **Net:** Best self-guided tooling (GPS app + e-bikes) for Stefanie at a friendlier price point. Mobility is yellow-to-red like the others; the lone accessible cabin is a thin edge over Avalon if Rob needs one — book early or it's gone.

### 4. SCENIC — *the split-pace answer: same-day Active/Moderate/Relaxed grading*

- **Itinerary (CONFIRMED line + corridor):** "Rhine Highlights," 8 days/7 nights, Amsterdam⇄Basel aboard a Space-Ship (e.g., Scenic Opal). Sample ports: Amsterdam (overnight), Cologne, Koblenz + Rhine Gorge, Mannheim (Heidelberg), Rastatt (Baden-Baden), Kehl/Strasbourg, Basel. *(Rüdesheim/Breisach UNVERIFIED on the specific 2027 manifest; exact May 2027 dates need a live booking-engine pull — Scenic's deep-link pages now redirect to the destinations hub.)* *(adventure-life.com/europe/rhine-river/cruises/14292/rhine-highlights; scenic.cruises)*
- **Ship / cabin (CONFIRMED):** All-inclusive **ultra-luxury** "Space-Ships." Signature **Scenic Sun Lounge** — push-button window lowers to make an indoor/outdoor space (on Balcony/Junior/Royal Suites). **Butler in every suite** (laundry, canapés, in-suite dining). 4 decks; **elevator serves the 3 cabin decks, NOT the Sun Deck.** *(scenic.cruises/.../ultra-luxury-suites; cruisemapper.com/deckplans/Scenic-Jewel-1088)*
- **Independence (CONFIRMED — strong, and uniquely split-pace):** **Scenic Freechoice** = menu of included excursions **graded the same day into Active / Moderate / Relaxed.** Plus **Scenic Enrich** (exclusive signature events) and **complimentary e-bikes** for independent exploration. The three-tier *same-day* grading is the key differentiator: **Stefanie takes the Active e-bike/hike while Rob takes the Relaxed museum/easy-walk option — on the same port day.** Caveat: tiers are *guided* small-group; pure unguided "no group at all" isn't an explicit Freechoice category, but Relaxed tier + free time + e-bikes deliver the independence in practice. *(scenicusa.com/river/river-experience/excursions-experiences/europe-included-excursions; deluxecruises.co.uk/cruise-lines/scenic-tours-operator/)*
- **Mobility (CONFIRMED — best hardware of the four):** Scenic offers a **purpose-built accessible cabin category** — a **Junior Suite with balcony + wheelchair facilities, near reception and the lift** (named **Suite 302** on the Sapphire/Emerald/Diamond/Ruby), with **accessible cabins reported across all 13 European ships** — rather than the zero (Avalon) or single token cabin (Emerald) of the others. *(Corroborated across multiple accessibility sources: travelsignposts.com/river-cruises/riverboats/river-cruising-for-disabled; wheeliegoodcruises.com/river-cruise-meet-mobility-issues/; cruisecritic.com/articles/a-guide-to-accessible-river-cruises)* Still subject to the line-agnostic risks: Sun Deck stairs-only, steep/rafted gangways, wheelchairs generally can't be used on the gangway, companion typically required. Scenic's exact verbatim onboard-disclaimer text was gated (JS accordion) — confirm literal wording from live FAQ/Booking Conditions at booking. *(scenic.cruises/US/en-US/plan-your-journey/faqs/river)*
- **Net:** The best simultaneous answer to BOTH signals — independence-friendly (Freechoice + e-bikes) AND the only line whose excursion structure is explicitly built for two travelers at different paces on the same day, with the best accessible-cabin story.

## BOTTOM LINE — CASTILLO-STYLE RECOMMENDATION

**The mobility degree is UNKNOWN and it is the hinge.** Two scenarios, two answers — but one line wins both:

**#1 — SCENIC ("Rhine Highlights").** Best simultaneous fit for the two signals we have. Scenic Freechoice's **same-day Active / Moderate / Relaxed grading** is purpose-built for exactly this couple: Stefanie takes the active e-bike or hiking option while Rob takes the relaxed, easy-pace option — *same port, same day, no compromise.* Add complimentary e-bikes for independent roaming, ultra-luxury all-inclusive with a butler, and — critically — **a confirmed purpose-built accessible cabin category** (Junior Suite w/ balcony + wheelchair facilities near reception/lift; accessible cabins reported across all 13 European ships), versus Avalon's zero and Emerald's single token cabin. **If Rob uses any wheeled aid, Scenic is the only one of the four with hardware built for it.** Caveat to hold honestly: the *labels* (Active/Moderate/Relaxed) are Scenic's distinguishing pitch — Avalon and Emerald also offer same-day pace choice, just less explicitly graded. **Recommend as lead.**

**#2 — AVALON ("Romantic Rhine") IF Rob's limitation is mild** (tires on long walks, no mobility device). Avalon is the purest independence play — Avalon Choice tiers, 16 bikes + e-bikes, the hardest-marketed free time, and the unbeatable Panorama Suite where the bed faces an open-air glass wall (a genuinely special "European vibe" room). The disqualifier is accessibility: **no accessible cabins, restrictive wheelchair policy.** Great if Rob is ambulatory; wrong if he isn't.

**Set aside:**
- **Emerald** — strong self-guided tooling (GPS app) at a better price; keep as the value alternative if budget tightens, but its lone accessible cabin and value-tier finish put it behind Scenic on this specific profile.
- **Uniworld** — beautiful, supremely hand-held, and the most *supportive experience* for a slow pace — but it **fights** the one preference Stefanie stated outright (independence), and its 2027 Amsterdam–Basel routing needs confirmation. Re-introduce only if the couple tells us they'd actually prefer guided immersion over independence.

**Decision-driver to collect before recommending to the client:** *the degree of Rob's mobility limitation* (Open Item #2 in the dossier). It moves the answer between Scenic-or-Avalon (mild) and Scenic-only (any wheeled aid).
