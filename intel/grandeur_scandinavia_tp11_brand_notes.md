## What I Changed

- **Oslo / Hadeland paragraph — rewritten (voice violation).** Luna's original: "...is one of those experiences that is genuinely difficult to describe beforehand and impossible to reduce afterward." This is the only real brochure sentence in the piece — it signals the experience is ineffable, which is what travel copy defaults to when it runs out of precision. Replaced with a sentence that describes it precisely: the heat, the breath, the rotation, the speed of it. Show the thing; don't tell them they won't be able to describe it.

- **Kristiansand — "Dallas" removed (shared-block issue).** Luna wrote: "...smoked salmon and local shellfish that are harder to find done right once you're back in Dallas." This is specific to one couple, but this file serves as the shared body for three TP 1.1 emails. Replaced with "once you're home" — neutral, still carries the same implicit contrast. **Flagged for Dani below.**

## What I Kept

- **Closing line.** "Ten nights from now, you'll disembark with a different understanding of what the North actually is — and what it takes to live well inside it." This works. "Live well inside it" is the right phrase — it picks up the thread from the opening sensibility paragraph ("a northern European seriousness about craft, history, and the natural world") and delivers it at weight. Leave it.

- **Tivoli / Disney line.** "Walt Disney visited before designing Disneyland. The comparison flatters Disneyland." Best line in the piece. Exactly D2M register — confident, specific, slightly arch. Nothing to touch.

- **Stockholm City Hall observation deck close.** "...is available to you, and then it isn't." Rhetorical, but earns it through the factual setup. Actionable information wearing a good coat.

- **Arc structure.** Luna built this correctly: Stockholm (wonder, layers) → Baltic pause → Berlin (weight, history) → Copenhagen (tempo, pleasure) → Passage North (register shift) → Kristiansand (accessible, human-scale) → Oslo (resolution, polar memory). Nothing to restructure.

- **HTML.** Single-column table, full inline CSS, `background-color:#f7f3ea`, all port headers `color:#0000ff`, body copy `color:#333333`. No `<style>` block. No floats. Gmail-safe throughout. Clean.
