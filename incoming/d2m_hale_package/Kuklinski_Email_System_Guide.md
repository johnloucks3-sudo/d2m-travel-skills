What's In Each Arc
Arc 1 — Airfare + Pre-Cruise Hotel Covers the two naturally linked pre-cruise decisions in a single arc. Email A collects flight preferences (departure city, cabin, miles programs) and hotel style preference. Email B presents three flight options for each routing (RIC→PTY and RSW→FLL) plus three Panama City hotel candidates with a distance-to-pier column. Email C confirms all bookings with reference numbers. Email D checks for schedule changes and flags seat selection gaps.


Arc 2 — Shore Excursions Covers all three ports in one arc rather than three separate arcs. Email A collects activity level, Canal preference (onboard vs. shore), and wildlife interest. Email B presents three options per port — Viking group tour, second Viking option, and a private alternative — with a key advisory question embedded in each port section. Email C confirms all excursion bookings with Viking reference numbers and cancellation terms. Email D does a status pass and flags mobility or weather considerations.


Arc 3 — Airport & Pier Transfers Covers all three transfer segments (PTY arrival, hotel-to-pier morning of Dec 17, Port Everglades to FLL post-cruise) as one arc. Email A collects arrival times, luggage volume, and FLL departure timing. Email B presents three options per segment with a clear note on the risk tradeoffs (shared shuttle vs. private, pre-booked vs. rideshare). Email C confirms all three segments with day-of contact instructions. Email D checks for flight time changes that would affect transfer timing.


Arc 4 — Specialty Dining (Full) Covers Manfredi's and The Chef's Table aboard Viking Mars. Email A collects dietary restrictions, celebration nights, dining pace preference, and by-name dish requests. Email B presents three dining strategies (both restaurants, Chef's Table only, Manfredi's twice) with a cost comparison table rather than three individual restaurant options. Email C confirms reservations with cover charge amounts and dress code reminders. Email D reminds about embarkation day podium confirmation and dietary notes.


Arc 5 — Specialty Dining (Light) Two emails. Email A provides context on both restaurants with links and asks two questions — dietary restrictions and celebration nights. Email C puts notes on file and gives embarkation day podium instructions.

How Hale Uses This System
Current state: You read the calendar, draft the email, send it.


Target state: Hale watches the trigger dates, pulls the right arc and position, drafts the email into your Gmail drafts folder before you think to ask. You review, personalize if needed, and send.


What Hale needs to make that work:


1. A machine-readable version of the master calendar above (JSON or YAML with trigger_date, client_id, arc, position, style_template, status)
2. Access to the email templates as structured text (the .md files are the source)
3. A Gmail draft-creation tool (already in D2M-COMMAND-HUB via Gmail integration)
4. A status flag system so Hale knows which emails are sent, pending, or superseded


That rebuild is the next session's work. For now, the .md files are the source of truth.

Cruise Line Style Templates
The emails in this library are written in STYLE: VIKING — defined by:


* Warm, direct, practical tone
* Emoji section headers (✈️ 🏨 🚢 🗺️ 🍽️)
* Bold key facts inline
* Comparison tables for candidate emails
* Embedded advisory question per table (fact-based, not prescriptive)
* Short, direct closing CTA
* ~300 words per email


STYLE: REGENT (not yet built) would differ:


* Quieter, more confident tone — no emoji
* Fewer bullets, more prose
* Price assumptions shift significantly (suite minimums, butler service, all-inclusive)
* Table format retained but column headers shift (e.g., "Butler accessibility" replaces "Distance to pier")
* Closing is softer — "When you're ready to proceed, I'm here" vs. "Reply and I'll lock this in"
