**MIAMI, FL — Embarkation (December 29)**
New Year's Eve eve in Miami. Regent's embarkation at Terminal D, Port of Miami. Check-in opens at noon. Plan to arrive by 2:00 PM. The ship sails at 5:00 PM — Miami skyline at sunset is the opening act.

**GEORGE TOWN, GRAND CAYMAN (Day 3)**
Crystal-clear water, Seven Mile Beach, and Stingray City. This is the warm-up port — easy, beautiful, no passport stamp needed (British territory). The snorkeling here is world-class. George Town itself is walkable from the tender pier.

**CARTAGENA, COLOMBIA (Day 5)**
The jewel of this itinerary after the Canal. A UNESCO walled city with 500 years of colonial architecture, vibrant color on every street, and some of the best food in South America. Walk the old city walls at sunset. Café del Mar on the bastion is the spot. The street food in Getsemaní is outstanding.

**PANAMA CANAL TRANSIT (Day 6-7)**
The centerpiece. Full transit from Atlantic to Pacific — Agua Clara locks on the Atlantic side, Gatun Lake crossing, then Pedro Miguel and Miraflores locks on the Pacific side. Roughly 50 miles of engineered precision, rising and dropping 85 feet. Be on deck all day. This doesn't repeat.

Grandeur transits the newer Agua Clara (Neopanamax) locks — wider chambers, modern infrastructure. The narration aboard will cover the history, engineering, and ecology. Photography from the upper decks is exceptional.

**PUNTARENAS, COSTA RICA (Day 8)**
Gateway to Costa Rica's Pacific coast. Two directions from here: the cloud forests of Monteverde (3-hour drive, zip-lining, hanging bridges, wildlife) or the beaches and surf towns along the Nicoya Peninsula. The biodiversity is staggering — more species per square mile than almost anywhere on earth.

**PUERTO QUETZAL, GUATEMALA (Day 9)**
The most adventurous port on the itinerary. Gateway to Antigua Guatemala — a UNESCO colonial city in the shadow of three volcanoes. The jade markets, the Arco de Santa Catalina, the coffee plantations in the highlands. It's a 90-minute drive from port to Antigua — worth every minute.

**ACAPULCO, MEXICO (Day 11)**
Classic Pacific Mexico. The cliff divers at La Quebrada are still performing — a tradition since 1934. The old town has character. Fort San Diego offers the best panoramic views. The bay at sunset is spectacular. This port has recovered significantly from its rough years — security has improved, tourism is rebuilding.

**CABO SAN LUCAS, MEXICO (Day 13)**
Land's End — where the Sea of Cortez meets the Pacific. El Arco (the famous rock arch) is best seen by water taxi from the marina. The whale watching season peaks December through March — you'll be there at the perfect time for humpback sightings. Medano Beach is the social center.

**SAN DIEGO, CA (Day 15)**
The Gaslamp Quarter, Balboa Park, the USS Midway Museum. San Diego is one of the most livable cities in America. If you have time ashore, the craft beer scene here is unmatched. Little Italy for lunch.

**LOS ANGELES, CA — Disembarkation (January 14)**
Arrive at the World Cruise Center in San Pedro. Disembarkation typically begins at 8:00 AM. LAX is 30 minutes without traffic, 60-90 with. Plan your return flight for afternoon to avoid the rush.

---

**BOTTOM LINE**
Ten ports. Eight countries/territories. A full Panama Canal transit. Two sea days to recover. This is one of the best value itineraries Regent runs — 16 nights touching both oceans with the Canal as the centerpiece.
