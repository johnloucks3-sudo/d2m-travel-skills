1. GOAL DESCRIPTION


Standing Directive (2026-07-31): "Ideas are our lifeblood, keep them coming from searches and idea generation."


Build an automated, continuous Innovation, Technical Proposals & Incubation Engine (`core/innovation/incubation_engine.py`) that aggregates ideas across 5 input streams, packages them into USAF Point Paper proposals, and delivers them directly to the Commander via our Executive HTML Briefing Portal and 3-tier notification hierarchy (Email -> Slack -> Telegram).


---


2. USER REVIEW REQUIRED


> [!IMPORTANT]

> 5 Ingest Streams & Delivery Mandate:

> 1. ELON Tech Vanguard: Daily AI, LLM, and travel tech breakthroughs (`run_innovation_scan`).

> 2. Staff Proposal Harvester: Automated proposals generated by 5 Wing personas (Hale, Dani, Sterling, Intel, Harlan).

> 3. Academic OSINT Sweeps: ArXiv, PubMed, and Europe PMC literature mining (`academic_scan`).

> 4. Competitive Intelligence: Route shifts, airline pricing changes, host agency tech shifts (`run_competitive_surveillance`).

> 5. Incubator Backlog Miner: Mining Obsidian vaults and Google Drive notes for unexecuted concepts.

>

> Inviolable Delivery Rule: No idea pools silently in a database. Every vetted proposal is rendered to the Executive Portal (`http://localhost:9090/output/html/`) and dispatched via Email -> Slack -> Telegram.
