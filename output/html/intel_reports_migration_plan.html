1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & BLUF


Our recurring Wing Intel reports and sweeps (Morning Briefing, Airline Route Impact, Innovation Scans, Academic Research, Dossier Anchors) are prime candidates for Temporal and n8n.


Currently, these sweeps rely on systemd cron timers (`~/.config/systemd/user/*.timer`). If an API fails mid-sweep, the entire cron run fails quietly. Migrating them to Temporal Workflows guarantees 100% step-level retries and zero lost reports, while n8n handles multi-channel broadcasts to Telegram and the Executive Portal.
