1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & BLUF


  • **Temporal** is our **Primary Execution Engine** for all complex, stateful, code-heavy, and high-stakes travel workflows (Client Intake, Fare Watches, Commission Reconciliation). It provides 100% durable event-sourcing and code-level retries.
  • **n8n** is our **Visual Integration & Webhook Gateway** for rapid SaaS connections, external webhooks (AppSheet, client forms), and multi-channel alerts (Telegram, Gmail).
  • **Synergy:** n8n listens for external webhooks and passes structured payloads to Temporal via gRPC/HTTP endpoints for durable, fault-tolerant execution.

3. RECOMMENDED WORKFLOW ALLOCATION


A. Temporal Workflows (Core Business Operations)

1. `ClientInquiryWorkflow` — 6-step client intake, Centrav/RSSC scan, Dani narrative generation, WF-17 approval gate, email dispatch.

2. `FareWatchWorkflow` — Hourly airline route changes, Centrav flight watches, fare drop alerts.

3. `CommissionReconciliationWorkflow` — OA/Nexion portal scraping, Harlan 6-step financial sign-off, ledger updates.


B. n8n Workflows (Ingestion & Notification Layer)

1. `n8n-webhook-intake` — Captures AppSheet and Typeform client submissions and triggers Temporal workflows.

2. `n8n-alert-router` — Formats and broadcasts system alerts to Telegram @D2MC2C_bot and mobile push.

3. `n8n-drive-mirror` — Syncs files between Google Drive and external client folders.
