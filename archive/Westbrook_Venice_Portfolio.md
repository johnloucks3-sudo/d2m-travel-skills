# Venice Portfolio: The Westbrook Family
Prepared by: D2M Travel, LLC
Date: May 4, 2026

## Executive Summary
For the Westbrook family tour—Brent, Kim, Ava (18), and Allie (16)—Venice requires a balance between historic immersion, walkability for teenage engagement, and the classic Venetian aesthetic. We have curated three elite properties to suit your pre-cruise stay.

---

## 1. The Gritti Palace (A Luxury Collection Hotel)
**The Iconic Venetian Choice**
*   **Vibe:** Historic, ornate, and deeply traditional. This is old-world Venice at its absolute best.
*   **Location:** Grand Canal.
*   **Major Attractions Nearby:** Directly across from the Peggy Guggenheim Collection; moments by water taxi to the Accademia Bridge.
*   **Family Fit:** Exceptional service that will make the girls feel like royalty. Very quiet and refined.
*   **Estimated Daily Rate (2 rooms / BB):** $3,800 - $4,500 total (varies by season)

---

## 2. The St. Regis Venice
**The Contemporary Glamour Choice**
*   **Vibe:** Modern, bright, and impeccably high-design. The most "Instagrammable" of the three.
*   **Location:** Grand Canal, walking distance to St. Mark's Square.
*   **Major Attractions Nearby:** St. Mark’s Basilica, Doge’s Palace, and the luxury shopping district.
*   **Family Fit:** Perfect for 16 and 18-year-olds who appreciate modern aesthetics and immediate access to the city’s heart.
*   **Estimated Daily Rate (2 rooms / BB):** $4,200 - $5,000 total

---

## 3. JW Marriott Venice (Isola delle Rose)
**The Resort Escape Choice**
*   **Vibe:** A private island oasis. If the bustle of Venice becomes too much, this is the serene sanctuary you need.
*   **Location:** Private island (15-minute shuttle to St. Mark's).
*   **Major Attractions Nearby:** Access via private resort shuttle.
*   **Family Fit:** Expansive grounds, swimming pools, and spa facilities. Best for "down time" before boarding your cruise.
*   **Estimated Daily Rate (2 rooms / BB):** $2,200 - $2,800 total

---

## D2M Recommendation
For the Westbrooks, **The St. Regis** is the primary recommendation. It bridges the gap between classic Venetian allure and the modern energy that will keep Ava and Allie fully engaged, while providing the walkability required to experience the city at their own pace.
