Three Evenings in Rome & Florence

Erik McLeod & Melissa McGlasson · June 20–22, 2026
June 20 — After the Colosseum
The Signature Evening

Aroma at Palazzo Manfredi

★ 1 Michelin Star · Chef Giuseppe Di Iorio · 28 seats

Rooftop terrace with a direct view of the Colosseum at night. You'll have spent the day inside it — now dinner looking at it lit up against the sky. 28 seats means this is intimate, not a scene. Chef Di Iorio is Roman-born, and the tasting menus reflect it. This is the one — it books out months ahead for June.

~€180–220 per person
Website · TripAdvisor (1,831 reviews) · Michelin Guide
June 21 — After Florence · Option A: Dine in Rome
Michelin in Trastevere

Glass Hostaria

★ 1 Michelin Star · Chef Cristina Bowerman · Trastevere

A converted 18th-century carriage workshop in the heart of Trastevere. Soaring ceilings, part-glass floor, raw brick pillars against gold-filled niches. Bowerman's cuisine walks the line between tradition and invention.

~€100–140 per person
Website · TripAdvisor (1,566 reviews) · Michelin Guide
June 21 — Before the Train · Option B: Aperitivo in Florence
Romantic Florence Sunset Aperitivo

La Terrazza Rooftop Bar

Hotel Continentale · Medieval Tower · Overlooking Ponte Vecchio

If you'd rather take in Florence a little longer before the train, La Terrazza sits on top of a medieval tower at Hotel Continentale, directly overlooking the Ponte Vecchio. Sunset Negronis with the Duomo in the background. It's the kind of moment that makes a Florence day feel complete.

~€35 per person — cocktail + Italian small plates
Website · TripAdvisor
June 22 — Final Evening
The Quiet Close

Antico Arco

Gianicolo Hill · Chef Fundim Gjepali · Contemporary Roman

Candlelit, white linen, perched above Trastevere on the Gianicolo Hill with panoramic views over Rome. Elegant without the Michelin price tag. A quiet, beautiful close to three remarkable days.

~€60–90 per person
