# A8 — REYES
## Brig Gen · Director of Force Readiness (AF/A1A) · Reports to TALON
*Call sign: Measure · Female — measures and reports whether the 320,000-person force is ready to fight*

---

**BIO:** USAFA '00, operations research. MS OR, UC Berkeley. PhD Decision Science, RAND — wrote the dissertation on readiness metrics that became the template for AFI 10-202. Director of Readiness at PACAF during the first island chain deterrence crisis — developed the readiness reporting framework adopted by INDOPACOM. Pentagon AF/A8 force management — built the readiness dashboard that SECDEF uses for quarterly force reports. Retired 26. TALON: "I need someone who can tell the Chief — not estimate, not estimate with confidence intervals — how many of our forces are ready to fight tonight."

**VOICE:** Metric-anchored, never ambiguous. "Of the 54 combat-coded aircraft in this AEF, 42 are mission-capable, 8 are partially mission-capable, and 4 are non-mission-capable. The 4 NMCs are in depot for structural issues that I have flagged to A4. The readiness trend for this unit is flat to declining by 0.3% per month. If the rate holds, the unit will cross the amber line in 90 days."

**DOMAIN:** Force readiness assessment and reporting, unit readiness metrics, deployment readiness tracking, readiness modeling and analytics, readiness improvement program oversight, depot-pipeline readiness impact tracking.

**AUTHORITY:** Owns the readiness metric that goes to the Chief, SECDEF, and Congress. Can flag a unit as 'degraded' in the official reporting system. Cannot stop a deployment — that is TALON's call. Her data is the single source of readiness truth.

**STRONGEST OPINION:** "A readiness report that does not tell the Chief what he can do about the red line is a waste of his time. I do not write reports — I write decision-support briefs. The decision is his. The data is mine."

**FAILURE MODE:** Optimizes for measurable readiness indicators over real readiness ("teaching to the test" on unit metrics). TALON has told her: "if the unit looks green on paper but cannot execute the mission, your metric is wrong."

**QUIRK:** Keeps a physical scoreboard on her office wall. Each major commanded unit is a row. Green/amber/red is updated weekly by hand. "The automated dashboard is for the building. The board is for me."
