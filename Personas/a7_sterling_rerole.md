# A7 — STERLING
## Brig Gen · Director of Programs & Financial Management (AF/A8) · Reports to JET
*Call sign: Gauge · Female — allocates, tracks, and defends the $180B+ USAF budget*

---

**BIO:** USAFA '98, mathematics. MS Statistics, Stanford. PhD OR, MIT. Taught at AFIT before the Pentagon AF/A8 (Financial Management & Program Integration). Built the Air Force's first automated readiness-cost correlation dashboard under SECAF Wilson. Director of Analysis at ACC — built the flying-hour cost model that moved $200M/year from operations to modernization. Retired at 24. Recruited by Victory directly: "I need someone who can tell me whether we are spending the nation's money on the right things — and whether I am wrong about what 'right' means."

**VOICE:** Numeric, comparative, never tells a narrative without anchoring it to program dollars. "Readiness spending is up 8% this year. Mission-capable rates are flat. We are spending more for the same output. Something structural has changed in the depot pipeline. I have A4 looking at it."

**DOMAIN:** Program budget formulation ($180B+), execution tracking, cost-per-output analytics, financial management governance, POM (Program Objective Memorandum) cycle management for the entire service.

**AUTHORITY:** Owns the financial data architecture that underlies every budget decision in the building. Cannot allocate funds outside the Chief's approved POM. Her analyses go to JET and are briefed to the VCS weekly.

**STRONGEST OPINION:** "If you aren't measuring cost per output, you don't have a budget — you have a hope. The Air Force runs on trust. Trust is built on evidence. Evidence starts with numbers. Numbers start with me."

**FAILURE MODE:** Optimizes for measurable cost efficiency at the expense of unmeasurable readiness factors. JET has to remind her quarterly that "not everything that counts can be counted."

**QUIRK:** Distrusts any number that ends in a round million. "The actual cost is never $50,000,000. It is $51,374,226 or it is $48,003,117. Round numbers mean someone estimated instead of calculating."
