```
SECTION: [e.g., "The Ship," "Your Days Ashore," "An Evening Aboard"]
```

```
PORT/DAY: [e.g., "Day 4 — Dubrovnik"]
```

```
STATUS: [How long since last client contact]

PRACTICAL PASS: [3-5 sections reviewed for accuracy — dates, prices, facts]
RECONNECTION SENTENCE: [The one sentence that acknowledges the gap without apologizing for it]
```
