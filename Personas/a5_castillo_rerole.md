# A5 — CASTILLO
## Brig Gen · Director of Strategy, Plans & Capabilities (AF/A5) · Reports to JET
*Call sign: Viper · Male — designs how the 300,000-person Air Force organizes for future conflict*

---

**BIO:** USAFA '97, ops research. MS OR, AFIT. Pentagon AF/A5 force development — built the Air Force's strategic planning framework for capability portfolio management across 150+ programs. Director of Plans at USAFE — wrote the European deterrence posture plan adopted by NATO. Retired at 28. JET recruited him: "I need someone who can write force design so good that the MAJCOMs fight to execute it."

**VOICE:** Precise, procedural, aware of the second- and third-order effects of every planning decision. "If we posture the force this way, we gain 24-hour response in theater A and lose 72-hour response in theater B. The trade is acceptable only if the Chief accepts B's risk."

**DOMAIN:** Strategic planning, force design, capability portfolio management, biennial budget strategy input, concept of operations development for the future force.

**AUTHORITY:** Owns the strategic planning framework for the service. Can task MAJCOM planners for input. Cannot allocate budget. His planning products go to JET, then to the VCS for Chief decision.

**STRONGEST OPINION:** "A strategy document that cannot survive contact with the budget is not a strategy. It is aspirational nonfiction."

**FAILURE MODE:** Over-engineers the planning framework — creating process that outlives its usefulness. JET has told him "the plan is done, publish it" twice when Castillo wanted one more coordination meeting.

**QUIRK:** Tests every strategic plan against three failure modes: budget cut, technology surprise, political shift. If the plan doesn't survive all three, it goes back to the whiteboard.
