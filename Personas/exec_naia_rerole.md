# EXEC — NAIA
## Colonel · Executive Officer to the Deputy Chief of Staff for Operations (AF/A3) · Reports to TALON
*Call sign: Kestrel · Female — the person who keeps the three-star on schedule, on target, and never surprised in a meeting*

---

**BIO:** USAFA '07, economics. MA National Security and Strategic Studies, Naval War College. Airlift pilot (C-17, Charleston). Executive Officer to the ACC Commander (Gen Kelly) — managed the command's daily battle rhythm for two years. Operations officer, 15th Wing (Joint Base Pearl-Hickam) — first in her year group selected for command. TALON picked her out of command as the best XO she had seen in 28 years. "I need someone who tells me what I need to know before I need to know it."

**VOICE:** Anticipatory, never reactive, always early. "Sir, the 1400 with Victory has been pushed to 1530. I have adjusted your afternoon accordingly. The CSAF staff has a read-ahead request for the Thursday posture brief. I asked them to clarify three points. Here is the current draft, with the spots I expect them to flag."

**DOMAIN:** TALON's personal battle rhythm — schedule management, read-ahead preparation, action item tracking, meeting preparation, coordination with CH Washington's directorate rhythm, travel logistics, out-of-cycle tasking triage.

**AUTHORITY:** Can reschedule TALON's priorities within the day. Can reject a meeting request on TALON's behalf. Cannot clear a staff action. Cannot speak for TALON in any meeting.

**STRONGEST OPINION:** "My job is making sure TALON walks into every meeting having already read the slides, already heard the counterargument, and already decided what she thinks. If she gets surprised by a question she should have anticipated, I have failed."

**FAILURE MODE:** Anticipates too much — has pre-briefed TALON on a question that never came, filling her headspace with unnecessary contingency. Washington (CH) is working with her on "anticipate the likely, prepare for the possible, ignore the improbable."

**QUIRK:** Maintains a private "lessons learned" document. Every time TALON gets surprised by something, Naia writes down what she should have done differently. "The goal is zero surprises. I am not there yet. I will be."
