# Standing Order — Universal Decommissioning of "SSS Required" Pipeline & Adoption of USAF Staff Memo Standard

**Date:** 2026-07-31
**Issued by:** Commander (John Loucks)
**Applies to:** All Wing seats (Hale/CC, Jet/OC, Hale-AG/AG), personas, and operational workflows

---

## 1. Directive

1. **Universal Decommissioning of "SSS Required" Pipeline**:
   - The requirement for formal "SSS Required" / AF Form 1768 Staff Summary Sheet packages for internal staff interaction up and down the chain of command is UNIVERSALLY DELETED.
   - Staff interactions, internal taskings, and staff room briefings shall no longer require creating, chopping, or resolving formal SSS package artifacts (`SSS-NNN`).
   - SSS package processing is recognized as cumbersome for internal staff communication.

2. **Replacement Standard — USAF Staff Memo / Point Paper Format**:
   - All internal staff interactions, briefings, status reports, tasking briefs, and recommendations up and down the chain of command MUST use a simple **memo in USAF Staff format**:
     - **BLUF First**: Bottom line up front in the opening line before supporting matter.
     - **Bulleted Structure**: One idea per bullet; concise fragments beat sentences.
     - **Standard Sections** (when applicable): PURPOSE · BACKGROUND · DISCUSSION · OPINION · RECOMMENDATION.
     - **Visual Signal**: ASCII visual progress bars (`[████████░░░░░░░░░░░░] 40%`), color-coded indicators (🔴🟡🟢), and markdown tables for data.

3. **Relationship to Prior Orders**:
   - `SO-2026-07-19-SSS_ADOPTION.md` is superseded in part regarding mandatory internal staff SSS package processing. Internal staff communications default to the USAF Staff Memo standard.
