Tokyo: One-Day Ginza Shopping & Culinary Tour

Aloha Commander and Susie,

Here is your perfectly paced, 1-day subway and walking itinerary for Ginza on April 20th, focusing on luxury shopping, a romantic gift, and incredible Katsudon.

🚇 Logistics: Hilton Odaiba to Ginza

  • Departure: Walk from the Hilton to Daiba Station (Yurikamome Line).
  • Transit: Take the Yurikamome Line to Shimbashi Station (about 15 mins, great views of the bay).
  • Transfer: At Shimbashi, transfer to the Tokyo Metro Ginza Line.
  • Arrival: Take the Ginza Line just one stop to Ginza Station. Use the underground walkways to head toward Exit A4 to pop up right at the famous Ginza 4-chome intersection.
  • Return: Reverse the route: Ginza Line to Shimbashi, then Yurikamome Line to Daiba.

🛍️ Morning: Arrival & The Golden Intersection (10:00 AM)

  • 10:15 AM — The Iconic Wako Building: Right at the intersection, you will see the Wako department store with its famous Seiko clock tower. This is the historic anchor of Ginza.
  • 10:30 AM — Mitsukoshi Ginza (The Depachika): Directly across the street is Mitsukoshi. Before shopping upstairs, go down to the basement levels (the Depachika). It is a mind-blowing food hall with perfectly crafted sweets and teas.

💎 Mid-Day: A Romantic Gift (11:30 AM)

  • Mikimoto Ginza Main Store: Walk to the flagship store of Mikimoto, the inventor of the cultured pearl. Buying Susie a beautiful piece of Japanese pearl jewelry direct from the Ginza flagship is incredibly romantic and uniquely tied to Japan.
  • Alternative: Ginza Six for modern luxury and exclusive boutiques.

🍱 Lunch: The Perfect Katsudon (1:00 PM)

  • Lunch at Ginza Bairin (銀座 梅林): Established in 1927, this was the first Tonkatsu specialty restaurant in Ginza. They are famous for their Special Katsudon (kurobuta pork cutlet simmered in a rich, sweet/savory broth with egg over rice). There might be a short line, but it moves fast and is absolutely worth it.

🖋️ Afternoon: Japanese Craftsmanship (2:30 PM)

  • 2:30 PM — Itoya (G.Itoya): Walk back up Chuo-dori to this 12-story building dedicated entirely to premium paper, pens, and Japanese stationery. It is mesmerizing.
  • 3:30 PM — Kyukyodo: Stop by Kyukyodo (near Wako) for premium incense, calligraphy supplies, and stunning washi paper since 1663.

🍸 Evening: Sunset Toast & Return (5:30 PM)

  • 5:30 PM — Sunset Drinks: Head to the top of Ginza Six to The Grand Ginza lounge to grab a quick cocktail or coffee as the sun sets over the Tokyo skyline.
  • 6:30 PM — Subway Back: Walk back to Ginza Station and take the train back to the Hilton Odaiba to rest your feet!
