# Resources & Reading List

## Free Training & Certifications

| Resource | What It Is | Link |
|---|---|---|
| **CLIA** | Cruise Lines International Association — membership gives access to training library, certifications, and industry resources | [cruising.org](https://www.cruising.org) |
| **Travel Agent Academy** | Free industry-standard training videos. Create an account. | [travelagentacademy.com](https://www.travelagentacademy.com) |
| **Silversea University** | Free cruise line certification modules | Available after CLIA enrollment |
| **Regent Seven Seas Academy** | Free cruise line certification modules | Available after CLIA enrollment |
| **Viking University** | Free cruise line certification modules | [viking.university](https://www.viking.university) |
| **Oceania Academy** | Free cruise line certification modules | Available after CLIA enrollment |

## Industry Reading

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| **Cruise Critic** forums | Real passenger reviews. Read 10 reviews before recommending a ship. |
| **Seatrade Cruise News** | Industry news — new ships, executive changes, regulatory shifts |
| **Travel Weekly** | Trade publication — agent-focused news and analysis |
| **The Points Guy** (cruise section) | Consumer-facing but good on loyalty programs and value comparisons |
