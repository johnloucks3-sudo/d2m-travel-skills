This Is Your Wing

How the Back Office Works

The Wing is what John calls the operations system behind every client relationship. It is not a single tool or a single person — it is a team of AI personas, each trained in a specific domain, each available on demand, and you never have to manage any of them directly.

You have me.

I am Hale. I am your front door. You ask me one question. I route it to the right specialist. You get back a finished product — a research brief, an email draft, an itinerary, whatever you need. You never need to know how it works. You just need to know what to ask for.

The Personas at a Glance

| Persona | Role | What They Do For You | |---|---|---| | Hale | COO/COS | Your single point of contact. You ask me. I route requests, enforce process, keep things moving. | | Dani | Client Concierge | Writes client-facing emails. Every draft you send runs through her process — warm, specific, consistent. | | Intel | Research & Intelligence | Cruise comparisons, fare checks, destination research, competitive analysis. | | Sterling | Process & Standards | Builds and maintains the tools. Audits quality. Keeps everything working. | | Harlan | Financial Verification | Every dollar figure that goes to a client passes through him. He catches errors before they become problems. |

How You Ask

You and John and I share a Telegram channel. That is your work desk.

Any of these are fair game:

  • "Hale, can you get me three Regent Alaska options for a couple in their 60s, budget around $12K, July 2027?"
  • "Hale, I need a draft email following up with my client after they put down a deposit. Warm but professional."
  • "Hale, what is the difference between Silver Muse and Silver Dawn — which is better for first-time cruisers?"

You ask in plain English. I handle the routing. You get the answer.

One rule: include the client name in every request. Even prospects. Even "just researching." That way everything stays organized.

What You Will Not Do

  • Operate the AI tools directly
  • Handle client money (payments go directly to the cruise line)
  • Calculate commissions or modify pricing
  • Make financial commitments or promises to clients without John

What You Will Do

  • Build relationships with your clients
  • Listen to what they want and ask good questions
  • Review drafts and briefs before they go out
  • Send approved communications to your clients
  • Escalate anything that feels off
