Welcome to the Wing

From: John Loucks To: Bryana Date: June 2026

Bryana,

Thank you for saying yes.

I built Dreams2Memories Travel because I got tired of being the person my friends called and thinking "I should do this professionally." It started solo — research, booking, emails, itineraries, all on one desk. It worked, but there was a ceiling. One person can only carry so many clients before something starts slipping.

So I built a different kind of agency. One person on the front line — you — with a full back office behind you that handles the research, the drafting, the scheduling, the financial tracking, and the market intelligence. You do what only you can do: build relationships, ask good questions, and be the person your clients trust.

This folder is your training file. Work through it at your own pace. The first section is orientation — read it first. After that, the training curriculum gives you a four-week path to a working knowledge of the business. The Wing bios introduce you to the people behind the names.

You are not alone in this. You never were.

Welcome to the Wing.

— John A. Loucks III Dreams2Memories Travel, LLC
