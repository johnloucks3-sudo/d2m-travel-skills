## Module 3 — Destinations & Itineraries

**Goal:** Learn the major cruise regions, seasons, and what makes each itinerary work.

| Region | What to Study |
|---|---|
| **Mediterranean** | Western (Barcelona, Rome, Nice, Florence) vs Eastern (Greece, Turkey, Croatia) vs Adriatic. Best May-Oct. Most common entry point for new cruisers. |
| **Alaska** | Inside Passage (Seattle/Vancouver round-trip) vs Gulf (one-way Anchorage-Vancouver). May-Sep. Hubbard Glacier vs Glacier Bay. |
| **Caribbean** | Eastern (USVI, St. Maarten, Bahamas) vs Western (Cozumel, Roatan, Costa Maya) vs Southern (ABC islands, Dominica). Year-round. Hurricane season Jun-Nov. |
| **Northern Europe / Scandinavia** | Norwegian fjords, Baltic capitals (St. Petersburg — currently limited), Iceland, Greenland. May-Sep. |
| **Asia** | Japan, China, SE Asia. Strong demand. Silversea and Regent sell out regularly. |
| **South Pacific / Australia / NZ** | Season opposite Northern Hemisphere. Oct-Apr peak. |
| **Expedition** | Antarctica (Nov-Mar), Galapagos (year-round), Kimberley, Northwest Passage. High price point, very high service expectations. |

| Free Resource | Use |
|---|---|
| [Lonely Planet — Cruise Destinations](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/cruises) | Destination overviews, port guides |
| [What's in Port](https://whatsinport.com) | Each cruise port — what to do, where to dock, transportation |
| [Cruise Critic — Ports of Call](https://www.cruisecritic.com/ports/) | Reviews, shore excursion ideas, local tips |

**Deliverable:** Pick 3 destinations. Write a one-paragraph pitch for each as if you're describing it to a client who has never been.

## Module 4 — The Booking Process (Industry Standard)

**Goal:** Understand how a booking actually works from inquiry to commission payment.

**Free resources:**
- [CLIA — Cruise Booking Process for Travel Agents](https://cruising.org/en/travel-agents/training/cruise-booking-process) — free webinars and courses
- [Cruise Critic — First-Time Cruiser Guide](https://www.cruisecritic.com/articles.cfm?ID=112) — understand what a client experiences
- [YouTube: "How to Become a Travel Agent" by Travel Agent Academy](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=travel+agent+academy+booking+process) — free video series

**The 8-step flow every advisor needs to know:**

1. **Discovery call** — Learn client's goals, budget, constraints, travel history
2. **Research** — Find 2-3 options that match (cruise line, ship, itinerary, cabin category)
3. **Proposal** — Present options with pricing, narrative, recommendations
4. **Deposit** — Collect client info, take deposit, make the booking
5. **Payment plan** — Track remaining payments and due dates
6. **Pre-cruise** — Flights, hotels, transfers, documents, visa requirements, excursions
7. **Sailing** — Monitor for changes, handle emergencies
8. **Post-cruise** — Follow-up, feedback, future booking pipeline

**Deliverable:** Diagram the 8-step flow in your own words. Identify which steps you feel confident about and which feel unclear.

## Module 5 — Client Communication & Sales

**Goal:** Learn how to talk to clients — discovery, proposals, objections, and closing.

**Free resources:**
- [YouTube: Sales techniques for travel advisors](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=travel+advisor+sales+training) — search for "travel advisor sales" or "consultative selling travel"
- [Cruise Critic — Travel Agent Forum](https://www.cruisecritic.com/forum/) — read how experienced agents handle common situations
- [CLIA — Marketing & Sales Toolkit](https://cruising.org/en/travel-agents/training-and-tools) — free downloadable resources

**Key frameworks to learn:**

| Skill | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Consultative selling** | Ask open-ended questions first. Understand before recommending. Never pitch before qualifying. |
| **Handling objections** | "It's too expensive" → unpack what "expensive" means. Compare to land-based equivalent. |
| **Building trust** | Share personal experience where you have it. Be honest when you don't. Follow through on commitments. |
| **The close** | "Would you like me to place a 24-hour hold on this cabin while you decide?" |

**Deliverable:** Write a 5-question discovery script you would use on a first call with a new cruise client.

## Module 6 — Industry Terminology & Professional Development

**Goal:** Sound like you belong in the room.

### Terms to Know

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| GTY / Guarantee | You book a category guarantee — the line assigns your cabin later, usually a better one |
| OBC | Onboard Credit — money to spend on the ship |
| FPD | Final Payment Date — the day full balance is due |
| Amadeo / TESS | Backend booking systems used by travel advisors |
| Consortia | Groups like Virtuoso, Travel Leaders that give advisors extra benefits for their clients |
| Net rate | The wholesale price advisors pay before adding commission markup |
| Waves | January-March, the industry's peak booking season |
| Port charges / taxes | Non-commissionable fees in a cruise fare |
| Deviation | Flying in early or staying after a cruise (vs same-day flights) |
| B2B | Back-to-back cruises — two sequential sailings |

### Professional Development (All Free)

| Resource | What You Get |
|---|---|
| [CLIA Certification Program](https://cruising.org/en/travel-agents/training-and-tools) | Free accredited courses. Start with "CLIA Accredited Cruise Counselor (ACC)" path. |
| [Cruise Line Universities](https://www.cruise-agent-university.com) | Lines offer free training: Silversea University, Regent Academy, Viking Academy, Celebrity Learning. Each gives you product knowledge and sometimes certifications. |
| [Travel Agent Academy (free)](https://www.travelagentacademy.com) | Industry-standard training videos. Create a free account. |
| [YouTube: Travel Agent Community](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=travel+agent+training) | Hundreds of hours of free content from experienced agents. |
