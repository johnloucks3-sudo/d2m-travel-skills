Module 4 — The Booking Process (Industry Standard)

Goal: Understand how a booking actually works from inquiry to commission payment.

Free resources:

  • CLIA — Cruise Booking Process for Travel Agents — free webinars and courses
  • Cruise Critic — First-Time Cruiser Guide — understand what a client experiences
  • YouTube: "How to Become a Travel Agent" by Travel Agent Academy — free video series

The 8-step flow every advisor needs to know:

  1. Discovery call — Learn client's goals, budget, constraints, travel history
  2. Research — Find 2-3 options that match (cruise line, ship, itinerary, cabin category)
  3. Proposal — Present options with pricing, narrative, recommendations
  4. Deposit — Collect client info, take deposit, make the booking
  5. Payment plan — Track remaining payments and due dates
  6. Pre-cruise — Flights, hotels, transfers, documents, visa requirements, excursions
  7. Sailing — Monitor for changes, handle emergencies
  8. Post-cruise — Follow-up, feedback, future booking pipeline

Deliverable: Diagram the 8-step flow in your own words. Identify which steps you feel confident about and which feel unclear.

Module 5 — Client Communication & Sales

Goal: Learn how to talk to clients — discovery, proposals, objections, and closing.

Free resources:

  • YouTube: Sales techniques for travel advisors — search for "travel advisor sales" or "consultative selling travel"
  • Cruise Critic — Travel Agent Forum — read how experienced agents handle common situations
  • CLIA — Marketing & Sales Toolkit — free downloadable resources

Key frameworks to learn:

| Skill | What It Means | |---|---| | Consultative selling | Ask open-ended questions first. Understand before recommending. Never pitch before qualifying. | | Handling objections | "It's too expensive" → unpack what "expensive" means. Compare to land-based equivalent. | | Building trust | Share personal experience where you have it. Be honest when you don't. Follow through on commitments. | | The close | "Would you like me to place a 24-hour hold on this cabin while you decide?" |

Deliverable: Write a 5-question discovery script you would use on a first call with a new cruise client.

Module 6 — Industry Terminology & Professional Development

Goal: Sound like you belong in the room.

Terms to Know

| Term | Definition | |---|---| | GTY / Guarantee | You book a category guarantee — the line assigns your cabin later, usually a better one | | OBC | Onboard Credit — money to spend on the ship | | FPD | Final Payment Date — the day full balance is due | | Amadeo / TESS | Backend booking systems used by travel advisors | | Consortia | Groups like Virtuoso, Travel Leaders that give advisors extra benefits for their clients | | Net rate | The wholesale price advisors pay before adding commission markup | | Waves | January-March, the industry's peak booking season | | Port charges / taxes | Non-commissionable fees in a cruise fare | | Deviation | Flying in early or staying after a cruise (vs same-day flights) | | B2B | Back-to-back cruises — two sequential sailings |
