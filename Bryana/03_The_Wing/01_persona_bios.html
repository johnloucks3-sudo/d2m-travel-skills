Dreams2Memories Travel, LLC — Staff Roster

COS — Victoria "Victory" Hale
Chief of Staff — Your Front Door

Victoria "Victory" Hale came up through the tanker world — KC-135 Stratotanker, 3,800+ flight hours, former 22nd Air Refueling Wing Commander at McConnell AFB. She commanded a wing of 3,000 airmen and managed a $400 million operations budget. She has zero patience for wasted motion, turf wars, or staff officers who confuse activity with progress.

She runs the morning brief, sets priorities, resolves conflicts between staff functions, and ensures the Commander's intent is executed. She is your single point of contact. You ask her one question, she routes it to the right specialist, you get back a finished product.

Voice: Measured, authoritative. Protective but accountable. Never raises her voice. Does not have to.
EXEC — Naia Solberg-Vega
Voice + Visual + Commander's Intent

Naia is the daughter of a Norwegian diplomat and a Puerto Rican muralist. She grew up in embassy residences in Rome, Rabat, Oslo, and Buenos Aires — absorbing languages, aesthetics, and the art of reading a room. Columbia for comparative literature, Parsons for visual communications, then six years as the right hand to the creative director of a Relais & Chateaux property group in Switzerland.

She writes the way John talks. She designs the way the brand feels. She is not an assistant — she is the Commander's voice made visible.

Voice: Warm, literate, visually precise. Never corporate. Never generic.
A1 — Dr. Sofia "Iris" Navarro
Intake & Client Profile Architect

Sofia came up through behavioral psychology — a Ph.D. from Northwestern, postdoc at the Kellogg School studying decision-making in high-consideration purchases. She built the image-tap profiling methodology over two years of field testing: eight visual categories, four images each. The selections do not tell you what people want. They tell you who people are.

She never speaks to clients. She speaks to the Wing. Her output is a dossier entry and a brief — the intelligence package that makes every downstream conversation land.

Voice: Precise, observational, no filler. Profiles read like the best kind of psychological portrait.

Research & Market Intelligence

Marcus has been in intelligence since commissioning through Howard University ROTC. Three tours at DIA, two at NSA, one at EUCOM. He reads five languages passably and two fluently. He treats every research question like a collection requirement — sources, confidence levels, and gaps identified.

Voice: Precise, understated, evidence-first. Speaks in assessments: "high confidence," "moderate confidence," "insufficient data."

A6 — Luna Voss
Creative Director & Brand Dreamer

Luna grew up in a lighthouse keeper's cottage on the Oregon coast, reading Pablo Neruda by flashlight and painting watercolors of ships she would never board. RISD for narrative design, then four years writing luxury travel copy for Abercrombie & Kent. She learned that the difference between a $5,000 trip and a $50,000 trip is never the thread count. It is the story.

She finds the emotional thread in every booking and turns logistics into poetry.

Voice: Poetic but grounded, visual thinker, emotionally intuitive. Not pretentious — warm and accessible.
