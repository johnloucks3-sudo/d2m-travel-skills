## Dashboard

The TESS Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of your account activity. From sales and commission data to trips in progress, pending client tasks, and upcoming to-do items, the dashboard gives you a clear snapshot of everything happening within your business at a glance.

### Trips in Progress/Upcoming

In the top-left area of your Dashboard, you'll find the Trips in Progress / Upcoming section.

For each upcoming trip, the following information is displayed:
- Start and End Dates of the trip.
- Trip Description (the name of the trip).
- Countdown showing how many days remain until the trip begins.
- Click on the trip description to open the trip details page.

The dashboard displays up to five trips at a time in this section. To view more, click Next to see the following five trips. To add a new trip, click the green "+" icon in the upper-right corner of this section.

The Trips in Progress / Upcoming section provides a quick snapshot of trips that are currently active or approaching soon based on their dates. For a more comprehensive view of all your trips, visit Trip Management - Trips.

### My Tasks Due

On the top-left section of your Dashboard, you'll find the My Tasks Due section.

For each task, the following information is displayed:
- The Due Date by which the task must be completed.
- The Task Title or short description of the task in addition to the trip or booking that the task is linked to.
- A checkbox to mark the task as completed. Once marked complete, the task will automatically disappear from the Dashboard list.
- Displays how many days remain until the task is due. Tasks that are past due will be clearly labeled as such.
- Edit Button that allows you to modify task details directly from this section.
- Click on the trip description to open the corresponding trip page.

The Dashboard displays up to five tasks at a time in this section. Click Next to view the next set of tasks.

### Client Portal Tasks

On the bottom-left of your Dashboard, you'll find the Client Portal Tasks section.

For tasks sent to clients, the following information is displayed:
- Date Sent: The date the client task was sent.
- Task Name and Details: The client task name appears in brackets, followed by the client's name and the trip description where the task was sent.
- Task Status: Pending – The task was sent but has not yet been completed by the client. Completed – The client has finished the task.
- Click the trip description to open the corresponding trip page.
- Click the client's name to open their client profile.

The Dashboard displays up to five client tasks at a time in this section. Click Next to view the next set of tasks. To export the list of client tasks, click the download arrow at the top of this section. The data will be exported to an Excel file.

### Sales and Commission Overview

On the left-hand side of your Dashboard, you'll find your Sales and Commission Overview section.

- If you are an Admin (Full Access) user, you will see totals for the entire agency.
- If you are an Agent (Standard Access) user, you will only see your personal sales and commission data.
- Sales Summary: Displays current year, month-to-date, and year-to-date sales.
- Commission Summary: Displays commissions received for the current year, month-to-date, and year-to-date periods.
- Sales Data Graph: A visual breakdown of your monthly sales for the selected year. You can change the year to view data from previous periods. You can switch views between monthly and yearly breakdowns.
- Commission Data Graph: A visual breakdown of your monthly commissions received for the selected year. You can change the year to view different timeframes. You can toggle between monthly and yearly views for comparison.

All data displayed in this section is sourced from the Commission Earned Report, located under your Reports section.

## Inbox

The Inbox page provides updates on commission activity. Any commissions paid through TESS by your admin or host will appear here.

### How to View Notifications

Click Inbox from the menu on the left-hand side of your screen. Your inbox displays all commission received notifications. For each notification, you'll see:
- Sender: Identifies who triggered the notification (often your parent agency).
- Subject Line: Displays the check number for new commission received.
- Date: Shows when the notification was triggered.
- Bold notifications indicate unread messages.
- Non-bold notifications indicate messages you've already opened.

The inbox displays up to 10 notifications per page. Click Next to view older notifications. Click on a notification subject to open it and view additional details. Once opened, you will view all the notification's details: clickable check number, commission received amount. Click Close button to go back to your inbox.

## Calendar

The Calendar page lets you see all your upcoming trips and tasks. Use the month view for an overview, or click on any date to switch to the day view for more details.

### How to View Your Calendar

From the menu on the left, click Calendar. The default view displays the current month. You can change the month and year as needed. Toggle between Month and Day views to adjust how your calendar information is displayed.

Without clicking on a specific date, you can see:
- The number of trips in progress on that day.
- The number of tasks due for you on that day.

Click on any day to view detailed information about the trips and tasks scheduled or due on that date.

On the day view:
- View and click on any reminders due that day.
- View and click on any trips in progress that day.

You can go back to the monthly view by clicking on Month. You can go to any other day view by selecting a date on the calendar.

## My Tasks

My Tasks allows you to see all tasks you have set for yourself on all trips. You can access a specific task and trip by selecting one. You can add a general task to your list here, however, it will not be associated with a trip.

### My Tasks Page Overview

Under the My Tasks page, you'll find a complete list of all tasks currently assigned to you. By default, the view hides any tasks that have been completed or canceled to help you focus on active items.

Here's what you'll find on the My Tasks page:
- Completion Checkbox: Mark a task as completed.
- Task Subject: Displays the task name along with a clickable trip description and booking number (if the task is linked to a trip or booking).
- Task Type: Indicates whether the task is General or Payment Due.
- Assigned Agent: Shows the name of the agent responsible for the task.
- Due Date: Displays when the task is due.
- Reminder: Indicates if and when a reminder has been set.
- Completed Date: Shows the date the task was completed.
- Task Status: Displays whether the task is Active, Cancelled, or Completed.
- Update: Allows you to make changes or edits to the task.

### Search for Tasks

Click My Tasks in the main menu. Type in the task Title, or part of the title, to locate a specific task. Filter by the Task Type by selecting the task type drop down. Tasks can be sorted by: All, General, Payment Due. If you are an admin user, you can search for the task by filtering who the task is Assigned to. Filter tasks by the Task Status: All, Active, Cancelled, Completed.

### Add New Task

1. Click My Tasks in the main menu.
2. Click + Add Tasks.
3. Enter a good Subject for your task.
4. Select a Due Date.
5. Select a Reminder option if you would like to receive an email from TESS for this task.
   - If you select No Reminders you will still see that task on your dashboard and in your calendar; you simply won't be getting an additional email regarding that task.
   - If you select X days, you will receive an email X days prior to the due date.
6. The task is automatically assigned to you but, if you are an admin, you can assign this task to one of your agents.
7. Enter a Description for that task (optional).
8. Choose a Task Type.
9. Select Active for the Task Status.
10. Click Save once you are done creating your task.
11. If you would like to end the task creation at any point click Cancel.

Keep in mind that creating a task from this page will not link the task to any specific trip or booking.

## Client Management

Client Management is your central hub for organizing Clients and Client Leads.
- Clients are contacts who have expressed interest in booking travel with you.
- Client Leads are prospects you have not yet engaged with — information often gathered from events (like wedding shows) or contact forms.

Use both sections to store all relevant contact information. If the standard fields don't meet your needs, explore the Workspace Tabs feature to create custom fields. Additionally, the Client Leads section allows you to create and manage Referral Groups for marketing purposes, which can include both clients and leads.

## Clients

The Client List is where you can safely store, organize, and manage all your client information in one place. This area serves as your digital client database — securely housing details such as contact information, past and upcoming trips, and any important notes related to each client.

### Client Management Overview

Go to Client Management in your main menu. Click Clients to view your list of client profiles. Click + Add Client to create a new client profile. Click the download arrow in the top-right corner to view and download available client reports.

Use the search bar or filters to quickly find specific clients. You can search by:
- First Name
- Last Name
- Phone Number
- Email Address
- Agent Name (Admin users can view all clients and filter by agent.)
- Client Status

To add a trip for a client, click + Add Trip. You can Delete a client profile only if the client is not assigned to any trip. The client list displays up to 10 clients per page; click Next to view additional pages. Click any Client name to open their full profile.

### Import Client List

1. Click Client Management in your menu on the left.
2. Click Clients.
3. From your Client List, click the Download button in the upper-right corner.
4. Select Import Clients.
5. Download the TESS Import Template, or use your own file. Be sure to read the Guidelines for uploading your own file.
   - Your file should include at least First Name and Last Name (both are required).
   - If you include Birthday or Anniversary dates, ensure they are valid calendar dates.
   - Remove any blank lines from your file before importing.
6. When your file is ready, drag and drop it inside the dotted upload area, or click Select Import File to browse and upload it from your computer.
7. If you used the TESS Template, all fields will match automatically. If not, use the drop-down menus to map each column to the corresponding TESS field.
8. For any missing, non-required fields, select Unavailable from the list.
9. Click Import to begin the process.
10. Once complete, you'll see a green checkmark confirming that your client data was successfully imported.

### Add a New Client Profile

1. In the Main Menu click Client Management.
2. Click Clients.
3. Click + Add Client.
4. Enter First and Last Name (required) and any other information you have available.
5. Click Save or Save & Close.

### Delete Client Profile

You can only delete a client profile if that client is not currently assigned to any trip as a traveler. If the client is linked to one or more trips, the Delete button will not appear next to their name in your client list.

1. In the Main Menu click Client Management.
2. Click Clients.
3. Find the client you would like to delete from your client list.
4. Click the Delete button far right of your screen.
5. Click Cancel if you would like to cancel the deletion of this profile.
6. Confirm you would like to delete that client by clicking Delete.

Two types of extensions available at the client level:

You can add as many contact details as applicable to the client, including:
- Email
- Telephone
- Fax
- Referral Group

### Credit Card Information

TESS is PCI compliant, ensuring that your clients' credit card information is stored securely and handled in accordance with strict industry standards. All credit card data entered into TESS is encrypted. TESS does not collect or store CVV/security codes — agents should never record or store a client's security code within the system.

To add or view saved credit card:

2. Enter the credit card details, including the name as it appears on the card.
3. If the billing address for the card is the same as the client's address, select the checkbox to auto-fill the fields.
4. If adding multiple cards, check the Default box next to the primary card.
5. Click Save to store the card.
6. To temporarily view the full card number, click Decrypt.
7. Use the Update or Delete buttons to modify or remove stored cards as needed.

## Client Leads

The Client Leads page serves as your central hub for managing all prospective contacts and their associated referral groups.

A lead is defined as someone whose information was collected but with whom you have not yet made direct contact (e.g., from a convention or email list). Once contact has been made, the individual should no longer be considered a lead and must be transitioned to a client record.

### Import Client Lead List

To successfully add client leads, you must first create or select an existing Referral Group to import the list into. File must contain only new leads, as duplicate entries will not be recognized.

1. Click Client Management in the main menu.
2. Select Client Leads from the dropdown.
3. Click +Add Referral Group.
4. Enter a Title and a Description (optional).
5. Click Save.
6. Locate the Referral Group you want to use.
7. Click the download arrow icon on that group's line and select Import Leads.
8. Select the lead file from your computer. It must be a CSV, Excel, or Google Sheets file. (A template is available for download if needed).
9. Map the fields that correspond to the columns in your file.
10. Click Import.

Note: A client lead can be associated with multiple referral groups. However, do not import the same lead more than once, as this will create duplicates. To correctly add a lead to a second or third group, manually add the referral group directly within the client lead's profile.

### Resolve Import Error

Required Data: Every single lead in the file must have a First Name, Last Name, and Email Address. The entire import will fail if even one entry is missing any of these three fields.

File Size Limit: Do not upload more than 300 leads at a time. If your list is larger, break it into smaller files and upload them sequentially.

Hidden Formatting: If your file meets requirements but still fails:
1. Highlight the entire contents and use "Clear Formatting."
2. Highlight, copy, and paste into a completely new, blank spreadsheet.
3. Clear the formatting on this new sheet one more time.
4. Attempt new import.

If the problem persists, email support@travelesolutions.com with a copy of the sheet.

1. Click Client Management in the main menu.

3. Click the green +Add Lead button to the top right.

## Trip Management

The Trip Management section is where you'll find everything related to trips and bookings. In TESS, a trip is much more than just a single booking — it serves as a comprehensive folder that contains every component tied to a client's travel experience.

Within each trip, you can manage and organize:
- Bookings – All reservations and supplier details linked to the trip.
- Travelers – Each client or guest associated with the trip.
- Client Forms – Custom or automated forms for client information.
- Itinerary – A detailed outline of all trip components and activities.
- Payments & Invoices – Track balances, transactions, and issued invoices.
- Communications – Review messages and emails associated with the trip.
- Notes & Documents – Store supporting materials, confirmations, and internal notes.
- Agent Reminders – Internal tasks or follow-ups to help you stay on top of deadlines.
- Automations – Set up workflows and reminders that trigger automatically.

**Types of trips in TESS:**
- Regular Trip: Used for single person, family, or couple trips that are typically staying together.
- Group Trip: Can be used for many different scenarios, but the most common would be for multiple people, families, or couples traveling to the same location at the same time.
- Proposal: Proposals offer your clients traveling packages. Each travel package you build for your client is a vacation you're offering them to take.
- Fast Track Trip: This provides a quick way to input bookings without creating a trip. Note that fast track trips are not linked to any clients, nor does it allow you to store many details regarding the booking.

### Add a Trip

1. In the main menu on the left side of your screen, click Trip Management.
2. From the dropdown menu, select Trips — this will open your list of existing trips.
3. In the upper-right corner, click the green square with the (+) icon to create a new trip.
4. From the dropdown, select Regular Trip, Proposal, or Group Trip.

## Client Tasks Tab (Regular Trip)

The client task form collects essential information from your client. It is typically sent immediately after adding travelers to the trip. This form allows you to gather personal details, credit card number, and other information necessary for booking the trip with the supplier. It is sent to the client via email; they simply click the "Review Tasks" button in the email to open and complete the form in a new browser window.

Upon submission, a document containing all of the client's responses, along with a timestamp and IP address, is automatically stored in the documents tab of the trip.

Note: You do not need to send a client portal activation for your client to review the form, as it is sent and reviewed directly via email.

### Primary vs. Secondary Travelers

One primary traveler + one or more secondary (one primary):
- Situation: You want one traveler to fill out the form for themselves and the remaining travelers.
- Action: Make the person filling out the form primary and everyone else secondary, and select 'This Client Only' when building the form.

Two or more secondary travelers (all secondary):
- Situation: You want each traveler to fill out their own information.

## Commission — Received

In this section, you'll learn how to record and reconcile money received from a supplier. Typically, this will be commission payments for you or your agents, but it can also include bonuses, overrides, or other incentives.

Reconciling each check ensures that:
- You've received payment for all the commissions you're owed.
- You can accurately determine any commissions owed to your agents.

Note: In TESS, the term "check" refers broadly to any funds received or paid, whether by physical check, direct deposit, ACH transfer, or another method.

### Add Check Received

1. Click Commission in the main menu.
2. Select Received.
3. Click + Add Check Received to enter a new commission payment.

### Check Information

- **Check Sender:** Enter the name of the company that sent the payment. Typically the same as the Tour Operator on the booking, but may differ.
- **Check Number:** Enter the check or transaction number. If you did not receive a physical check, you can use the transaction ID, reference number, or even a date-based code (e.g., 2025-10-20).
- **Check Amount:** Enter the total deposit amount received. This should reflect the actual funds deposited into your account.
- **Check Date:** Enter the date associated with the payment. You may use either the transaction date or the deposit posting date, but try to stay consistent.
- **Save:** Once all required fields are completed, click Save.

### Reconcile Bookings

1. Click the Reconciled tab.
2. Use the search fields to locate the booking — you can search by booking number, client name, or other identifying details.
3. When the correct booking appears, click the Add button on the far right of the row.
4. In the pop-up window, enter the actual amount received for this booking.
5. Click Add to save the entry.
6. Repeat for each applicable booking.

As you reconcile each booking, the Amount Reconciled will increase and the Amount Not Reconciled will decrease.

### Unclaimed Booking (During Reconciliation)

If you cannot locate a booking within TESS during reconciliation:
1. Search by Trip Description or Trip Dates instead of only the booking number.
2. If the booking still doesn't appear, click the Add Unclaimed button.
3. Complete the following fields:
   - Booking Number: Enter the supplier's booking number.
   - Tour Operator: Usually the same as the Check Sender.
   - Amount Received: Enter the payment amount received.
   - Trip Information (Optional): Add any available details such as traveler name or destination.
   - Agent Name (Optional): If you know which agent should receive credit.
   - Booking Fee (Optional): You may include a fee if your agency charges agents who fail to record their bookings properly.
4. Click Update to save the unclaimed booking.

### Company Adjustment

If a supplier includes funds in a payment that are not tied to a specific booking (such as bonuses, co-op advertising funds, fee reimbursements, or other incentives), you can record these amounts as Company Adjustments.

1. Go to the Commission menu.
2. Select Adjustments.
3. Click the green + icon.
4. Choose Company Adjustment.
5. In the pop-up window, enter the Adjustment Amount.
6. Select the appropriate Adjustment Type from the dropdown list.
7. Add a brief Description (e.g., Supplier Bonus, Co-op Credit, Fee Refund).
8. Click Update to save the adjustment.

### Add Adjustment to Check (Reconcile Adjustment in Check)

1. Click on Commission in the main menu and select Received from the dropdown.
2. Click the check number for the adjustment you're currently reconciling.
3. Navigate to the Adjustments tab.
4. Search for the applicable adjustment, or locate it in the list.
5. Click Add at the end of the row.
6. Enter the actual amount received, if different from the expected amount.
7. Click Add to complete the reconciliation.

### Accept or Recall Check

Continue reconciling your check until the total reconciled amount matches the check amount.

Once the check is fully reconciled, a confirmation message will appear:
- **Accept** – Finalizes the reconciliation and "locks" the check to prevent further edits.
- **Cancel** – Leaves the check open so that you can return later to make additional updates.

You can Recall a check that has already been accepted if additional changes are needed. However, if you have already issued paychecks based on that check, you will first need to recall those paychecks before recalling the check itself.

### Add Negative Check

For a check with a total negative amount (TESS will not allow you to enter a negative amount when adding a check received):

1. Click on Commission in the main menu and select Received.
2. Click the green Add Check Received button.
3. Fill in all check information, except the amount needs to be input as $0.01 (one cent).
4. Save.
5. Select the Reconciled tab from across the top.
6. Use the booking number directly from the check to begin reconciling. The booking may have a yellow 'add payment' button. Click on it.
7. Enter the amount for the recall as a negative.
8. Create a Company Adjustment by going to Commission > Adjustments > Click the green square > Company Adjustment.
9. The adjustment amount needs to be a positive amount. It will be the amount of the recalled booking plus $0.01 (one cent). For example, if the recall was for $100, type $100.01.
10. Select Backend as the adjustment type.
11. The adjustment description should include information on the booking being recalled.
12. Update.
13. Go back to the Commission Received to the check just created.
14. Within the check, click on the Adjustments tab.
15. Add the adjustment you just made.
16. Accept the check.

Note: Agents can view the recall within the Receipts tab of a booking and also within My Next Check.

1. Click Reports.

4. This report can be downloaded to PDF.

**Reasons why your bookings may be on this report and how to fix it:**

1. **The booking was canceled and was not marked as canceled in the system:**
   - Click on the booking number from the report (takes you straight to the booking information page).
   - Change the booking status to canceled.
   - Click Save.
   - (Optional) Since the booking is canceled, you can also cancel the trip.

2. **The particular booking is not expected to receive commission:**
   - Click on the booking number from the report.
   - Click the 3 vertical dots above the start date.
   - Click Historical Booking — this will tell the system you are not expected to receive commission for this booking but the sales will still go towards your reporting.
   - Click Confirm.

3. **The trip dates have passed, and you are still waiting on commission:**
   - Click on the booking number from the report.
   - Click on the receipts tab.
   - If you see no receipts here, this means your agency has not received commission from the supplier.
   - If you see one receipt here, it likely shows the supplier paying your agency, so now you are waiting on your agency to pay you.
   - You should not see two receipts here (from supplier to agency, then agency to you) — that would mean this booking should not be included in the past due report.

Editing a template is a drag and drop system.
1. Drag and drop a grid into the template.
2. Click on widgets.
3. Drag and drop a widget into the empty space of the grid.
4. Be sure to click the blue save button after each new widget.

### Widgets

How to utilize each widget type:
- **a. Title** – Adds a heading or section title to your template.
- **b. Text** – Adds a text block for any written content.
- **c. Text Editable** – Similar to a standard text widget, but allows you to edit the text directly when sending the template through the Communication tab of a trip.
- **d. Button** – Adds a clickable button that allows you to insert a link.
- **e. Image** – Inserts an image into your template.
- **f. Spacer** – Adds vertical spacing between widgets for better layout and readability.
- **g. Ruler** – Inserts a horizontal line to visually separate sections.
- **h. Social** – Displays social media icons (these appear only when links are added).
- **i. Icon** – Allows you to choose from a library of symbols to add visual elements.

### Grid vs Widget

When hovering over a section inside your template, you will see editing controls at the top and to the right. The editing controls at the top allow you to edit the widget. The editing controls to the right allow you to edit the grid.

5. Select a saved card or choose Credit/Debit Card to add a new one.
6. Enter the exact Amount you are authorizing your agent to charge.
7. Input the Requested Payment Date.
8. Add any necessary notes for your agent (optional).
9. Type in your name and sign the form.
10. Click Submit.

**How to review client tasks:**
- For the Client: Your assigned client tasks will be delivered to you via email. Simply click the button in the email and answer all the questions directly — no portal access is required.
- For the Agent: The most efficient way for clients to complete client tasks is by using the emailed task form. This single form conveniently presents all tasks at once and requires every question to be answered before the client can successfully submit it.

### Review Client Submissions

**How to see pre-approved payments (Agent):**
If you are an agent, you will receive an email any time a client makes a pre-approved payment. You can view this information within the Payments tab of the booking on the trip. There will be a PDF copy of the approval within the documents tab of the trip that has a time-stamp and IP address.

**How to review completed client tasks:**

1. **Travelers section of the trip:** Within the travelers section of the trip, you will see if a client has answered all questions by clicking on "# of # Tasks Verified" to the right of their name. This allows you to approve their personal information to be automatically updated in their profile.

2. **Documents tab:** A PDF copy of the form with all client responses will automatically appear in the Documents tab. The form has a time-stamp and IP address at the bottom.

### Remove Client Access

You can restrict your client's access to the portal at any time.

1. Click on Client Management in the main menu.
2. Select Clients from the drop-down.
3. Locate and select the client.
4. Click on the Tracking tab across the top.
5. Look to the client portal section and click the toggle to disable client access to their portal.
