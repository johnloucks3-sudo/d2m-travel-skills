# Dreams2Memories Travel, LLC
## Data Security Brief

*How we protect your information*

---

You share personal details with us because you trust us. Here is exactly how we protect that trust.

### What We Store

| Data Type | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| Names & contact info | Communications, booking records |
| Passport details | Booking submission to cruise lines / airlines only |
| Travel preferences | Building itineraries you'll love |
| Payment confirmations | Trip accounting and deadline tracking |
| Trip correspondence | Context for ongoing service |

We do **not** store your full credit card numbers. Payment processing goes directly to suppliers (Regent, Silversea, etc.) or via secure portals.

### Where It Lives

- *Google Workspace* — encrypted at rest and in transit (AES-256 / TLS 1.3)
- *Google Drive* — access controlled, shared only with John
- *No local unencrypted storage* — no spreadsheets on personal laptops with open sharing
- *Your client portal* — magic-link authenticated, HTTPS only, no passwords stored

### Who Has Access

- John Loucks — full access
- Our AI assistant — operates under John's credentials, same access controls
- Third parties — **none**, except the suppliers (cruise lines, hotels) who require it to fulfill your booking

### Breach Protocol

In the unlikely event of a data incident, John will notify you within 72 hours with a plain-language explanation of what occurred, what was affected, and what we are doing about it.

### Your Controls

- Request your data: Email concierge@d2mluxury.quest
- Request deletion: Same — we will confirm within 5 business days
- Opt out of AI assistance: No data change required — just let John know
