# Dreams2Memories Travel, LLC
## AI Privacy Statement

*Effective: March 2026 | Issued by: John Loucks, Owner & Principal Advisor*

---

We use artificial intelligence as a research and drafting assistant — not as a decision-maker.

### What AI Does in Our Practice

- Researches destinations, cruise itineraries, hotel options, and excursions on your behalf
- Drafts initial communications and itinerary documents
- Monitors booking deadlines, payment due dates, and trip milestones
- Summarizes market intelligence to find you the best options faster

### What AI Does NOT Do

- AI does not book travel on your behalf — John Loucks authorizes and signs every reservation
- AI does not send emails to you without John's review and approval
- AI does not access your credit card or payment data autonomously
- AI does not share your information with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers

### Your Data

- *What we collect:* Names, travel preferences, passport details (travel-required), contact information, and trip-specific details you share with us
- *Where it lives:* Encrypted Google Workspace (Drive, Gmail) — the same infrastructure trusted by millions of businesses
- *Who sees it:* John Loucks and our AI assistant (operating under John's account and control). No outside parties.
- *How long we keep it:* Active trip files retained through trip completion + 3 years. You may request deletion at any time.
- *We never sell your data.* Ever.

### Your Rights

You may request a copy of your data, ask us to delete it, or opt out of AI-assisted services at any time — with no change to your service quality or relationship with John.
